# Help centre information architecture (2026-09-28)

Phase (a) of the help centre rebuild: the nine job groups, the mapping of every existing help page to its new article, the Get started series, the word budgets, and the findability question bank. The navigation source is `help/tree.yaml`; the questions are in `help/questions.yaml`. The figures on this page come from `npm run help:inventory` (`frontend/scripts/help-inventory.mjs`) run on branch `feat/help-centre-wave-0` on 28 September 2026. The inventory counts words after the front matter and the sources comment are removed, so its total (42,219 words over 54 pages) is below the `wc -w` figure the plan quotes (about 43,600).

## Why these groups

The old site was organized by page type: Concepts, Get started, Tasks, Reference, Troubleshooting, Glossary. A reader with a job in mind had to know which type of page answers it before they could start. The new hub follows the Confluence model: each group is named for the reader's job, in statement form, and holds every kind of page that job needs. A person entering a gas bill finds the task, the CSV columns and the explanation of what a record is in one place.

The nine groups follow the order of the work. Get started is first because it is the guided path. Access, organization, activity data and factors are the set-up jobs, in the order a new organization does them. Inventories and reporting are the yearly cycle. Administration is the platform team's job and sits apart from the organization's work. Fix a problem is last, where a reader looks when something refuses. The glossary is a top-level page with A to Z jump links rather than a group.

Three rules shaped the mapping:

- The 12 concept essays become "What is X?" articles of at most 400 words inside the group they serve, so the explanation sits next to the task it explains. One essay, "Why does changing a record not change last year's report?", splits in two: "What is an activity record?" under Record activity data and "What is a calculation run?" under Build, check and run an inventory.
- The statuses reference splits by area into "Check a ... status" articles, so a status table sits in the group whose screens show it. The activity record statuses fold into "What is an activity record?" and the recalculation candidate statuses into "Decide a recalculation candidate", since neither area has enough states for a page of its own.
- The pre-flight reference keeps its findings table under "Clear the pre-flight findings"; its freeze blockers move into "Freeze the inventory and launch a run", which is where a reader meets them.

Two counts differ from the plan and are deliberate:

- The plan lists 12 articles for Build, check and run an inventory but names 13 (eight tasks and references, five what-is articles). The tree keeps all 13, because each of the five concepts (lifecycle, scope classification, pre-flight gate, dual reporting, calculation run) answers a different question in the bank.
- The plan drops "the six index pages". Five are dropped. The sixth, `tasks/administration/index.md`, is not an index: it describes the console and becomes "What is the administration console?". `troubleshooting/index.md` is the troubleshooting content and splits into the four Fix articles.

Totals: 9 groups, 66 articles (65 in groups plus the glossary), 49 existing pages mapped, 5 dropped, 25 questions.

### Product findings the writers and reviewers raised

Each was verified in the source. They are recorded here for triage; none was fixed in the content waves, and the help describes the product as it is.

- A saved emission factor's validity end cannot be set anywhere in the UI, although the delete refusal says to set it: `useUpdateEmissionFactor` and `PUT /api/ghg/emission-factors/{id}` exist and nothing calls them.
- There is no password change or reset anywhere; the administrator's user form still says "they should change it later", and its temporary-password field enforces 8 characters where the backend rule is 12.
- Changes to a legal entity's ownership or relationship are not written to the organization history (`GhgAuditEvent.Action` has no entity or facility action), so question q20 of the findability bank has no answer in the product.
- A published inventory still shows **Launch calculation run** and "Ready to launch a run" although the backend refuses the launch.
- Classification findings and history entries print enum-derived category names ("purchased goods services") instead of the labels the screens use ("1. Purchased goods and services").
- **Mark as final** does not re-check factor approval; a factor unapproved after the run passes designation.
- The freeze dialog's gate summary shows "Reporting boundary 1 error" on every draft, which reads like a blocker although the freeze itself clears it.
- The boundary's exclusion reasons offer "Record removed", an automatic record reason, for an entity or facility.
- The reporting company is listed with the relationship "Subsidiary" under **Legal entities** and in the facility form, although the entity form says it has no relationship.
- The API accepts an organization detail edit under support access (`updateOrganization` uses `checkOwner`); the UI hides **Settings** under a grant.
- A member's display name on the Members card is a snapshot taken when the member was added; a later profile change does not reach it.
- Only an owner can see any member's role on screen; a preparer or reviewer learns their role from tooltips and refusals.
- A factor pack edition that applies inside a published period is blocked permanently; the refusal suggests reopening, which a published inventory cannot do.
- The **History** card on **Settings** does not refresh after **Add member** (a query invalidation gap).

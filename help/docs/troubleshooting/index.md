# Troubleshooting

When CarbonOS refuses an action it says why, in a sentence on the screen,
and most of those sentences are enough. This page is for the cases where
the sentence is not: it lists the message or symptom you see, the check
that tells you the cause, and the step that recovers.

The entries arrive with the task pages they belong to.

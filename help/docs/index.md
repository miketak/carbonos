# CarbonOS help

CarbonOS keeps a greenhouse gas (GHG) inventory the way the GHG Protocol
Corporate Standard describes one: the facts of what an organization burned,
bought and did are recorded once, and each inventory is an accounting view
over those facts for one reporting period. This site is for the people who
do that work: the owners, preparers, reviewers and verifiers of an
organization, and the administrators who run the platform.

## Start here

- **New to CarbonOS and to carbon accounting?** Read the
  [concepts](concepts/index.md) first, then follow the guided path in
  [Get started](get-started/index.md).
- **New to CarbonOS but not to carbon accounting?** Skim
  [the workflow](concepts/index.md) to see where the Standard's steps sit in
  the product, then go to the [tasks](tasks/index.md).
- **A platform administrator?** Your work is in the administration console.
  Start with the [tasks](tasks/index.md) for administration.

## Two things to know before you start

Every page on this site describes what CarbonOS does today. Where the GHG
Protocol requires something, the page says so and names the chapter; where
CarbonOS enforces a rule of its own, the page says that instead; and where a
choice is yours to make, the page says "you should" or "you can", never
"you must".

Saving, validating or publishing a figure in CarbonOS records what you
decided and why. It does not make the figure correct, and it is not a
verification. The product keeps the record a verifier will ask for; the
judgment stays yours.

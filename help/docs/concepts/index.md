# Concepts

These pages explain what CarbonOS is built from and how the pieces relate,
so that you can predict what an action will change before you take it.
They are written to be read in order the first time.

<!-- sources: spec 00; the running example is defined in the-running-example.md -->

Read them in this order:

1. The workflow: how an inventory gets from facts to a published report.
2. The running example: one small company that every page reuses.
3. Roles: who can do what in an organization, and what a platform
   administrator can and cannot see.
4. Facts, views and runs: why changing a record does not change last
   year's report.
5. The inventory lifecycle: what Draft, Frozen, Final and Published mean.
6. Boundaries and consolidation approaches: which operations belong in an
   inventory.
7. Classification: how CarbonOS decides a record's scope, and when it asks
   you to justify a departure.
8. The pre-flight gates: why a launch is on hold, and what a warning means.
9. Emission factors, packs and editions: where the numbers come from and
   what a new edition changes.
10. The base year and recalculation: when a candidate is raised and how you
    decide it.
11. Support access and confidentiality: what the platform team can see.
12. Why scope 2 has two figures: location-based, market-based, instruments
    and the residual mix.

Read them in this order the first time; afterwards the list is the map.

## Problem

Four gaps shared one root: the facts behind a line could not be examined by
anyone but the person who typed them. A correction edited the record in place
with no reason and no trace of the previous value, so a verifier could not
tell a corrected figure from an original one. A record, a facility or a legal
entity disappeared on one click. Evidence was a free-text reference with no
document behind it, and it did not print on the lines a verifier samples. Data
quality was a three-way method label with no score and no uncertainty, and
the report said nothing about either. A record exclusion was a menu of seven
reasons with no words and no magnitude, so "Methodology exclusion" stood
where a justification should be.

## Behavior

- **Data quality tiers.** Every record carries a **quality tier** from 1 to
  5, after the Scope 3 Standard's indicators: 1 metered or invoiced primary
  data, 2 primary data with minor estimation, 3 calculated from partial
  primary data, 4 estimated from secondary or proxy data, 5 a rough estimate
  or assumption. The method already recorded (measured, calculated,
  estimated) stays and defaults the tier (1, 3, 4) when none is given. A
  record can also carry an **uncertainty** as a percentage. The gate lists
  tier 4 and 5 records as information. Every line snapshots the method, the
  tier and the uncertainty.
- **Data-quality table.** The report prints, under Methodology, each tier's
  emissions by scope and its share of the total, an emission-weighted
  uncertainty over the lines that record one, and a statement in words. The
  accountant can add a qualitative **uncertainty statement** to the report
  header; it prints with the table.
- **Evidence.** A record accepts **evidence attachments**: files (PDF,
  images, spreadsheets, text, up to 20 MB) kept in the object store under a
  key of their own, and links to documents held elsewhere. A contractual
  instrument accepts the same. Each attachment records who added it and when.
  A line snapshots the names of the files attached when the run was
  launched; the calculation file carries them in an `evidence_files` column
  beside the record id and the evidence reference (spec 07.5).
- **Corrections with a reason and a history.** A correction still edits the
  record in place (CORRECT-01: past runs are snapshots), but it needs a
  **reason** of at least 5 characters and leaves a **revision**: who, when,
  why, and each changed field's old and new value. The record's history
  lists every revision.
- **Removals leave a tombstone.** A record, a facility or a legal entity is
  removed with a reason after a confirmation. It stays in the database with
  who removed it, when and why, and no longer appears in any list. A record
  a run calculated, a facility with records or one an unpublished inventory
  still holds in its boundary, and an entity with facilities or dependants,
  still cannot be removed. A draft inventory that had reviewed a removed
  record excludes it on its next review with the reason `RECORD_REMOVED` and
  the tombstone as the detail; until then the gate blocks the run and says
  so.
- **Justified exclusions.** A record excluded by hand for any reason the
  review cannot compute (`NON_GHG`, `DUPLICATE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`,
  `METHODOLOGY`, `OTHER`) needs a **justification** of at least 10
  characters and an **estimated magnitude** in kg CO2e (0 when the record
  emits nothing). `OUTSIDE_PERIOD` and `OUTSIDE_BOUNDARY` keep their computed
  detail. Run exclusions snapshot both; the report totals the estimated
  emissions left out per reason, says how many records carry no estimate,
  and prints each justification with its record.

Given the cyanide record at Nkran excluded as `METHODOLOGY` with "no
published factor for sodium cyanide; supplier study pending" and 8,400 kg
CO2e: the report's exclusions section shows "Methodology exclusion: 1 record,
about 8.4 t CO2e left out" and the sentence beside the record. Given the
diesel record corrected from 1,000 to 1,200 litres "after reconciliation with
the supplier invoice": its history shows the two values, the reason, the
preparer and the time, and the run launched before the correction still
reads 1,000. Given a duplicate record removed "entered twice from the same
dispensing log": the activity page no longer lists it, the inventory's gate
says it was removed, and the next review excludes it as a removed record.

## API

- `CreateActivityRequest` gains `dataQualityTier?` (1 to 5),
  `uncertaintyPercent?` and `reason?`; `PUT /activities/{id}` returns 422
  with `errors.reason` when the reason is missing or shorter than 5
  characters. `ActivityResponse` gains `dataQualityTier`,
  `dataQualityTierLabel`, `uncertaintyPercent`, `removed`, `removedAt`,
  `removedBy`, `removeReason`, `evidenceCount` and `revisionCount`.
- `GET /activities/{id}/revisions` → `{id, kind: CORRECTED|REMOVED, reason,
  changes: [{field, before, after}], changedBy, changedAt}[]`, newest first.
- `DELETE /activities/{id}?reason=`, `DELETE /facilities/{id}?reason=`,
  `DELETE /entities/{id}?reason=`: 422 `errors.reason` without a reason; the
  existing 409 rules come first.
- `GET|POST /activities/{id}/evidence` (multipart `file`),
  `POST /activities/{id}/evidence/links` `{name?, url}`, the same under
  `/market-factors/{id}/evidence`; `GET /evidence/{id}` streams the file as
  an attachment (409 for a link); `DELETE /evidence/{id}`. 422 `errors.file`
  for an empty file, one over 20 MB or a type not accepted. Tenant-checked
  through the record or the instrument (404 for an outsider).
- `ExcludeRequest` gains `justification?` and `estimatedKgCo2e?`; 422
  `errors.justification` or `errors.estimatedKgCo2e` when a manual reason
  lacks them; 409 for `RECORD_REMOVED`, which only the review records.
  `AssignmentResponse`, `RunExclusionResponse` gain `exclusionJustification`
  and `estimatedKgCo2e`; `AssignmentResponse` gains `dataQualityTier` and
  `uncertaintyPercent`.
- `RunLineResponse` gains `dataQuality`, `dataQualityTier`,
  `uncertaintyPercent` and `evidenceFiles`.
- `ReportMetadataRequest` and `InventoryResponse` gain
  `uncertaintyStatement`.
- `ReportResponse` gains `exclusionSummary: [{reason, recordCount,
  estimatedKgCo2e, estimatedTCo2e, unestimatedCount}]` and `dataQuality:
  {byTier: [{tier, label, lineCount, scope1KgCo2e, scope2KgCo2e,
  scope3KgCo2e, totalKgCo2e, sharePercent}], weightedUncertaintyPercent,
  linesWithUncertainty, lineCount, statement, uncertaintyStatement}`. The
  PDF prints both; the CSV files carry the new columns.

## Data

`V29__activity_data_quality_evidence_corrections.sql`: `data_quality_tier`
(backfilled from the method) and `uncertainty_percent` on `ghg_activities`;
`deleted_at`, `deleted_by` and `delete_reason` on `ghg_activities`,
`ghg_facilities` and `ghg_entities`; `ghg_activity_revisions`;
`ghg_evidence` (a record or an instrument, kind, name, URL or storage key,
content type, size, who and when); `exclusion_justification` and
`estimated_kg_co2e` on `ghg_assignments` and `ghg_run_exclusions`;
`RECORD_REMOVED` in the exclusion-reason checks; `data_quality`,
`data_quality_tier`, `uncertainty_percent` and `evidence_files` on
`ghg_run_lines` (backfilled from the records); `uncertainty_statement` on
`ghg_inventories`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Per-stream data-quality scores and a default tier per stream; the tier is
  recorded per record.
- Uncertainty propagation by Monte Carlo or the IPCC error propagation
  formula; the report weights the recorded percentages by emissions and says
  how many lines record one.
- Virus scanning of uploads; the server chooses the content type it serves
  and accepts documents only.
- Restoring a removed record, facility or entity; a removal is a deliberate
  act and the row stays for the audit trail.

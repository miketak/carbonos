# 03.2: Effective-dated membership

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 3 (boundary) and Chapter 5 (structural changes are
  accounted from the date they occur)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-02; implemented 2026-09-08
- **Modules**: `ghg`, `src/features/ghg` (boundary section: membership window)

## Problem

A facility acquired on 1 July is owned by the company from 1 July. Chapter 5
of the Standard makes the acquisition a structural change that triggers
base-year recalculation (spec 06). On timing, the chapter's guidance
recommends the whole-year treatment: when a significant structural change
happens mid-year, recalculate the base year for the entire year and, for
consistency, report the current year for the entire year too. Accounting only
from the transaction date is the alternative many programs use, and it is
what CarbonOS implements. The report does not yet say which convention the
inventory used, and the base-year recalculation is not yet held to the same
one; spec 06.1 adds both. Before this spec the only tool was a per-inventory
override of the share, and scaling 100% to 50% is not the same number: it
applies half a year's ownership to twelve months of activity, including the
six months the company did not own it. This spec retires that suggestion.

## Behavior

- An entity's boundary treatment has **effective from** and **effective to**
  dates, both optional; absent means unbounded within the reporting period.
  Every facility of the entity shares the window.
- An activity record counts for the inventory only when its date falls inside
  its entity's membership window. Outside it, "Review activity data"
  auto-excludes the assignment as `OUTSIDE_BOUNDARY` with an
  `exclusionDetail` naming the window, for example `Takoradi Port Co: member from
  2025-07-01`. Re-review reconciles when the window changes, as it does for
  period and membership changes.
- A boundary version records the windows, so the version states not only
  which entities and facilities but from when to when. A run computes a
  facility's share on the activity's date from the version, so a record
  outside the window that was somehow included gets no share.
- The BOUNDARY gate warns when a window starts after the period start or ends
  before the period end, so a partial-year membership is a visible, deliberate
  statement; it errors when an included record falls outside the window.

Given Takoradi terminal acquired 1 July 2025 into a 2025 inventory: the
treatment is effective from 2025-07-01; a January shiploader-diesel record is
auto-excluded as outside the boundary with "member from 2025-07-01"; a
September record is included at the full share; the run cites the version with
the window and lists the January record among its exclusions.

## API

`BoundaryTreatmentRequest` gains `effectiveFrom?`, `effectiveTo?` and
`clearWindow?` (409 when the window ends before it starts);
`BoundaryEntityResponse` and version entries carry `effectiveFrom` and
`effectiveTo`; `AssignmentResponse` and `RunExclusionResponse` carry
`exclusionDetail`.

## Data

`V12__inventory_lifecycle.sql`: `effective_from` and `effective_to` on
`ghg_boundary_treatments` (with a check that from precedes to) and on
`ghg_boundary_version_entries`; `exclusion_detail` on `ghg_assignments`.

## Events

None.

## Verification

`GhgApiIntegrationTests.aMembershipWindowExcludesRecordsOutsideItAndIsRecordedInTheVersion`:
records before and inside the window, the gate's partial-period warning, the
run at the full share with the January exclusion, the version carrying the
window, and re-review after the window moves. Frontend:
`InventoryDetailPage.test.tsx` (setting a window).

## Non-goals and open questions

Automatic base-year recalculation (spec 06 records the trigger and the
decision); a window per facility rather than per entity (a facility acquired
into an existing entity is modeled as its own entity for the year); the
whole-year convention the Standard's guidance recommends for mid-year
structural changes, and the disclosure of which convention was used (spec
06.1).

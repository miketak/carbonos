# 06.1: Recalculation policy conformance

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 5 (base year, recalculation policy, significance
  threshold, triggers, timing of recalculations), Chapter 9 (base year and
  the emissions profile over time), the 2013 amendment (the same GWP values
  for the base year and the current period)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-08 (conformance review); implemented 2026-09-08
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`BaseYear`, `BaseYearRecalculation`, `BaseYearService`,
  the BASE_YEAR gate, the report), `src/features/ghg` (Base year page, report
  base-year section)

## Problem

Spec 06 implemented the base year and the structural-change flag. The review
of 2026-09-08 found six requirements of the Standard it does not meet.

1. **The reason for the base year.** "Companies shall choose and report a
   base year for which verifiable emissions data are available and specify
   their reasons for choosing that particular year." No reason is recorded.
2. **All three triggers are mandatory.** "The following cases shall trigger
   recalculation of base year emissions": structural changes, changes in
   calculation methodology or improvements in the accuracy of factors or
   data, and the discovery of significant errors. Spec 06 lets the policy
   switch each trigger off, and raises only structural-change flags; a
   methodology change or an error correction cannot be flagged at all.
3. **The threshold is cumulative.** "While a single structural change might
   not have a significant impact on the base year emissions, the cumulative
   effect of a number of minor structural changes can result in a significant
   impact." Each freeze is measured on its own, so ten 1% divestments never
   cross a 5% threshold.
4. **Timing.** The chapter's guidance recommends that a mid-year structural
   change be recalculated for the entire year in both the base year and the
   current year. CarbonOS accounts from the transaction date through
   membership windows (spec 03.2), which is a legitimate alternative, but the
   report does not say which convention was used and nothing holds the
   recalculated base year to the same one.
5. **The same GWP values.** The amendment: companies "should use the same GWPs
   for the current inventory period and the base year". Nothing compares the
   two inventories' GWP sets.
6. **The emissions profile over time.** Chapter 9 requires "an emissions
   profile over time that is consistent with and clarifies the chosen policy
   for making base year emissions recalculations". The report shows the base
   year and the current period only.

## Behavior

- **Reason.** The base year carries a required `reason` (free text) that the
  report prints beside the year.
- **No switches.** The three trigger booleans are removed; every policy
  honors all three triggers. Structural changes are still detected at freeze.
  Methodology changes and error corrections are **raised by the accountant**:
  a recalculation candidate with a trigger type, a reason, and the affected
  percent of base-year emissions, decided like any other flag (spec 06). The
  BASE_YEAR gate treats a raised flag exactly as a detected one.
- **Cumulative threshold.** Every candidate records its own affected percent
  and the **cumulative percent**: its own plus the affected percents of the
  outstanding earlier candidates, that is every undecided one and every
  declined one raised since the last `RECALCULATED` decision. A recalculation
  resets the running sum; a refusal does not. A candidate is above the
  threshold when either figure exceeds it, and the reason names both: "1.2%
  of base-year emissions on its own, 5.4% together with 4 earlier changes
  since the 2024 base year, above the 5% threshold, recalculation required".
  A candidate with no earlier changes keeps the short form of spec 06.
- **Convention.** The base year records a `structuralChangeConvention`,
  `TRANSACTION_DATE` (the default, which membership windows implement) or
  `WHOLE_YEAR`. The report prints it. Under `WHOLE_YEAR` the BOUNDARY gate
  warns when a treatment carries a membership window, since the convention
  expects the full year of the acquired operation, and the BASE_YEAR gate
  warns when a recalculated base run's version carries a window.
- **GWP consistency.** The BASE_YEAR gate warns when the inventory's GWP set
  differs from the base-year inventory's, naming both.
- **Profile over time.** The report's base-year section lists every
  inventory of the organization whose period lies between the base year and
  the reporting period and shares the base year's consolidation approach and
  GWP set, each with its final run's total and, where a `RECALCULATED`
  decision named a later run of that inventory, the recalculated figure
  beside the original. Inventories without a final run are listed as not yet
  final. Inventories over those periods under another approach or GWP set
  are not years of the same series; the report lists them apart as other
  views, with their approach and GWP set, so a reader does not take four
  2025 views for four years.
- **Scope of the hold.** A pending recalculation above the threshold blocks
  runs only on inventories that report against the base year (the same
  approach and a later period). An equity-share view of the same year, or a
  draft for an earlier period, is warned with the reason it is not held.

Given Sankofa's 2024 base year at 27,930 kg with a 5% threshold: a 2025
freeze removes Takoradi (4.76%, below) and the accountant declines; a 2026
freeze removes Accra office (0.9%). The 2026 flag reads "0.9% on its own, 5.66%
together with 1 earlier change since the 2024 base year; above the 5%
threshold, recalculation required", and the gate blocks the 2026 run until it
is decided. An equity-share view of 2025 raised in the meantime warns: "This
inventory is not held because it is an equity share view and the base year
is operational control."

## API

- `PUT /organizations/{id}/base-year` takes `{inventoryId, thresholdPercent,
  reason, structuralChangeConvention?}` (the convention defaults to
  `TRANSACTION_DATE`); `triggers` is retired (422 with `errors.triggers` when
  sent). The response carries `reason`, `structuralChangeConvention`, and
  each recalculation's `affectedPercent`, `cumulativePercent` and
  `raisedBy`.
- `POST /organizations/{id}/base-year/recalculations` `{trigger:
  METHODOLOGY_CHANGE|ERROR_CORRECTION, reason, affectedPercent}` raises a
  candidate (201); `STRUCTURAL_CHANGE` is refused with 409 because freezes
  raise it.
- The report's `baseYear` section gains `reason`, `structuralChangeConvention`,
  `gwpSetMatches`, and `profile[]` `{inventoryId, name, year, finalRunId?,
  totalKgCo2e?, recalculatedRunId?, recalculatedTotalKgCo2e?}`.
- Validation: the BASE_YEAR gate gains the GWP warning and the convention
  warning; the BOUNDARY gate gains the window-under-whole-year warning.

## Data

## Non-goals and open questions

- Multi-year average base years, which the Standard allows. Open: whether a
  base year should be allowed to name several inventories.
- Detecting outsourcing and insourcing. The Standard exempts them from
  recalculation when the moved emissions are reported in scope 2 or scope 3;
  they stay a decision the accountant records.
- Recalculating the arithmetic itself (spec 06 non-goal).
- Whether declined candidates should count toward the cumulative percent
  forever or expire; the first version counts them.

# 02.1: Emission factor library, provenance and packs

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 6 (choosing emission factors), Chapter 9 (factor
  sources documented), Scope 2 Guidance section 6.4 (the market-based
  hierarchy), Scope 3 Standard (supplier data preferred)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F17, F19, F20, F51, ticket T-03);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`EmissionFactor`, `FactorPacks`, the factor endpoints,
  the EMISSION_FACTOR gate), `src/features/ghg` (emission factors page,
  classification picker)

## Problem

The library was seventeen seeded, read-only factors. A mining client could
not quantify most refrigerants, emulsion explosives, heavy fuel oil,
kerosene, coal, fuels by mass, well-to-tank, transmission losses, any grid
but Ghana's, upstream transport, cement, steel, waste types, flight classes,
commuting or hotel nights; seven of the fifteen scope 3 categories could not
be quantified at all. Provenance was weak: an "Ecoriv factor library 2025"
citation with no data year for a third of the inventory, "(approx.)" in
source citations, and no vintage, validity, uncertainty or URL anywhere.

## Behavior

- **One tier.** Every factor belongs to an organization. A shared, seeded,
  read-only tier existed until spec 02.10 retired it; an organization now
  starts with nothing and establishes its baseline by importing a pack. A
  factor carries: name, default scope and category, whether the physics is
  scope-agnostic, unit, per-gas components (CO2, CH4 with its fossil or
  biogenic origin, N2O, HFC and PFC mass with a blend composition, SF6, NF3,
  biogenic CO2), the CO2e per unit on the stated basis, **source**, **source
  URL**, **publication year**, **data year**, **valid from** and **valid
  to**, a **note**, and an **approval** flag. A factor used by a run cannot
  be deleted; it can be retired by setting its validity end.
- **Provenance.** Every factor cites the publication, table and data year,
  and "(approx.)" appears nowhere. This began as a rule about the seeded
  library: each of its rows was made to name its publication, say where it had
  been rounded, and admit where it was an estimate rather than a published
  figure. Spec 02.9 removed the rows whose publisher was neither DESNZ nor the
  Ghana pack, and spec 02.10 removed the rest, so the rule now falls on what a
  pack delivers and on what a preparer enters by hand: a row states its
  publication and its years, or it states that it is a supplier or study
  figure. A factor that publishes CO2e only (the Ghana grid rows) says so, the
  gate repeats it, and the report lists its emissions on one row (spec 07.7).
- **Packs.** The server ships two importable packs, built from published
  tables (spec 02.9): `defra-2026` (UK DESNZ conversion factors 2026: fuels by
  mass, volume and energy, bioenergy, refrigerants including the Montreal
  Protocol gases, UK electricity and T&D, well-to-tank, water, materials,
  waste types, flights by class and haul, land and sea travel, hotel nights,
  freight) and `ghana` (Ember Ghana by year and a derived transmission-loss
  factor, unapproved until the year's loss rate is confirmed). Eight further
  packs shipped until 2026-09-15, among them the US EPA Hub, Ember's national
  grids, IPCC 2006 process defaults and three sector packs; spec 02.9 removed
  them, and a curator authors any further publication in the console rather
  than a release adding one. A pack lists its source, URL, publication year,
  GWP basis, licence and retrieval date. Each factor row also carries the
  provenance of the publication it comes from and keeps its row identifier, which is the factor's identity
  within an organization (spec 02.3): a re-import updates, and a second
  pack that selects the same row adds its tag rather than a copy. Importing
  a pack creates organization factors, approved unless the pack marks a
  factor as needing review. A factor entered by hand arrives **unapproved**:
  use in a run is a review step (Corporate Standard chapter 7, ISO 14064-1
  section 8.1), not a box nobody unticked. The form takes the seven gas
  groups of chapter 4 (CO2, CH4, N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6, NF3 in kg per unit) and,
  for a blend, its composition by mass fraction and the GWP basis of the
  published figure; fractions that do not add up to 1 are refused on the
  field, because a split that counts part of the gas twice or not at all is
  not a composition.
- **Gate.** The EMISSION_FACTOR gate errors when an included record is
  classified with a factor that is not approved, and warns when the
  factor's validity does not cover the inventory period or when a factor
  publishes CO2e only, so its emissions appear in the by-gas table on the
  reconciling row of spec 07.7 rather than under CO2, CH4 or N2O.
- **Units.** The registry gains `mmBtu`, `scf`, `MWh`-based grid rows,
  `tonne-km` and `short-ton-mile` (freight), `room-night` and
  `vehicle-km` and `vehicle-mile` as aliases, so the packs' units convert.
- The picker offers the organization's factors as one list (spec 02.10),
  filtered as before by the record's unit and stream, with each option's
  publication, year, tags and approval shown and unapproved rows hidden until
  revealed (spec 02.3).

Given Asante Gold Resources importing `defra-2026` and `ghana`: refrigerants,
HFO, coal, LPG per tonne, diesel per tonne and per litre, well-to-tank diesel,
the Ghana grid by data year, Ghana T&D losses, construction concrete and
metals, landfill waste by type, flights by class and bus commuting appear as
the organization's factors with their citations, and the classification picker
offers them. Explosives, cement, quicklime and a gas recorded as itself are
not among them: neither publication publishes such a factor, so the
organization enters its own (spec 02.9).

## API

- `GET /organizations/{id}/emission-factors` → the organization's factors,
  each with `organizationId`, `sourceUrl`, `publicationYear`, `dataYear`,
  `validFrom`, `validTo`, `note`, `approved`, `pack`, `packCode` and
  `co2eOnly`. Spec 02.10 removed `GET /emission-factors`, which returned the
  shared library, and the `tier` parameter that chose between the two.
- `POST /organizations/{id}/emission-factors`, `PUT|DELETE
  /emission-factors/{factorId}` (organization factors only; 409 to edit or
  delete a library factor, 409 to delete a factor a run applied);
  `POST /emission-factors/{factorId}/approve` and `/unapprove`.
- `GET /factor-packs` → `{id, name, source, sourceUrl, publicationYear,
  gwpBasis, license, retrieved, factorCount, notes}[]`;
  `GET /factor-packs/{packId}` → the pack with its factors;
  `POST /organizations/{id}/factor-packs/{packId}/import` → `{created,
  updated}`.

`publication_year`, `data_year`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `note`,
`approved`, `pack`, `pack_code`, `created_at` and `updated_at` on
`ghg_emission_factors`; the seeded rows restated with their citations, which
`V50` and `V51` later removed. The
packs were JSON files compiled into the server when this spec shipped. They
are now rows of the catalogue (`ghg_factor_packs`,
`ghg_factor_pack_editions`, `ghg_factor_pack_rows`), authored and published
in the console of spec 02.5; `V43__seed_factor_pack_editions.sql` carried the
ten shipped packs across unchanged.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Uncertainty percentages per factor (ticket T-14 handles data quality).
- Cyanide, grinding media, purchased lime and cement, explosives, land
  clearing, and tailings and mine-water treatment have no published factor.
  The mining pack shipped supplier-factor templates for some of them until
  spec 02.9 removed it, so each is now a supplier or study factor entered by
  hand.
- Gases outside the scopes (Montreal Protocol): spec 02.4, and the DESNZ pack
  carries the rows. Gases emitted as themselves left with spec 02.9.
- Automatic vintage updates when a new edition is published; a new pack is
  imported and the old factors retired by validity date.

- **Protocol**: Chapter 6 (choosing emission factors), Chapter 9 (factor

  hierarchy), Scope 3 Standard (supplier data preferred)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F17, F19, F20, F51, ticket T-03);

- **Modules**: `ghg` (`EmissionFactor`, `FactorPacks`, the factor endpoints,

## Problem

The library was seventeen seeded, read-only factors. A mining client could
not quantify most refrigerants, emulsion explosives, heavy fuel oil,
kerosene, coal, fuels by mass, well-to-tank, transmission losses, any grid
but Ghana's, upstream transport, cement, steel, waste types, flight classes,
commuting or hotel nights; seven of the fifteen scope 3 categories could not
be quantified at all. Provenance was weak: an "Ecoriv factor library 2025"
citation with no data year for a third of the inventory, "(approx.)" in
source citations, and no vintage, validity, uncertainty or URL anywhere.

## Behavior

- **Two tiers.** The **shared library** (seeded, read-only, visible to every
  organization) and each organization's **own factors**. An organization's
  factor carries: name, default scope and category, whether the physics is
  scope-agnostic, unit, per-gas components (CO2, CH4 with its fossil or
  biogenic origin, N2O, HFC and PFC mass with a blend composition, SF6, NF3,
  biogenic CO2), the CO2e per unit on the stated basis, **source**, **source
  URL**, **publication year**, **data year**, **valid from** and **valid
  to**, a **note**, and an **approval** flag. A factor used by a run cannot
  be deleted; it can be retired by setting its validity end.
- **Provenance in the seed.** Every seeded factor cites the publication,
  table and data year. "(approx.)" is gone: the Ghana grid factor says it is
  a secondary estimate with no published derivation and points to Ember's
  2024 figure in the `ghana` pack; the diesel factor is the UK 100% mineral
  diesel row, rounded, with the published components stated; ANFO cites the Australian National Greenhouse Accounts Factors; quicklime
  cites IPCC 2006 Volume 3 Table 2.4; the district cooling factor is marked
  as an assumption that needs a supplier figure. Seeded values are kept as
  they were (the run arithmetic and every earlier report rest on them); the
  citations say where each was rounded, and the packs carry the unrounded
  published rows. A factor that publishes CO2e only (the Ember grid rows)
  says so, and the gate repeats it.
- **Packs.** The server ships importable packs built from published tables:
  `defra-2026` (UK DESNZ conversion factors 2026: fuels by mass, volume and
  energy, bioenergy, refrigerants, UK electricity and T&D, well-to-tank,
  water, materials, waste types, flights by class and haul, land and sea
  travel, hotel nights, freight), `epa-hub-2025` (US EPA Hub: combustion per
  US unit, eGRID location-based, steam, freight, travel, WARM waste),
  `ember-grid-2025` (national grid intensities for every country Ember
  covers, by data year), `refrigerants-ar5` (every EPA Hub refrigerant with
  its composition, so blends follow the inventory's GWP set),
  `ipcc-2006-process` (clinker, lime, glass, ammonia), `nga-2024-explosives`,
  `ghana` (Ember Ghana by year and a derived transmission-loss factor,
  unapproved until the year's loss rate is confirmed), and three sector
  packs, `sector-mining`, `sector-oil-and-gas` and `sector-construction`,
  which select the streams those clients need from the packs above. A pack
  lists its source, URL, publication year, GWP basis, licence and retrieval
  date; each factor keeps its row identifier so a re-import updates rather
  than duplicates. Importing a pack creates organization factors, approved
  unless the pack marks a factor as needing review.
- **Gate.** The EMISSION_FACTOR gate errors when an included record is
  classified with a factor that is not approved, and warns when the
  factor's validity does not cover the inventory period or when a factor
  publishes CO2e only, so the by-gas table omits its methane and nitrous
  oxide.
- **Units.** The registry gains `mmBtu`, `scf`, `MWh`-based grid rows,
  `tonne-km` and `short-ton-mile` (freight), `room-night` and
  `vehicle-km` and `vehicle-mile` as aliases, so the packs' units convert.
- The picker offers the shared library and the organization's factors
  together, filtered as before by the record's unit and stream.

Given Asante Gold Resources importing `sector-mining`: R-407C, HFO, coal,
LPG per tonne, diesel per tonne, well-to-tank diesel, Ghana T&D losses,
explosives, lime, cement, steel, landfill waste by type, flights by class
and bus commuting appear as the organization's factors with their citations,
and the classification picker offers them.

## API

- `GET /organizations/{id}/emission-factors` → the shared library and the
  organization's factors, each with `organizationId` (null for the
  library), `sourceUrl`, `publicationYear`, `dataYear`, `validFrom`,
  `validTo`, `note`, `approved`, `pack`, `packCode` and `co2eOnly`.
  `GET /emission-factors` keeps returning the shared library.
- `POST /organizations/{id}/emission-factors`, `PUT|DELETE
  /emission-factors/{factorId}` (organization factors only; 409 to edit or
  delete a library factor, 409 to delete a factor a run applied);
  `POST /emission-factors/{factorId}/approve` and `/unapprove`.
- `GET /factor-packs` → `{id, name, source, sourceUrl, publicationYear,
  gwpBasis, license, retrieved, factorCount, notes}[]`;
  `GET /factor-packs/{packId}` → the pack with its factors;
  `POST /organizations/{id}/factor-packs/{packId}/import` → `{created,
  updated}`.

## Data

`V27__emission_factor_library.sql`: `organization_id`, `source_url`,
`publication_year`, `data_year`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `note`,
`approved`, `pack`, `pack_code`, `created_at` and `updated_at` on
`ghg_emission_factors`; the seeded rows restated with their citations. The
packs are JSON files under `backend/src/main/resources/factor-packs/`,
generated from the published tables by `scripts/gen-factor-packs.py`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Uncertainty percentages per factor (ticket T-14 handles data quality).
- Cyanide, tailings, grinding media wear and land-use change have no
  published factor; a supplier or study factor is entered by hand.
- Automatic vintage updates when a new edition is published; a new pack is
  imported and the old factors retired by validity date.

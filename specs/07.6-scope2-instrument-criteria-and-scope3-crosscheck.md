# 07.6: Scope 2 instrument criteria and the scope 3 declaration cross-check

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Scope 2 Guidance chapter 7 (the Scope 2 Quality Criteria,
  tested per instrument, with retirement statements and vintage), Scope 3
  Standard chapter 11 (a reported category is quantified, or the report says
  why not)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F23, F32; tickets T-02, T-09);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`MarketFactor`, `Scope2Criterion`, the declaration,
  the CLASSIFICATION and EMISSION_FACTOR gates, the report composite and
  exports), `src/features/ghg` (instruments card, declaration card, report
  page)

## Problem

"Meets the eight Scope 2 Quality Criteria" was one checkbox and a free-text
note. Nothing captured the certificate number, the registry, the vintage or
the retirement date a verifier tests, and nothing said which criterion an
instrument failed. The scope 3 declaration printed verbatim: categories 3,
4 and 15 were declared as covered with zero lines, and a reader took
"covered" to mean quantified.

## Behavior

- **Eight answers, not one.** An instrument answers each Scope 2 Quality
  Criterion on its own: met, not met, or not yet answered. Criteria 1 to 6
  shorten Table 7.1 of the Guidance (conveys the attribute, is the only
  claim, is retired for the company, vintage close to consumption, same
  market, supplier factor net of certificates sold); 7 and 8 restate its
  residual-mix and documentation requirements. An instrument **meets the
  criteria** only when all eight are met; while any is unanswered or not
  met it is not applied, the gate says which and how many, and the run's
  market-based figure falls back as spec 07.3 describes.
- **Certificate details.** An instrument records its certificate or
  contract reference, the registry, the vintage year and the retirement
  date, and accepts evidence attachments (spec 04.4): a registry
  attestation, a retirement statement, a supplier certificate.
- **The report tests per instrument.** The report's scope 2 section lists,
  per instrument, the eight outcomes, the certificate details and whether
  it was applied. The PDF prints the same.
- **The declaration cross-check.** The declaration can mark a declared
  category as **not quantified this year** with a reason of at least 10
  characters. The pre-flight warns for each declared category with no
  included record classified into it, no derived line an upstream rule
  would produce (spec 04.7), and no such reason, and for each
  category with records that is not declared. The report prints the
  declaration as a table: each declared category with its total, its
  lines, or "declared, not quantified: reason"; categories with lines but
  no declaration are listed as reported but not declared.

Given the Nkran I-REC instrument with certificate IREC-GH-2025-0417,
registry I-TRACK, vintage 2025, retired on 15 January 2026, and every
criterion met: the run applies it and the report lists the eight outcomes.
Given criterion 3 answered "not met" because the certificates were retired
in the developer's account: the gate says "1 of the eight criteria not
met", the instrument is not applied, and the report says why. Given
category 15 investments declared with "the associate reports its own
inventory; equity share to be quantified from its 2025 report": the
pre-flight is silent and the report prints the reason.

## API

- `MarketFactorRequest` gains `criteria` (eight booleans, null for
  unanswered; 422 `errors.criteria` for any other length), `certificateId`,
  `registry`, `vintage` and `retirementDate`; `meetsQualityCriteria` stays
  accepted for older clients (true answers all eight as met, false leaves
  them unanswered) and is ignored when `criteria` is given.
  `MarketFactorResponse` gains `criteria: [{code, title, answer}]`,
  `unansweredCount`, `notMetCount`, `certificateId`, `registry`, `vintage`
  and `retirementDate`; `meetsQualityCriteria` is derived.
- `OperationalBoundaryRequest` gains `notQuantified: [{category, reason}]`;
  409 for a category not declared; 422 `errors.notQuantified` for a reason
  under 10 characters. `InventoryResponse` and the report's
  `operationalBoundary` gain `notQuantified`.
- `ReportResponse.byScope3Category` covers every declared category:
  `declared`, `lineCount` (0 when none; derived lines of spec 04.7 count),
  `notQuantifiedReason`.

## Data

`V33__scope2_criteria_and_scope3_crosscheck.sql`: `certificate_id`,
`registry`, `vintage`, `retirement_date` and `criteria_answers` (one
character per criterion) on `ghg_market_factors`, backfilled to all met
where the flag was set; `scope3_not_quantified` on `ghg_inventories`.

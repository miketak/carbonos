# 01.4: Role-aware UI and visible refusals

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-12)
- **Protocol**: Corporate Standard chapter 7 (defined roles and a review
  step), ISO 14064-3 (a verifier relies on what the system shows during
  evidence gathering)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-12 (audit 2026-09-11, findings F2 and F4); implemented
  2026-09-12
- **Modules**: `src/features/ghg` (every page and form under an
  organization), `src/lib/api.ts` (problem details), `src/components`
  (toast, read-only banner)

## Problem

Spec 01.2 made the server refuse writes by role. The screen does not know
about roles. Signed in as a `VERIFIER`, the audit saw **Edit** and
**Delete** on the organization card, **Import CSV** and **+ Add activity**
on the register, **Create correction**, **Review activity data** and
**Launch calculation run** on the inventory page. The drawer opened,
accepted a record, and **Save** produced a 403 that the screen never showed.
The drawer stayed open with no message. A verifier given read-only access
will try to attach a note; a silent failure looks like a bug or, worse, like
a saved change. **Add member** with an email that has no account returned
404 and the form kept the email and said nothing.

## Behavior

### Controls follow the role

- Every page under an organization reads `myRole` from the organization
  and shows write controls only to roles the server accepts for them. The
  role sets: **write** is `PREPARER`, `REVIEWER`, `OWNER` and `ADMIN`;
  **approve** is `REVIEWER`, `OWNER` and `ADMIN`; **owner** is `OWNER` and
  `ADMIN`. `ADMIN` means active support access (spec 01.3).
- The controls and their sets:
  - Owner: organization **Edit** on the card and the overview. Deleting the
    organization, **Add member**, the role select and **Remove** on the
    members card need an owner **by membership**: support access never
    grants deletion or membership changes (spec 01.3), so they are hidden
    or disabled for an `ADMIN` too.
  - Write: **+ Add activity**, **Import CSV**, the drawer's **Save**,
    **Promote**, **Correct** and **Remove**, bulk **Remove ... selected**,
    source document upload and removal; **Add legal entity**, **Add
    facility**, entity and facility **Edit** and **Remove**, the stream
    register's add, edit and remove; **Add factor**, **Approve**,
    **Unapprove**, **Import pack**, factor edit and delete; custom units
    and densities; **New inventory** and **Edit inventory**; boundary
    ticks, windows, overrides and exclusions; the operational boundary
    declaration; contractual instruments and the residual mix; base-year
    policy and candidate outcomes; **Review activity data**, classify,
    exclude and include; **Freeze**, **Reopen as draft**; **Launch
    calculation run**; **Void**; the report metadata card.
  - Approve: **Mark as final**, **Withdraw final**, **Publish**, **Create
    correction**.
  - Everyone: **New organization** on the GHG home (anyone creates their
    own and becomes its owner), every read, every export and download.
- A control outside the caller's set is **hidden** when it is the only
  content of its region (a form, an empty-state call to action) and
  **disabled** when it sits beside read content (a row action, a lifecycle
  button), so the page keeps its shape. A disabled control carries a
  tooltip and `aria-describedby` text naming the role: "Needs the Preparer,
  Reviewer or Owner role."
- A `VERIFIER` sees a banner at the top of every organization page: "Your
  role in this organization is Verifier (read-only)." The drawer opens for a
  verifier in read mode: facts, evidence and history, no fields.
- The role is a display concern only. The server checks of spec 01.2 stay
  the authority; a request sent around the screen is still refused.

### Refusals are shown where the action happened

- The `api()` wrapper already throws an `ApiError` carrying the RFC 9457
  problem detail. Every mutation renders its failure:
  - inside a form or drawer: the problem's `detail` under the fields as an
    alert, field errors from a 422 inline as today; the form stays open with
    the typed values;
  - a row action, a lifecycle button or a download with no form open: a
    toast with the `detail`, tone `error`.
- The detail for a 403 is the server's sentence ("This action needs the
  PREPARER, REVIEWER or OWNER role in the organization."). The screen
  prefixes it with "Your role is read-only in this organization." when
  `myRole` is `VERIFIER`.
- A 404 from a write is shown, never swallowed: "The item was not found. It
  may have been removed by someone else." with the server's detail when it
  has one, and the list refreshes.
- A 409 renders the server's detail as it stands (it names the rule:
  "needs at least one owner", "cannot be removed while a run applied it").
- **Add member** with an unknown email shows, under the field: "No account
  with that email. Add the user under Manage users first." For a platform
  administrator the sentence ends with a link to `/admin/users`; for an
  owner who is not an administrator it ends with "Ask a platform
  administrator to add them." A pending account is accepted (spec 01.2).
- A network failure or a 5xx shows "CarbonOS could not reach the server.
  Try again." in the same place.

Given Abena Owusu as `VERIFIER` of Asante Gold Resources: her GHG home
shows the organization card without **Edit** and **Delete**; the register
shows the banner, no **Import CSV** and no **+ Add activity**; a record
opens in a read-only drawer; the inventory page shows **Launch calculation
run** disabled with "Needs the Preparer, Reviewer or Owner role." Given
Kofi as `PREPARER`: **Mark as final** and **Publish** are disabled with
"Needs the Reviewer or Owner role." and the members card has no form. Given
an owner typing `nobody@example.com` under **Add member**: the message and
the "Ask a platform administrator" sentence appear under the field, the
email stays typed.

## API

None new. The screen uses `OrganizationResponse.myRole` (spec 01.2, with
`ADMIN` as defined by spec 01.3) and the problem details the server already
returns. The `api()` wrapper gains one helper, `refusalMessage(error,
myRole)`, that returns the sentence to show for a 403, 404, 409 or network
failure so every page prints the same wording.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Per-inventory roles or a formal sign-off state; the roles stay
  organization-wide (spec 01.2 non-goal).
- Hiding navigation entries by role; every page stays reachable read-only.
- Optimistic UI or offline queues; a refused write is shown, not retried.
- Whether **New organization** should be reserved to administrators on a
  hosted deployment; today anyone can create one, and this spec keeps it.

# 07.2: Required disclosures

- **Status**: Draft
- **Protocol**: Chapter 9 (required information), Chapter 4 (scope 2 covers
  electricity, steam, heat, and cooling), the 2013 amendment (gases in
  tonnes, the source of the GWP values), the Scope 2 Guidance Chapter 7
  (Scope 2 Quality Criteria, residual mix, the method behind a single total)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-08 (conformance review)
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`ActivityCategory`, `EmissionFactor`, `MarketFactor`,
  boundary treatments, the report endpoint), `src/features/ghg` (report page,
  market-factor card, boundary section)

## Problem

Spec 07.1 made the report carry the Chapter 9 elements. The review of
2026-09-08 found the following stated conditions unmet.

1. **Units and gas masses.** The amendment requires "emissions data for all
   seven GHGs separately in metric tonnes and in tonnes of CO2 equivalent".
   The report is in kilograms, and HFCs and PFCs appear as CO2e only because
   the factors carry blends as CO2e.
2. **The source of the potentials.** The amendment requires the report to
   "report the source of the GWP values and indicate if multiple Assessment
   Reports have been used". The methodology statement names the inventory's
   set and says that blends keep the source's potentials, but not which
   assessment report the source used.
3. **Cooling.** Chapter 4 defines scope 2 to cover electricity, steam,
   heating, and cooling. There is no category for purchased cooling.
4. **Scope 2 Quality Criteria.** The Guidance: "Companies shall ensure that
   any contractual instruments used in the market-based method total meet the
   Scope 2 Quality Criteria ... If instruments do not meet the Criteria, then
   other data shall be used as an alternative". Nothing records whether an
   instrument meets them.
5. **Residual mix.** "If a residual mix is not currently available, reporters
   shall note that an adjusted emissions factor is not available or has not
   been estimated to account for voluntary purchases and this may result in
   double counting between electricity consumers." Nothing records or prints
   this.
6. **The method behind a single total.** "If reporting a single corporate
   inventory total ... companies shall disclose which method was chosen." The
   run total uses the location-based figure and the report does not say so.
   The Guidance also asks for the method used for the base year's scope 2 and
   whether historic location-based data stands as a proxy for market-based.
7. **Excluded operations.** Chapter 9 requires "any specific exclusions of
   sources, facilities, and/or operations". The report lists excluded records
   and zero-share entities, but an entity or facility of the organization that
   the accountant simply did not tick into the boundary is not reported at
   all, and nothing asks for a reason.

## Behavior

- **Tonnes.** The report presents every emission figure in metric tonnes to
  three decimals, with the kilogram figure retained on lines for the
  arithmetic. Storage stays in kilograms.
- **Gas masses.** An HFC or PFC factor records the mass of gas per unit and
  the blend's GWP with its assessment report, in place of a CO2e-only
  component. Run lines and totals carry the mass for all seven gases, and the
  by-gas table shows mass and CO2e for each. The methodology statement names
  the inventory's assessment report and, when any factor's blend used
  another, names it and says that more than one report was used.
- **Cooling.** `PURCHASED_COOLING` joins the scope 2 categories; the
  declaration and the report label the scope 2 rows "electricity, steam, heat,
  and cooling".
- **Quality Criteria.** A market factor records `meetsQualityCriteria`
  (required) and `qualityNotes`. When false, the run does not apply the
  instrument: the facility's market-based figure falls back to the residual
  mix recorded for the inventory, or to location-based when there is none,
  and the line says why. The report lists instruments that did not meet the
  criteria with their notes.
- **Residual mix.** An inventory with any market factor records
  `residualMixAvailable`. When false, the report prints the Guidance's
  sentence that an adjusted factor was not available and double counting
  between consumers may result.
- **Method disclosure.** The report states that the inventory total uses the
  location-based scope 2 figure, states the base-year inventory's scope 2
  method, and states whether the base year's market-based figure is a
  location-based proxy (true when the base-year run had no instruments).
- **Excluded operations.** Every entity of the organization not in the
  boundary, and every facility of a member entity not ticked in, needs a
  **boundary exclusion** with a reason from the exclusion list (spec 04) and a
  detail. The BOUNDARY gate errors on an entity or facility that is neither
  in the boundary nor excluded with a reason, so a boundary cannot be frozen
  with an undocumented omission. The version copies the exclusions, and the
  report lists them under "Operations excluded from the boundary" beside the
  record exclusions.

Given an inventory whose only instrument is a supplier certificate that fails
criterion 5 (sourced from another market): the accountant records
`meetsQualityCriteria` false with a note; the run reports that facility
market-based at the residual mix; the report names the instrument, the
criterion, and the fallback, and prints scope 2 in tonnes both ways.

## API

- `EmissionFactorResponse.gases` gains `hfcsKg`, `pfcsKg`, and
  `blendGwpSource`; `RunResponse.byGas` and `RunLineResponse.byGas` carry mass
  for every gas.
- `ReportResponse` figures gain `Tonnes` fields beside the kilogram ones;
  `methodology` gains `assessmentReports[]` and `multipleAssessmentReports`;
  `emissions` gains `totalMethod: LOCATION_BASED`, `baseYearScope2Method`,
  and `baseYearMarketBasedIsProxy`; `exclusions` gains `boundary[]`.
- `MarketFactorRequest` gains `meetsQualityCriteria` (required) and
  `qualityNotes?`; `PUT /inventories/{id}` gains `residualMixAvailable?`.
- `PUT /inventories/{id}/boundary/entities/{entityId}/exclude` and
  `PUT /inventories/{id}/boundary/{facilityId}/exclude` `{reason, detail?}`;
  `DELETE` clears the exclusion. 409 unless the inventory is a draft.
- `ActivityCategory` gains `PURCHASED_COOLING`.

## Data

`V19__required_disclosures.sql`: `hfcs_kg_per_unit`, `pfcs_kg_per_unit`, and
`blend_gwp_source` on `ghg_emission_factors`, with the seed's blends restated
as mass plus their DEFRA-published potentials; `hfcs_kg` and `pfcs_kg` on
runs and lines; `meets_quality_criteria` and `quality_notes` on
`ghg_market_factors` (backfilled true, notes "not assessed before 07.2");
`residual_mix_available` on `ghg_inventories`; `ghg_boundary_exclusions`
(inventory, entity or facility, reason, detail) and
`ghg_boundary_version_exclusions`; the category check widened to
`PURCHASED_COOLING`.

## Verification

`GhgApiIntegrationTests`: the by-gas table carries mass for an HFC factor and
the methodology names two assessment reports when a blend differs; a failing
instrument falls back and prints; a missing residual mix prints the
disclosure; the total method and the base-year proxy print; an unticked
facility blocks the freeze until excluded with a reason and then prints under
boundary exclusions; a purchased-cooling record classifies into scope 2.
Frontend: `RunDetailPage.test.tsx` (tonnes, boundary exclusions, disclosures),
`InventoryDetailPage.test.tsx` (quality criteria, excluding an entity).

## Non-goals and open questions

- The emission factor hierarchies of the Guidance (Tables 6.2 and 6.3) and
  certificate tracking or retirement records.
- Whether a boundary exclusion should be required for the reporting company's
  own facilities only, or for every entity the organization lists. The first
  version requires it for every entity and facility, because completeness of
  the boundary is what a verifier checks.
- An assurance statement and PDF export (spec 07 non-goals).

- **Owner**: Michael Takrama

- **Modules**: `ghg` (`GhgRunFactor`, run lines, `Inventory`, `Organization`,

## Problem

The report gave scope 1, scope 2 both ways, scope 3 and a total, and nothing
per scope 3 category, facility, legal entity or country; a reader had to sum
the snapshot lines. Section 08 listed factors by name only, with no value,
unit, gas split, vintage or source. Nothing said who prepared the report,
who approved it, when, for which entity at which address, whether it was
assured, or which version of the period's report it was. The methodology
text cited internal spec numbers.

## Behavior

- **Breakdown tables.** The report carries emissions by **scope 3
  category**, by **facility**, by **legal entity** and by **country**, each
  in kilograms and tonnes and, for facilities and entities, split by scope.
  Every line snapshots the facility's legal entity and country at run time,
  so the tables read the same after a facility moves or is renamed.
- **Country.** A facility records a country (an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code,
  optional). Lines without one are grouped under "not recorded".
- **Factor table.** A run snapshots every emission factor it applied into
  `ghg_run_factors`: id, name, unit, the CO2e per unit under the run's GWP
  set, the per-gas components, the blend composition, methane origin, source
  and the GWP set. The report prints the table; the frozen factor set is also
  what the export of spec 07.5 serializes.
- **Header.** The report opens with a header: the reporting entity and its
  address and contact (recorded on the organization), the reporting period
  and its label, **prepared by** (who launched the run; every run records
  its author), **approved by** (who published the inventory, recorded at
  publication; an override can be typed before publication), the dates the
  run was calculated and the inventory was published, the report **version**
  in the supersession chain (1 for the first inventory of the period, one
  more for each correction, with the names of the inventories it supersedes
  and the one that supersedes it), and the **assurance** level (`UNVERIFIED`,
  `LIMITED`, `REASONABLE`), provider and statement reference. Assurance and
  the approver override are edited on the inventory while it is not
  published.
- **Intensity.** An inventory may record intensity denominators (name,
  value, unit: "Gold produced, 120,000 oz"); the report prints total tCO2e
  per unit of each.
- **Wording.** The methodology statement cites the Corporate Standard by
  chapter and the Scope 2 Guidance by chapter; internal spec numbers do not
  appear in the report page.

Given the Asante FY2025 run: section 04 shows Obuom and Nkran by scope,
Asante Gold Resources and Tarkwa Mine Ltd by scope, Ghana as the only
country, and purchased goods, fuel- and energy-related and business travel
as the scope 3 categories with figures; section 08 lists Diesel at 2.66 kg
CO2e per litre, CO2 2.6307, CH4 0.0001 (fossil), N2O 0.0001, from the UK
Government conversion factors 2025, under AR5.

## API

- `FacilityRequest` and `FacilityResponse` gain `country?`;
  `OrganizationRequest` and `OrganizationResponse` gain `address?` and
  `contact?`.
- `PUT /inventories/{id}/report-metadata` `{approvedBy?, assuranceLevel,
  assuranceProvider?, assuranceStatement?, intensityMetrics[]}` (409 once
  published); `InventoryResponse` carries them plus `publishedBy`.
- `RunResponse` gains `createdBy`; `RunLineResponse` gains `entityId`,
  `entityName` and `country`.
- `ReportResponse` gains `header`, `byScope3Category[]`, `byFacility[]`,
  `byEntity[]`, `byCountry[]`, `factors[]` and `intensity[]`.

## Data

`V24__report_tables_and_metadata.sql`: `country` on `ghg_facilities`;
`address` and `contact` on `ghg_organizations`; `entity_id`, `entity_name`
and `country` on `ghg_run_lines` (backfilled from the facilities);
`created_by` on `ghg_runs`; `ghg_run_factors`; `approved_by`, `published_by`,
`assurance_level`, `assurance_provider` and `assurance_statement` on
`ghg_inventories`; `ghg_intensity_metrics`.

## Non-goals and open questions

  what the library records, and the library's provenance is ticket T-03.
- Business-unit breakdown beyond the legal entity.
- Preparer and approver as roles with a review step (ticket T-22); the
  header records who did what.

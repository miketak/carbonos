# 07.3: Scope 2 instrument coverage and dual reporting

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Scope 2 Guidance chapter 4 (dual reporting), section 6.2
  (instruments apply to the megawatt-hours they cover), section 7.4 (the
  balance takes the residual mix or the grid average), Corporate Standard
  chapter 9 as amended in 2015
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F31 and F34, ticket T-01);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`MarketFactor`, the run, the EMISSION_FACTOR gate, the
  report), `src/features/ghg` (market-factor card, report page, lines table)

## Problem

Spec 07.1 made a contractual instrument a factor per facility, applied to
every scope 2 electricity line of that facility. The audit of 2026-09-08
found two consequences.

1. **Partial coverage cannot be expressed.** A power purchase agreement
   covering 20,000 MWh of a plant's 46,500 MWh zeroed the whole plant. The
   Guidance applies an instrument to the megawatt-hours it covers and prices
   the balance at the residual mix, or at the grid average where no residual
   mix is published. The market-based figure was 1,475 tCO2e against a
   correct 13,162 tCO2e, in the direction that flatters the client.
2. **Without instruments there was no market-based figure.** The report
   printed location-based only. Any company with operations in a market that
   has contractual instruments (Ghana has I-RECs) shall report both methods.
   Where no instruments are held, the market-based figure is the residual mix
   or, failing that, the grid average, and it must still be printed.

## Behavior

- **Coverage.** An instrument records the quantity it covers, in kWh
  (entered as MWh in the page), and optionally the period it covers; without
  a period it covers the inventory's period. One instrument per facility and
  inventory, as before.
- **Allocation.** The run walks the facility's scope 2 electricity lines
  dated inside the instrument's period in date order, earliest first, and
  applies the instrument to each line's kWh until the covered quantity is
  used up. What the instrument does not cover is the **balance**. An
  instrument that fails the Scope 2 Quality Criteria covers nothing (spec
  07.2). Instruments recorded before this spec have no covered quantity and
  cover every kWh, as they did.
- **The balance and lines without an instrument** are priced at the
  residual mix the inventory records (spec 07.2), or at the line's
  location-based factor (the grid average) when no residual mix is
  available or its availability is not stated. Each line records its
  covered kWh, its balance kWh, the balance factor and its basis
  (`RESIDUAL_MIX` or `GRID_AVERAGE`), and a note that prints the split:
  "20,000,000 kWh at 0 kg/kWh (contract), 26,500,000 kWh at 0.441 kg/kWh
  (grid average; no residual mix is available)".
- **Purchased heat, steam, and cooling** have no contractual instrument in
  CarbonOS; their market-based figure equals their location-based figure
  and the line says so. A scope 2 line recorded in a unit that does not
  convert to kWh is treated the same way and the gate warns, as before.
- **Every run reports both methods.** The run total, the report, and the
  page carry the market-based scope 2 figure on every run, with its
  **basis**: `INSTRUMENTS` when any instrument was applied, `RESIDUAL_MIX`
  when none was and a residual mix priced the lines, or `GRID_AVERAGE` when
  the location-based factors stood in. The report's methodology says which,
  and the residual-mix disclosure prints on every run: the factor when one
  is available, the Guidance's double-counting sentence when none is, and
  "the inventory does not state whether a residual mix is available" until
  the accountant says. The base year's method and proxy disclosures (spec
  07.2) follow the basis: a base-year run with the `GRID_AVERAGE` basis is
  a location-based proxy.
- **The gate.** The EMISSION_FACTOR gate warns when the residual-mix
  availability is not stated (on every inventory now, since every run
  reports market-based), when an instrument covers more kWh than the
  facility's scope 2 electricity in its period, and when an instrument's
  period lies outside the inventory's.

Given the Obuom plant with 46,500 MWh of grid electricity at 0.441 kg/kWh,
a PPA at 0 kg/kWh covering 20,000 MWh, and no residual mix: the line reports
location-based 20,506.5 tCO2e and market-based 11,686.5 tCO2e, and the note
prints the split. With a residual mix of 0.5 kg/kWh recorded, the
market-based figure is 13,250 tCO2e.

## API

- `MarketFactorRequest` gains `coveredKwh` (required, positive),
  `periodStart?` and `periodEnd?` (422 when the end precedes the start);
  `MarketFactorResponse` carries them (null coverage on rows older than this
  spec).
- `RunLineResponse` gains `marketCoveredKwh`, `marketBalanceKwh`,
  `marketBalanceKgCo2ePerKwh` and `marketBalanceBasis`; `marketBasedKgCo2e`
  is set on every scope 2 line. `RunResponse.scope2MarketBasedKgCo2e` is
  never null and the run gains `scope2MarketBasis`.
- `ReportResponse.emissions` gains `scope2MarketBasis`;
  `scope2MarketBasedKgCo2e`, `scope2MarketBasedTCo2e` and
  `residualMixDisclosure` are never null.

## Non-goals and open questions

- More than one instrument per facility and inventory, and instruments that
  span facilities. One instrument per facility covers the common case; a
  portfolio of certificates is recorded as one instrument with its total
  covered quantity.
- The eight Quality Criteria one at a time, certificate identifiers,
  registry, vintage and retirement (ticket T-02).
- Instruments for purchased heat, steam and cooling.

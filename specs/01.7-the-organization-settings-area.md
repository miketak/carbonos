# 01.7: The organization settings area

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-16); amended 2026-09-29, approved by the
  owner: the History card records changes to the organization's structure;
  amended again 2026-09-29, approved by the owner: Settings has two tabs, and
  the base year moves in as **Baseline and targets**, open to every member
- **Protocol**: Corporate Standard chapter 7 (inventory quality management:
  defined roles, and a named person accountable for them), ISO 14064-1
  section 8 (records of who did what)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-16
- **Modules**: `src/features/ghg` (the settings layout and page, the
  organization layout, the overview, the organization list). No backend change until the
  2026-09-29 amendment, which adds the structural history to `ghg`
  (`GhgService`, `StructureChanges`, `GhgAuditEvent`).

## Problem

A platform administrator has a panel: a sidebar at `/admin` with a dashboard,
the registers they act on, and the deployment's own policy at the end of it
(spec 01.5). The owner of an organization, who administers a client's whole
inventory programme, has nothing equivalent. The functions exist, but they are
scattered, and two of them are where nobody looks:

- **Members** was a card at the foot of the organization overview, below the
  setup checklist and the headline inventory, with no entry in the sidebar and
  no page of its own. An owner who wanted to add a preparer had to know to
  scroll. Spec 01.2 said "members are managed from the organization overview",
  which was true and undiscoverable.
- **Name, address and contact** were editable only from an **Edit** button on
  the organization card grid at `/app/ghg`, a list the owner passes through
  once and then leaves.
- **Delete organization** was on that same card grid, beside Open.
- **The organization's history**, which spec 01.3 promises records every
  support grant assumed, ended and expired, had no page at all. It was a tail
  list inside `SupportAccessCard`, and that card returns nothing when there is
  no grant and no event, so the history could not be read except by a reader
  who already had one.

## Behavior

- **One place.** An organization has a **Settings** page at
  `/app/ghg/{organizationId}/settings`, last in the sidebar and set apart by a
  divider, carrying the gear the platform panel uses. It has two tabs, each at
  its own address:
  - **Organization** (`.../settings`) holds four cards: **Details** (name,
    address, contact), **Members**, **History**, and a **Danger zone** holding
    the deletion.
  - **Baseline and targets** (`.../settings/baseline`, amended 2026-09-29)
    holds the base year and its recalculation policy (spec 06), which had its
    own sidebar entry until then. The base year belongs to the organization,
    not to an inventory, so it sits with the organization's other standing
    decisions. Reduction targets will join it. The old address
    `.../base-year` redirects here.
- **Organization is the owner's; Baseline and targets is everyone's.** The
  sidebar entry appears for every member, because every member uses the base
  year. The **Organization** tab appears only for an owner **by membership**:
  a preparer, a reviewer and a verifier do not see it, and neither does a
  platform administrator under support access, because support access never
  grants membership changes or deletion (spec 01.3). Any of them who opens
  `.../settings` is taken to **Baseline and targets**, and the server refuses
  the owner's writes regardless (spec 01.2). On **Baseline and targets** the
  base-year page decides who may change what exactly as before: preparers,
  reviewers and owners write, a verifier reads.
- **Details** saves through the same endpoint the Edit dialog used. The dialog
  itself stays for creating an organization, where an administrator also names
  the account that becomes the owner (spec 01.5).
- **History** lists the organization's own events, newest first: who, when,
  what and the reason. This is the record spec 01.3 requires a client to be
  able to read for themselves. Membership changes are among them: a member
  added, a member's role changed and a member removed each appear under the
  owner who made the change, naming the member and the role.
- **The structure is in the history too** (amended 2026-09-29). "Someone
  changed a subsidiary's ownership share: who, and when?" has an answer. Each
  of these acts writes exactly one row, under the person who made it, with the
  time:

  | Act | Action | Label | Reason |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Legal entity added | `ENTITY_ADDED` | Legal entity added | `Gye Nyame Camp Services Ltd added: subsidiary, economic interest 100%, legal ownership 100%` (legal ownership and "held through" only when set) |
  | Legal entity edited | `ENTITY_UPDATED` | Legal entity edited | `Gye Nyame Camp Services Ltd: economic interest 100% → 60%, legal ownership 100% → 60%` |
  | Legal entity removed | `ENTITY_REMOVED` | Legal entity removed | `Tarkwa JV removed: <the reason the user typed>` |
  | Facility added | `FACILITY_ADDED` | Facility added | `Nkran camp added under Gye Nyame Camp Services Ltd, location Nkran` |
  | Facility edited | `FACILITY_UPDATED` | Facility edited | `Nkran camp: location Nkran → Nkran, Ashanti` |
  | Facility removed | `FACILITY_REMOVED` | Facility removed | `Nkran camp removed: <the reason the user typed>` |
  | Source stream added | `STREAM_ADDED` | Source stream added | `Camp generator diesel added at Nkran camp: stationary combustion` |
  | Source stream removed | `STREAM_REMOVED` | Source stream removed | `Camp generator diesel removed from Nkran camp` |

  The decisions behind the rows:

  - **An edit names what changed, as "field old → new"**, in form order,
    separated by commas, after the record's name as it stands after the edit.
    For a legal entity the fields are name, relationship, economic interest,
    legal ownership, operated, financially controlled (franchises only, spec
    03.3), held through, acquired on, disposed of on, jurisdiction, financial
    control (`follows Table 1`, `consolidated by decision`, `not controlled by
    decision`) and basis of the decision. For a facility they are name, legal
    entity, location, country, grid region, facility type, lease (`owned, not
    leased` when none), lease from and lease until. An empty value reads
    `none`, a percentage drops trailing zeros (`60%`), a date is ISO
    (`2025-07-01`).
  - **A free-text note is named, not quoted**: "basis of the decision
    changed". The note itself is on the entity; the reason column holds 500
    characters, and a reason longer than that is cut with an ellipsis.
  - **An edit that changes nothing writes no row.** The comparison is between
    the record before and after the save, so saving the form untouched, or
    retyping `60` as `60.00`, is not an act.
  - **A removal carries the reason the user typed**, after "removed:".
  - **The acts creation performs are not repeated**: the reporting company
    that creating an organization makes, and its name following a rename, are
    covered by the organization's own rows.
  - **Editing a source stream is not recorded**; adding and removing one is.
    A stream's name, kind and meter are register housekeeping, and a stream
    with records cannot be removed (spec 04.3).
  - **Append-only as before.** Rows are never edited or deleted; a later
    change is a later row.
  - **Support access is marked.** An act by a platform administrator under
    support access is recorded under the administrator, with
    "(under support access)" appended to the reason, as every such act is
    (spec 01.3).
- **What leaves the overview.** The members card goes to Settings. The support
  access card stays: spec 01.6 requires the page a member lands on to state a
  live grant, and it still does. Only the history tail moves, so the card now
  states the grant and nothing else.
- **What leaves the organization list.** **Edit** and **Delete** go from the
  card grid. The list opens organizations; it no longer administers them.

Given Ama Mensah, owner of Sankofa Gold plc: her sidebar carries **Settings**
below Units; she opens it on the **Organization** tab, renames the
organization, adds Abena Owusu by email as a `PREPARER`, reads that a platform
administrator assumed support access on Tuesday and why, and finds the
deletion behind a danger zone that still makes her type the name. Abena,
signed in as the preparer, also has a Settings entry; it opens on **Baseline
and targets**, the only tab she sees, where she can designate the base year.

## API

Unchanged, including by the second 2026-09-29 amendment: the base-year
endpoints keep their gates (spec 06). Every endpoint already exists and is already gated:
`PUT /ghg/organizations/{id}` and `DELETE /ghg/organizations/{id}`,
`GET /ghg/organizations/{id}/events`, and the four member endpoints under
`/ghg/organizations/{id}/members`. `GhgAccess.checkMemberOwner` requires an
owner by membership and refuses an administrator under support access.

## Data

None originally. The 2026-09-29 amendment adds
`V60__structure_changes_in_history.sql`, which re-creates the action check on
`ghg_audit_events` with the eight structural actions.

## Events

None new. The page reads the organization events of spec 01.3, which since
the 2026-09-29 amendment include `ENTITY_ADDED`, `ENTITY_UPDATED`,
`ENTITY_REMOVED`, `FACILITY_ADDED`, `FACILITY_UPDATED`, `FACILITY_REMOVED`,
`STREAM_ADDED` and `STREAM_REMOVED`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- **A preparer can no longer see who is on the team.** The roster moved into an
  owner-only page, where before every member could read it on the overview
  without controls. If that turns out to matter, the remedy is a read-only
  roster card on the overview, with the management staying here.
- An organization dashboard. Settings administers; the overview is still where
  the work is (spec 01.6).
- Moving units or densities here. They are organization level and would fit,
  but each has a page of its own already. The base-year policy did move, by
  the second 2026-09-29 amendment.
- `MembersCard` hides its controls outright rather than disabling them with the
  required role, which is the one place that departs from the visible-refusal
  rule of spec 01.4. Owner-only routing makes it moot for now.

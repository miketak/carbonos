# 04.2: Activity periods, cut-off and pro-rating

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 5 (structural changes accounted from the date they
  occur), Chapter 7 (data attributable to the reporting period), Chapter 9
  (the reporting period)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F9 and F24, ticket T-07);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`ActivityRecord`, review, the COMPLETENESS and
  BOUNDARY gates, the run, coverage), `src/features/ghg` (activity form and
  list, activity view, inventory form)

## Problem

An activity record carried one date. An annual total dated 31 December was
included in full even when its entity joined the boundary on 1 July; a
record dated 15 November 2024 silently fell out of the FY2025 view; meter
reads straddling year-end, quarterly invoices and mid-year acquisitions
could not be represented. An 18-month inventory was created without a word,
and periods that do not follow the calendar year were labelled by their
first year only. Verifiers test period cut-off first.

## Behavior

- **A record covers a period.** Every activity record has a **period start**
  and a **period end** (the end may equal the start for a single reading;
  neither may be in the future, and the end may not precede the start). A
  record's period is the period the quantity was consumed or emitted over,
  not the invoice date.
- **Cut-off.** Review compares the record's period with the inventory's
  period and with the facility's membership window (spec 03.2):
  - no overlap with the inventory period: auto-excluded `OUTSIDE_PERIOD`;
  - no overlap with the membership window, or a facility outside the
    boundary: auto-excluded `OUTSIDE_BOUNDARY`, as before;
  - partial overlap with either: included, and **straddling**.
- **Straddling records** are handled per an inventory setting,
  `straddleTreatment`: `PRO_RATE` (the default) or `BLOCK`. Under
  `PRO_RATE` the run multiplies the line by the **period share**: the days
  of the record's period that fall inside both the inventory period and the
  membership window, over the days of the record's period (a 365-day record
  of which 184 days fall inside counts 50.41%). The line records both day
  counts and the share, every gas column carries the share as `kgCo2e`
  does (spec 07.7), and its note says "pro-rated: 184 of 365 days inside
  the reporting period and the membership window". The COMPLETENESS gate
  warns for every straddling record so the pro-rating is a visible,
  deliberate statement. Under `BLOCK` the gate errors instead, naming the
  record, and the accountant splits the record or excludes it.
- **The period length.** The BOUNDARY gate warns when the inventory period
  is not twelve months, and the inventory form says so before the inventory
  is created. Chapter 9 expects an annual inventory; a longer or shorter one
  is allowed but must be deliberate.
- **Period labels.** An inventory carries a `periodLabel`: the calendar
  year when the period lies within one, otherwise a fiscal-year label such
  as "FY2025/26". The base-year page, the emissions profile and the report
  use the label rather than the first year alone.
- **Coverage.** The activity view shows, per facility and activity type,
  which months of the inventory period have data from included records and
  which have none, so a missing quarter is visible before the run.

Given Tarkwa acquired on 1 July 2025 with one 2025 annual diesel record of
100,000 litres covering 1 January to 31 December: the record straddles the
membership window, the gate warns, and the run counts 184 of 365 days,
50,410.959 litres, at the full share with the note on the line. Under
`BLOCK` the same record blocks the run until it is split.

## API

- `CreateActivityRequest` takes `periodStart` and `periodEnd` in place of
  `activityDate` (422 when the end precedes the start or either is in the
  future); `ActivityResponse`, `AssignmentResponse` and
  `RunExclusionResponse` carry both.
- `InventoryRequest` gains `straddleTreatment?` (`PRO_RATE` by default);
  `InventoryResponse` carries it and `periodLabel`.
- `RunLineResponse` gains `periodStart`, `periodEnd`, `periodDays`,
  `coveredDays`, `periodShare` and `periodNote`; `kgCo2e` and the gases
  include the period share. Spec 07.7 records that the first implementation
  applied the share to `kgCo2e` only and names the regression test.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/coverage` → `{facilityId, facilityName,
  activityType, months[], coveredMonths[]}[]` over the inventory's months.
- `ReportResponse.baseYear.profile[]` entries and `BaseYearResponse` carry
  `periodLabel`.

## Data

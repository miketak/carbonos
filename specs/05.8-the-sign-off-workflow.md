# 05.8: The sign-off workflow

- **Status**: Approved (2026-10-06)
- **Protocol**: Chapter 7 (inventory quality management: defined roles, a
  review by someone other than the preparer, records of who decided what),
  ISO 14064-1:2018 section 8.1 (information management procedures,
  including review) and 8.3 (records that let the inventory be
  reconstructed), ISO 14064-3 (a control a verifier can sample: attributable,
  dated, and tied to the data it covers)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-10-06 (ECO-13, carrying ticket T-22 of the 2026-09-08
  audit; GHG inventory officer review of the plan and owner decisions,
  2026-10-06)
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`InventoryStatus`, `Inventory`, `InventoryService`

  the Runs tab, the sign-off card, the report header)

## Problem

Chapter 7 asks for a quality management system with defined roles and a
review step, and ISO 14064-1 section 8.1 for a review by someone other than
the preparer. The product records who launched each run, who designated the
final run and who published, and the organization roles stop a Preparer
from designating or publishing (specs 01.2, 05.5). But one Reviewer or Owner
can launch the run, designate it final and publish it alone; nothing records
that anyone but its author looked at the figure; and the report's "Approved
by" is free text typed before publication, defaulting to whoever published
(spec 07.4). A verifier sampling the control finds a name, not an evidenced
act. Specs 01, 01.2, 01.4, 05.1, 05.5 and 07.4 each deferred this.

## Behavior

### The review state

The lifecycle gains **In review** between Frozen and Final: Draft, Frozen,
In review, Final, Published.

- **Submit for review.** On a run of a frozen inventory, a member with a
  write role (Preparer, Reviewer, Owner) submits the run for review, with an
  optional note for the approver of up to 500 characters. The inventory
  moves to In review and records the run, who submitted it, when, and the
  note. Submitting another run of the same inventory while it is in review
  replaces the submission and records a new one. Submission checks what the
  sign-off checks (spec 05.7's holds, the base-year hold of spec 06.1, a
  voided run), so the preparer learns of a hold before the approver does.
- **Return to the preparer.** A member with an approve role (Reviewer,
  Owner) returns the inventory with a reason of 5 to 500 characters. It goes
  back to Frozen and the submission is cleared.
- **Mark as final is the sign-off.** It acts only on the run submitted for
  review, on an inventory in review: "Run 4 has not been submitted for
  review. Submit it before marking it final." It stays an approve-role act
  with its optional review note (spec 05.5), and it is refused to the person
  who submitted the run while another member who may approve exists: "Run 5
  was submitted by Kofi Mensah, who cannot also sign it off. Ask Ama Owusu or
  another reviewer or owner to mark it final." The direct Frozen to Final act
  is gone.
- **A self-approval is disclosed, not hidden.** Where nobody else in the
  organization may approve (a sole owner, or an owner whose other members are
  preparers and verifiers), the submitter's sign-off goes through, is
  recorded as a self-approval, and the report prints "self-approved: nobody
  else in the organization could check it" beside the approver, as factor
  approval does (spec 02.11).
- **What clears a submission.** Launching a run on an inventory in review
  withdraws the submission and returns the inventory to Frozen, because the
  approver would otherwise sign something other than what was submitted:
  "submission of run 5 withdrawn: run 6 launched after it". Voiding the
  submitted run does the same ("run 5 was voided"), and so does reopening the
  inventory as a draft, which is allowed from In review as from Frozen.
  Withdrawing the final designation (spec 05.2) clears the sign-off and the
  submission together and returns the inventory to Frozen; the preparer
  resubmits.
- **Runs** may be launched while the inventory is Frozen, In review or Final
  (in review, the launch withdraws the submission). The boundary and the
  records stay read-only in review, as when frozen.
- **Publication stays a separate act** (spec 05.1): a member with an approve
  role publishes a final inventory, and the approver may be that member. The
  history records both acts.

### Assignment narrows; roles stay the ceiling

- A Reviewer or Owner may name an inventory's **preparer** (a member with a
  write role) and **approver** (a member with an approve role), either or
  both, at any time before publication. A role that does not allow the act
  is refused: "Esi Boateng holds the Preparer role and may not sign off an
  inventory; roles are the ceiling." The two may not be the same person:
  "Kofi Mensah cannot be both the preparer and the approver of one
  inventory: name someone else for one of the two."
- When a preparer is named, only that person submits: "Esi Boateng is this
  inventory's preparer; only they submit it for review." When an approver is
  named, only that person returns or signs it ("Kofi Mensah is this
  inventory's approver; only they return it or sign it off"), and that person
  may not submit it. When nobody is named, the role ceiling applies and the
  acts record whoever acted.
- An assignment is recorded in the history ("preparer: Esi Boateng;
  approver: Kofi Mensah") and can be cleared. A member whose role later drops
  below the act is refused at the act, so the ceiling holds even when an
  assignment is stale.

### The record

- **Identities are accounts.** Every act records the member's user id,
  email and display name as they were at the act. The report prints names
  with the email, never typed text.
- **History.** The inventory's history gains "Submitted for review" ("run 5
  submitted for review: note"), "Returned to preparer" (the reason),
  "Submission withdrawn" (why) and "Sign-off assigned". "Final run
  designated" is the sign-off and reads "run 5 signed off and designated
  final: note", with "(self-approved: nobody else in the organization could
  check it)" when it applies. Each entry carries the actor and the time;
  under support access the marker of spec 01.3 is added.
- **Report header** (spec 07.4, amended):
  - **Prepared by**: the person who submitted the run for review, with the
    run, the date and the note: "Esi Boateng (esi@gyenyame.example), run 5,
    2 Mar 2026: reconciled against the fuel ledger". On a run that was never
    submitted, the person who launched it, as before.
  - **Approved by**: the person who signed it off, with the run and the
    date: "Kofi Mensah (kofi@gyenyame.example), run 5, 4 Mar 2026", followed
    by the self-approval sentence when it applies. Before the sign-off it
    reads "not yet approved".
  - The typed "Approved by" override on the report header is retired. An
    inventory published before this spec keeps the text it was published
    with, which its stored report already holds, and its live report falls
    back to the text.
- **A correction** (spec 05.3) is a new inventory: it starts with no
  submission and no sign-off, and the assignment is not copied.
- **Support access** (spec 01.3): an administrator under a support grant may
  submit and sign, as marking final and publishing stay available today, and
  never signs their own submission. Each act carries the support-access
  marker.

Given Gye Nyame Gold with Ama Owusu (Owner), Kofi Mensah (Reviewer), Esi
Boateng (Preparer) and Yaw Darko (Verifier), and FY2025 frozen with run 5:

- When Esi submits run 5 with the note "reconciled against the fuel ledger",
  the inventory reads In review and the history "Submitted for review" by
  Esi. Esi sees **Mark as final** disabled with "Needs the Reviewer or Owner
  role."
- When Kofi submits run 5 himself and then marks it final, he is refused:
  "Run 5 was submitted by Kofi Mensah, who cannot also sign it off." When he
  returns the inventory with the reason "Esi prepared this; her submission
  stands", it reads Frozen and the history carries the reason.
- When Esi resubmits and Kofi marks run 5 final, the report header reads
  "Prepared by: Esi Boateng (...), run 5" and "Approved by: Kofi Mensah
  (...), run 5".
- When Ama launches run 6 while the inventory is in review, it returns to
  Frozen with "submission of run 5 withdrawn: run 6 launched after it".

Given Solo Ltd, whose only member Yaw is its Owner: Yaw submits and signs,
and the header prints "self-approved: nobody else in the organization could
check it".

## API

- `POST /api/ghg/runs/{id}/submit-for-review` with `{ "note"?: string }`
  returns the inventory. 409 `ghg.inventory.submit-needs-frozen` for a draft,
  final or published inventory; 409 `ghg.run.voided-not-final`; 409
  `ghg.run.final-holds` and the base-year hold; 403
  `ghg.inventory.not-the-preparer`; 409 `ghg.inventory.approver-is-preparer`
  for the named approver.
- `POST /api/ghg/inventories/{id}/return-to-preparer` with
  `{ "reason": string }` returns the inventory. 422 `ghg.reason-too-short`;
  409 `ghg.inventory.not-in-review`; 403 `ghg.inventory.not-the-approver`.
- `PUT /api/ghg/inventories/{id}/sign-off` with
  `{ "preparerUserId"?: uuid, "approverUserId"?: uuid }`, where null clears,
  returns the inventory. 422 `ghg.inventory.sign-off-role`; 409
  `ghg.inventory.approver-is-preparer`; 404 for someone who is not a member;
  409 `ghg.inventory.sign-off-on-record` on a published inventory.
- `POST /api/ghg/runs/{id}/finalize` and `POST /api/ghg/inventories/{id}/finalize`
  keep their shape and gain 409 `ghg.run.not-submitted`, 409
  `ghg.run.self-signed`, 409 `ghg.inventory.already-final` (another run is
  already signed off; withdraw the designation first) and 403
  `ghg.inventory.not-the-approver`.
- `InventoryResponse` gains `signOff`: `preparer` and `approver` (each
  `{ userId, email, name }` or null), `submittedRunId`, `submittedBy` (same
  shape), `submittedAt`, `submitNote`, and `submitterMaySign` (true when
  nobody else may approve, so a sign-off by the submitter would be a
  disclosed self-approval); and `finalDesignatedByName` and
  `finalSelfApproved`.
- `ReportResponse.header` gains `preparedByEmail`, `preparedRunNo`,
  `preparedNote`, `approvedByEmail`, `approvedAt` and `selfApproved`.
  `ReportMetadataRequest` loses `approvedBy`.
- `AuditEventResponse.action` gains `SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW`,
  `REVIEW_RETURNED`, `SUBMISSION_WITHDRAWN` and `SIGN_OFF_ASSIGNED`.

Rules (`GhgRules`, cited by the QA pack per ADR 0008): `ghg.run.not-submitted`,
`ghg.run.self-signed`, `ghg.inventory.already-final`,
`ghg.inventory.submit-needs-frozen`, `ghg.inventory.not-in-review`,
`ghg.inventory.not-the-preparer`, `ghg.inventory.not-the-approver`,
`ghg.inventory.approver-is-preparer`, `ghg.inventory.sign-off-role` and
`ghg.inventory.sign-off-on-record`.

## Data

`V67__sign_off_workflow.sql`:

- `ghg_inventories.status` accepts `IN_REVIEW` (the check constraint is
  replaced).
- `ghg_inventories` gains the assignment (`preparer_user_id`,
  `preparer_email`, `preparer_name`, `approver_user_id`, `approver_email`,
  `approver_name`), the submission (`submitted_run_id`,
  `submitted_by_user_id`, `submitted_by`, `submitted_by_name`,
  `submitted_at`, `submit_note`) and the sign-off's account
  (`final_designated_by_user_id`, `final_designated_by_name`,
  `final_self_approved`). Plain identifiers with name and email snapshots and
  no foreign key across modules, as the boundary version's reopen and the
  audit actor already do.
- `approved_by` (V24) stays for inventories published before this spec;
  nothing writes it any more.
- `ghg_audit_events.action` accepts the four new actions (the check
  constraint is replaced).

No backfill: an inventory designated final before this spec has no
submission, and its header falls back as described.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A job title on the member profile, printed after the name; the display
  name stands until a client asks for a title.
- Notifying the named approver by email when a run is submitted (the
  notifications ticket, ECO-15).
- The sign-off trail in the evidence pack a verifier takes away (the report
  ticket, ECO-35).
- An approval checklist shown at the sign-off (spec 05.7's holds and the
  data-quality summary); the holds already refuse.
- Refusing the sign-off under support access: it stays available, as
  marking final does today (spec 01.3), until a client objects.

# 07.7: Emissions by gas that tie to the total

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-12)
- **Protocol**: Chapter 9 (required information: emissions data for each of
  the seven Kyoto gas groups separately, in metric tonnes and in tonnes
  CO2e), Chapter 5 and Chapter 7 (data attributable to the reporting period
  and pro-rated across structural changes), Chapter 1 (accuracy and
  transparency), ISO 14064-3 (a verifier re-performs the by-gas table from
  the calculation file)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-12 (audit 2026-09-11, findings F44 and F45); implemented
  2026-09-12
- **Modules**: `ghg` (the run calculation in `InventoryService`,
  `GhgRunLine`, `GhgRun`, `ReportResponse`, `ReportPdf`, `RunCsv`, the
  EMISSION_FACTOR gate), `src/features/ghg` (report page section 05)

## Problem

Section 05 of the Asante Gold FY2025 report (Run 003) listed CO2 49,439.149
t, CH4 4.679 t CO2e, N2O 514.040 t CO2e and HFCs 163.498 t CO2e, which add
to 50,121.4 t CO2e, against an inventory total of 80,850.9 t. Two defects
make up the 30,729.5 t gap.

First, every line whose factor publishes CO2e only (the Ember grid rows,
landfill, water, travel, and commuting) contributes to the scope totals and
to nothing in the by-gas table. The table omits them without a word; the
pre-flight warning says "the report says so", and the report does not.

Second, a pro-rated line carries its full-period gas masses. ACT-0029 (160,000
litres of diesel, 15 December 2025 to 14 January 2026, 17 of 31 days inside
the period, 54.84%) has `kg_co2e` 233,532.578 (pro-rated, correct) beside
`co2_kg` 420,508.8 (160,000 x 2.62818, the whole record), `ch4_kg` 1.6 and
`n2o_kg` 20 (also whole). Spec 04.2 states that the gases include the period
share; the calculation multiplies the gas columns by the accounting share
only. The by-gas table therefore includes about 190 t of CO2 the scope totals
do not, and a mid-year acquisition of a whole mine scales the error to
thousands of tonnes.

A by-gas table that does not foot to the total is the first thing a verifier
fails under ISO 14064-3, and 38% of this inventory was missing from it.

## Behavior

- **Every gas column carries both shares.** On a run line, CO2, CH4, N2O, HFC
  (mass and CO2e), PFC (mass and CO2e), SF6, NF3, and biogenic CO2 are each
  the converted quantity, multiplied by the factor's per-unit component,
  multiplied by the accounting share, multiplied by the period share. The same
  two shares that produce `kgCo2e` produce every gas. For a line with a gas
  split, the sum of the gas CO2e contributions under the run's GWP set equals
  `kgCo2e` (the gas CO2e contributions are computed from the unrounded
  masses and tie to `kgCo2e` within 0.001 kg; the masses are then stored to
  three decimals of a kilogram for display).
- **Lines without a gas split.** A line whose factor publishes CO2e only (no
  CO2, CH4, N2O, HFC, PFC, SF6, or NF3 component recorded) is an **unsplit
  line**. Its `kgCo2e` is counted in the scope totals as before, and every gas
  column is zero. The report and the exports identify these lines so a reader
  can reproduce the reconciling row.
- **The reconciling row.** The by-gas table lists the seven gas groups as
  before and then one more row, **CO2e from factors without a gas split**,
  whose CO2e is the sum of `kgCo2e` over the unsplit lines and whose mass
  column reads "not separable". Under the table the report names the factors
  the row comes from, in the order of their contribution, and states the rule
  in words: "Factors that publish CO2e only are listed on one row. Their CO2,
  CH4 and N2O are not separable; the source did not publish them." When no
  line is unsplit, the row and the sentence are omitted.
- **The invariant.** The sum of the CO2e column over the seven gas rows and
  the reconciling row equals scope 1 plus scope 2 (location-based) plus scope
  3, which is the report's total. The report computes both sides from the
  run's lines and prints the total under the table as **Total (scope 2
  location-based), ties to section 04**, followed by the sentence "The
  market-based scope 2 figure is not split by gas; its instruments and
  balance are in section 04." The two figures agree to the rounding of the table (three decimals of
  a tonne). Biogenic CO2 stays outside the table and the total (spec 07.2),
  and so do lines whose factor has the reporting basis
  `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO` (spec 02.4). Derived category 3 lines (spec 04.7)
  are lines like any other: one with a gas split feeds the gas rows, and one
  whose upstream factor publishes CO2e only is an unsplit line on the
  reconciling row.
- **The PDF and the CSV.** The PDF gas table carries the same rows, the same
  footing line, and the same sentence (spec 07.5). The lines CSV gains a
  column `co2e_unsplit_kg`: the line's `kg_co2e` when the line is unsplit,
  else 0, so the sum of that column reproduces the reconciling row and the sum
  of the other gas columns, each multiplied by its potential, reproduces the
  gas rows.
- **The gate says what the report does.** The EMISSION_FACTOR warning for a
  CO2e-only factor reads: "'<factor>' publishes CO2e only. Its emissions are
  counted in the scope totals and appear in the by-gas table on the row 'CO2e
  from factors without a gas split', not under CO2, CH4 or N2O."

Given Run 003 of Asante Gold Resources FY2025 (total 80,850.9 t CO2e):
section 05 lists CO2, CH4, N2O and HFCs, then "CO2e from factors without a
gas split: 30,921.9 t (Grid electricity, Ghana (2024); Waste to landfill;
Water supply; Flights, long haul; Commuting by bus)", and the footing line
reads "Total 80,850.9 t CO2e, ties to section 04". The CO2 row reads
49,249.2 t, not 49,439.1 t, because ACT-0029 now contributes 54.84% of its
gas masses. (The figures are illustrative to the tonne; the invariant is
exact to the rounding.) The CH4 and N2O rows fall to 4.659 t and 511.646 t
CO2e for the same reason.

Given ACT-0029 in `lines.csv`: `period_share` 0.5484, `kg_co2e` 233,532.578,
`co2_kg` 230,601.6, `ch4_kg` 0.877, `n2o_kg` 10.968, `co2e_unsplit_kg` 0.
Given the grid electricity line: `co2_kg` 0, `co2e_unsplit_kg` equal to its
`kg_co2e`.

## API

- `ReportResponse.byGas[]` gains one entry with `gas` =
  `"CO2E_UNSPLIT"`, `kg` and `tonnes` null, `kgCo2e` and `tCo2e` set, and a
  new field `factors[]` (the names of the factors the row comes from) that
  is empty on the seven gas rows. The entry is present only when at least
  one line is unsplit.
- `ReportResponse` gains `byGasTotalKgCo2e` and `byGasTotalTCo2e` (the
  footing figure) beside `emissions.totalKgCo2e`, so a client can show the
  tie without re-adding.
- `RunLineResponse.byGas` and `RunResponse.byGas` keep their fields; their
  values now include the period share. Both gain `co2eUnsplitKg` (they share
  one record): on the run it is the reconciling row's figure, on a line it is
  the line's own `kgCo2e` when the line is unsplit, else 0.
- `GET /runs/{id}/lines.csv` gains the column `co2e_unsplit_kg` after
  `biogenic_co2_kg`.
- The pre-flight `Finding` text for CO2e-only factors changes as in
  Behavior. No endpoint changes.

## Data

None. Whether a line is unsplit derives from its stored gas columns and
`kg_co2e`; the reconciling row and the footing figure are computed from the
run's lines when the report is assembled. The run listing, which does not
load lines, reads `co2eUnsplitKg` through a Hibernate `@Formula` (a subselect
over the run's lines on the same rule), so nothing is stored and no column
is added. Runs already stored keep their lines as calculated: their gas
masses stay wrong where they were pro-rated, and their PDF and CSV say so
through the report's `calculatedAt` date and
the release notes. A run launched after this change is right. Recalculating
a stored run is not done: runs are immutable snapshots (spec 05.1).

## Verification

- `GhgApiIntegrationTests.everyGasColumnCarriesThePeriodAndAccountingShares`:
  a record straddling year-end at 50% period share and a 40% accounting
  share yields `co2_kg`, `ch4_kg`, `n2o_kg`, `hfcs_kg`, `sf6_kg` and
  `biogenic_co2_kg` equal to quantity x component x 0.4 x 0.5, and the gas
  CO2e contributions sum to `kg_co2e` within 0.001 kg when computed from the
  unrounded masses.
- `GhgApiIntegrationTests.theByGasTableFootsToTheTotal`: a run with a diesel
  line, a refrigerant line and a CO2e-only grid line; `byGas` has eight
  entries, the `CO2E_UNSPLIT` entry names the grid factor, the sum of
  `kgCo2e` over `byGas` equals `emissions.totalKgCo2e` exactly and the sum
  of `tCo2e` agrees with `emissions.totalTCo2e` to the rounding of the table
  (each row is rounded to three decimals of a tonne before it is summed),
  `byGasTotalTCo2e` equals `emissions.totalTCo2e`, the CSV's
  `co2e_unsplit_kg` sums to the row, and the PDF text contains "CO2e from
  factors without a gas split" and "ties to section 04". A run with no
  CO2e-only line has seven entries and no sentence.
- The existing gate test for CO2e-only factors asserts the new wording.
- Frontend `RunDetailPage.test.tsx`: section 05 renders the reconciling
  row with its factor names, the footing line, and omits both when the
  report has no unsplit entry.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Attributing CO2e-only factors to CO2 with a footnote, which some
  reporters do for grid factors. The reconciling row keeps the CO2 column
  honest; a later spec can add the attribution as an option if a client's
  disclosure framework demands it.
- Recalculating stored runs. Their by-gas figures stay as calculated and
  the report's calculation date says which rule applied.
- Splitting a CO2e-only grid factor into gases from a national inventory's
  fuel mix. That is a factor-library matter (spec 02.1) and needs a source.

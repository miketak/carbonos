# 03.4: Entity dates and control, facility attributes, and boundary pre-population

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 3 (financial control is the ability to direct
  policies, not a percentage; every controlled operation is in the boundary
  under a control approach), Chapter 5 (structural changes must be dated),
  Scope 2 Guidance section 6.3 (the location-based factor of the grid where
  the facility sits), Appendix F (leased assets)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F5, F7, F21, F47; tickets T-19,
  T-20, T-21); implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`LegalEntity`, `Table1`, `BoundaryTreatment`,
  `Facility`, `EmissionFactor.gridRegion`, the boundary pre-population, the
  classification defaults, `BaseYearService`), `src/features/ghg` (entity
  and facility forms and pages, boundary section, inventory form, activity

## Problem

An entity had no acquisition or disposal date, no country, and no way to
say that a minority holding is consolidated under IFRS 10. The acquisition
date could only be typed as a membership window, per inventory, after the
entity was ticked in, so structural-change detection rested on manual
re-entry and the affected share of a manual recalculation candidate was
typed rather than computed. A facility was a name, a location and an
entity: nothing drove the grid factor (a UK grid factor was offered for a
mine in Ghana) and a lease had to be declared per record per inventory.
Every entity and facility started unticked, so an officer ticked seven of
each, and an associate at 0% could still be ticked.

## Behavior

- **Entity dates, jurisdiction, control decision.** A legal entity carries
  **effective from** and **effective to** dates (acquisition, disposal), a
  **jurisdiction** (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), and a **financial-control
  override** with a note: consolidated under financial control whatever
  the holding, or not controlled whatever the holding. The override gives
  the entity 100% or 0% under the financial-control approach; the other
  approaches follow Table 1 as before. The Table 1 row printed on the
  boundary and in the version says so ("100% (consolidated under financial
  control by decision, whatever the holding)"). A treatment created for the
  entity copies the override and defaults its **membership window** to the
  entity's dates, so a mid-year acquisition is accounted from its date in
  every inventory without re-entry, and the freeze detects the structural
  change from the window as spec 06 describes. Versions snapshot the
  override.
- **Affected share from the runs.** A manual recalculation candidate
  (methodology change, error correction) names either the affected share
  or a **comparison run** of the base-year inventory that applies the new
  method; the affected share is then the difference between that run's
  total and the base-year run's total, as a percentage of the base. Both
  can be given; the typed share wins and the run is kept as the reference.
- **Facility attributes.** A facility carries a **grid region** (an ISO
  3166-1 alpha-3 code such as `GHA`, or `US-<eGRID subregion>` such as
  `US-CAMX`), a **facility type**, and a **lease** (type, from, to). Grid
  factors carry the region they serve: the seeded Ghana and UK grids, the
  Ember national grids and the eGRID subregions of the packs. For a record
  at a facility with a region, or with a country that resolves to one, the
  activity view **suggests** the newest approved grid factor of that
  region and the picker preselects it. A record whose period overlaps the
  facility's lease **inherits the lease type**: the classification applies
  Appendix F under the inventory's approach unless the accountant chooses
  a lease type or none by hand; the view says which lease was inherited.
- **Boundary pre-population.** A new inventory starts with every entity
  whose share under the approach is above zero, and every facility of it,
  in the boundary (the form's default; the API flag `prefillBoundary`
  requests it). An entity at 0% under the approach is shown as **outside
  the boundary under this approach** and cannot be ticked. Unticking an
  operation is the exclusion flow of spec 07.2: the reason control opens,
  and the BOUNDARY gate blocks the run until a reason is recorded.

Given Takoradi Port Co, a 30% associate consolidated under financial
control by decision: under the financial-control approach its share is
100% and the row says "by decision"; under equity share it stays 30%.
Given Nkran Mine with grid region `GHA` and a metered electricity record:
the view suggests "Grid electricity, Ghana (2024)" from the Ember pack
when it is imported, else the seeded Ghana grid. Given a warehouse leased
in under an operating lease from 1 July: a record for August inherits the
operating lease and, under equity share, lands in scope 3 upstream leased
assets.

## API

- `EntityRequest` and `EntityResponse` gain `effectiveFrom`, `effectiveTo`,
  `jurisdiction`, `financialControlOverride` and `controlNote`; 422 when
  the disposal precedes the acquisition.
- `BoundaryEntityResponse` gains `financialControlOverride` and
  `shareUnderApproach`; `BoundaryTreatmentRequest` gains
  `financialControlOverride`; `BoundaryVersionEntryResponse` gains
  `financialControlOverride`.
- `FacilityRequest` and `FacilityResponse` gain `gridRegion`, `facilityType`,
  `leaseType`, `leaseFrom` and `leaseTo`; the response also carries
  `effectiveGridRegion`. `EmissionFactorResponse` gains `gridRegion`.
- `AssignmentResponse` gains `inheritedLeaseType`, `suggestedFactorId` and
  `suggestedFactorName`. `ClassifyRequest.leaseType` absent applies the
  inherited lease; `"NONE"` is not a value: the accountant who wants no
  lease on a leased facility's record passes `leaseType: null` with
  `ignoreFacilityLease: true`.
- `InventoryRequest` gains `prefillBoundary?` (create only).
- `RaiseRecalculationRequest.affectedPercent` becomes optional and
  `comparisonRunId?` is added; 422 when neither is given; 409 when the run
  is not a run of the base-year inventory. `RecalculationResponse` gains
  `comparisonRunId`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A country picker with names; the code is typed. Regions other than
  national grids and eGRID subregions (for example state grids in India or
  Australia) are typed as free text and matched exactly.
- Lease flags at the record or stream level; the facility's lease covers
  its records, and the classification can override it.
- Re-populating the boundary of an existing draft when the approach
  changes; the accountant adds or removes operations by hand.

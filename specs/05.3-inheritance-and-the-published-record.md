# 05.3: Inheritance between inventories and the published record

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 1 (consistency), Chapter 5 (a restatement states its
  reason and shows the change), Chapter 9 and Chapter 10 (a published report
  is a point-in-time document a verifier's opinion attaches to)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F29, F41, F49; tickets T-12, T-10);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (inventory creation and correction, assignments, the
  published report snapshot, the report composite), `src/features/ghg`
  (inventory form, lifecycle bar, inventory page, activity view, report page)

## Problem

A second inventory over the same period, and a correction of a published
one, both came back with every record unclassified: each factor, scope,
category, lease flag and exclusion had to be typed again, and each re-entry
was a chance for the correction to diverge from what was published. After
publication the published run's report changed underneath the reader: its
base-year section showed a candidate raised later and an emissions profile
listing inventories created afterwards, and the published inventory's page
showed facts corrected at organization level with no marker. A correction
asked only for a name.

## Behavior

- **Copy the view.** A new inventory can copy its view from any inventory
  of the organization: the boundary with its windows and overrides, the
  boundary exclusions, the contractual instruments and the residual mix,
  the upstream rules (spec 04.7), the declaration, the report header and
  intensity denominators, and every
  assignment of a record still on file (its inclusion or exclusion with the
  reason, justification and magnitude, its factor, scope, category, lease
  type, justifications, proxy flag and density). Copied assignments are
  marked **inherited**. The inventory page says what it inherited and how
  many records of its period the source never decided on; the next review
  adds those as usual. When the two inventories differ in consolidation
  approach, the boundary is rebuilt from Table 1 rather than copied,
  computed exclusions are dropped, and leased assignments are re-derived
  (spec 05.4).
- **Corrections inherit and need a reason.** Creating a correction requires
  a **reason** of at least 10 characters (what was wrong in the published
  inventory). The correction inherits the whole view as above, records the
  reason, and the published inventory's history says why the correction
  was created. The correction's report prints a **correction section**:
  what it restates, the reason, and how its lines differ from the published
  run (lines added, removed, changed, and the change in kg CO2e).
- **The published report is frozen.** At publication the report of the
  final run is snapshotted in full. Reading that run's report afterwards
  returns the snapshot, so the base-year section, the emissions profile and
  every other block read as they were published. Whatever happened later
  appears in a separate **since publication** block: the inventory's audit
  events after publication, inventories created for later periods, and
  records whose facts changed or were removed since the published run, each
  with the field, the published value and the current value.
- **The published inventory's view.** The activity view of a published
  inventory shows each record's facts as the published run snapshotted them
  and marks the records that have changed since, naming the fields. The
  page carries an "as published" notice with the count of changed records.

Given the 2025 inventory published and the LPG record corrected from 500 to
23,530 litres afterwards: the published report still reads 500 litres, its
since-publication block lists "Camp LPG: quantity 500 → 23530", and the
inventory page marks the record as changed since publication. Given a
correction created "LPG was recorded in kg as litres": the correction's
assignments start as published, the LPG line is re-examined, and its report
says "Correction of 2025 Corporate: 1 line changed, +4.2 t CO2e".

## API

- `InventoryRequest` gains `copyFromInventoryId?` (create only; 404 for
  another organization's inventory). `InventoryResponse` gains
  `copiedFromId` and `correctionReason`.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/inheritance` → `{sourceInventoryId, sourceName,
  inherited, undecided, correctionReason}`, or 204 when nothing was copied.
- `POST /inventories/{id}/supersede` `{name?, reason}`: 422 `errors.reason`
  when the reason is missing or shorter than 10 characters.
- `AssignmentResponse` gains `inherited` and `changedSincePublication`
  (null unless the inventory is published; else the changed fields, empty
  when none).
- `ReportResponse` gains `sincePublication` (null unless the run is the
  final run of a published inventory) and `correction` (null unless the
  inventory is a correction). The published snapshot is what
  `GET /runs/{id}/report`, the PDF and `inputs.json` render for that run.

## Data

`V32__inventory_inheritance_and_published_record.sql`: `copied_from_id`,
`correction_reason` and `published_report` (the report as JSON) on
`ghg_inventories`; `inherited` on `ghg_assignments`.

## Events

None new; `InventoryPublished` is published after the snapshot is stored.

`InventoriesPage.test.tsx` (the copy-from choice),
`InventoryDetailPage.test.tsx` (the correction reason, the as-published
notice), `RunDetailPage.test.tsx` (the since-publication and correction
sections).

## Non-goals and open questions

- Copying the view from another organization's inventory.
- Re-syncing inherited decisions when the source changes later; the copy
  is taken once, at creation.
- A diff of the published PDF against a later PDF; the since-publication
  block and the correction section carry the change in data.

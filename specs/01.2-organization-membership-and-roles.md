# 01.2: Organization membership, roles and attribution

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Corporate Standard chapter 7 (inventory quality management:
  defined roles and a review step), ISO 14064-1 section 8 (records of who
  did what)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F1 and F2, ticket T-22);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `user` (`UserDirectory`, pending accounts, the password
  policy), `ghg` (`OrganizationMember`, `GhgAccess`, audit events),
  `src/features/ghg` (members card), `src/features/admin` (user states),
  `src/features/access` (password rules)

## Problem

An organization was visible only to the user who created it. A second
approved user saw "No organizations yet" and a direct URL sent them to the
login page. Roles were platform-wide Member and Admin only. A client team
would have to share one login, so every action carried the same email and
the audit trail said nothing. An approved user did not appear in the Users
list until they had set a password, and the password policy was eight
characters with no other rule.

## Behavior

- **Members and roles.** An organization has members, each a platform user
  with one organization role: `OWNER` (everything, including membership and
  deleting the organization), `PREPARER` (records facts, draws boundaries,
  classifies, freezes, launches runs, voids runs, types the report header),
  `REVIEWER` (a preparer who can also designate a final run, withdraw it,
  publish and create a correction), and `VERIFIER` (read-only: every page
  and export, no write). The creator of an organization becomes its owner.
  A platform administrator who is not a member is an outsider to the
  organization unless they hold active support access (spec 01.3).
- **Access.** A signed-in user sees the organizations they are a member of.
  Anyone else still gets 404 for the organization and everything under it
  (spec 01). A member whose role does not allow an action gets 403 with the
  role the action needs.
- **Adding members.** An owner adds a member by the email of an existing
  platform account (active or pending), sets or changes the role, and
  removes members. The last owner cannot be removed or demoted. Members are
  managed from the organization overview.
- **Attribution.** Every act on an inventory is recorded with who did it
  and when, in the audit events of spec 05.2: classification (with the
  record and the decision), review of activity data, boundary freeze and
  reopen (with its reason), run launched, final designated (with the
  reviewer's note) and withdrawn, publication,
  correction created, run voided, report header saved. The inventory page
  lists them under History; the report's header names the run's author and
  the publisher (spec 07.4).
- **Pending accounts.** Approving an access request creates the account at
  once in the `PENDING` state, so the Users list shows it with that state;
  setting the password activates it. A pending account cannot sign in.
- **Password policy.** A password is at least 12 characters and contains a
  letter and a digit, wherever it is set (administrator-created accounts,
  the set-password page). The page says so before submission.

Given Abena Owusu (client analyst) added as a `PREPARER` of Asante Gold
Resources: her GHG home lists the organization; she classifies records and
launches runs, each recorded under her email; she cannot publish (403,
"needs the REVIEWER or OWNER role"); a verifier added read-only can open
every report and export but not change a thing.

## API

- `GET /organizations/{id}/members` → `{id, userId, email, displayName,
  role, createdAt}[]`; `POST /organizations/{id}/members` `{email, role}`
  (404 for an unknown email, 409 for an existing member); `PUT
  /organizations/{id}/members/{memberId}` `{role}`; `DELETE
  /organizations/{id}/members/{memberId}` (409 for the last owner).
  An owner by membership only: support access never grants membership
  changes (spec 01.3).
- `OrganizationResponse` gains `myRole` (`OWNER`, `PREPARER`, `REVIEWER`,
  `VERIFIER`, or `ADMIN` for a platform administrator holding active
  support access, spec 01.3).
- Writes by a member without the role: 403 problem detail, title "Access
  denied", detail naming the role.
- `UserResponse.status` gains `PENDING`; the password rules apply to
  `POST /admin/users` and `POST /access-requests/complete` (422 with the
  rule in `errors.password`).
- `AuditEventResponse.action` gains `CLASSIFIED`, `REVIEWED`, `FROZEN`,
  `REOPENED`, `RUN_LAUNCHED`, `FINAL_DESIGNATED`, `PUBLISHED`,
  `CORRECTION_CREATED`, `HEADER_SAVED`; `reason` carries the detail.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Single sign-on; a stronger policy is the alternative the ticket allows.
- Per-inventory roles and a formal review workflow with sign-off states;
  the roles gate the acts and the events record them.
- Email invitations to people without an account (they request access, spec
  01.1, and are then added).

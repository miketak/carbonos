## Problem

Spec 04.6 left four things on the register and the record, and spec 04.11
moved the monthly meter-read template here so that it lands together with
the documented zero. Two of the four are dropped or deferred (below). What
remains:

- A month in which a source consumed nothing cannot be recorded. The
  `quantity > 0` rule of spec 04.5 refuses it, so "the genset did not run in
  August" reads exactly like "nobody has entered August yet": an empty cell
  in the coverage matrix, a missing month to a verifier, and a temptation to
  type a draft or a token quantity to make the matrix green. Completeness
  under ISO 14064-1 section 9.3.3 is data for every source and every period,
  and a zero is data.
- The record has no field for who sold or billed the quantity. The source
  carries a meter or supplier, but a line is often billed by a counterparty
  that is not the source's, and for scope 2 the supplier is what a
  supplier-specific factor hangs on. Search cannot find a record by it.
- The governance walkthrough of 2026-09-24 saw, once, the register list all
  ten records right after **Resolve 7 items** with `tab=attention` in the
  address; a reload showed seven (P3 C1.2). It never reproduced, but the code
  has two ways to get there: a correction, removal, import or bulk act
  refreshes the register and nothing the inventory pages read from the same
  records (their coverage, review and validation), and while a tab switch
  fetches, the register keeps the previous tab's rows on screen with no sign,
  and on an error shows nothing at all.
- A template per facility and month (ticket T-18) was deferred by spec 04.2.

Dropped by the owner on 2026-10-05: the restructured sidebar and month grid
of spec 04.6 (the split register of spec 10 made them redundant). Deferred:
the register remembering the open record between visits (spec 10's open
question stays open).

## Behavior

### The documented zero

- A record may carry a quantity of 0. A zero is a fact like any other: it
  names its unit, its period, its data source (the meter, log or register
  that showed nothing) and its evidence (a typed reference or an attached
  document), and it carries a note of at least 10 characters saying what
  showed that nothing was consumed. The note explains; the evidence is what a
  verifier samples.
- A fact with quantity 0 and no such note is refused under the field:
  `ghg.activity.zero-needs-note` (422 under `note`), "A zero needs a note of
  at least 10 characters: what showed that nothing was consumed." The rule
  holds on entry, on the entry of a draft, and on a correction in either
  direction: a correction from 0 to a quantity, or from a quantity to 0, is
  a correction of spec 04.4 with its reason and its revision of old and new
  values. A draft may hold a 0 without the note until it is entered.
- The form's hint under **Quantity** when it reads 0: "A zero needs a note:
  what showed that nothing was consumed. A meter or log reading is measured;
  'the site said so' is estimated." The data quality tier follows how the
  zero is known, as for every record.
- Readiness (spec 04.6) treats a zero as it treats any record: the status is
  **Ready** when the source, the data source and the evidence are there,
  **Needs attention** when one is missing. A zero carries the informational
  issue `DOCUMENTED_ZERO`, which does not change the status, so the register
  and the preview can label it "Documented zero".
- A zero whose period is longer than one month gets the same warning as any
  long row, on the form and in the import preview, because a zero asserted
  for a year is twelve assertions in one.
- **Coverage.** A month whose included records on a source are all zeros is a
  documented zero month, drawn distinctly from a covered month (data), a
  pending month (a draft) and an empty month (nothing). A month with a zero
  and a non-zero record is covered.
- **Pre-flight.** The COMPLETENESS gate (spec 05) gains an INFO naming every
  documented zero in the boundary: "3 records state a documented zero:
  ACT-0007 (Boiler LPG, Aug 2025: 'Boiler off for relining', Fuel register);
  …", each with the months it covers, its note and its data source, so a
  verifier reads the bases in one screen.
- **The run and the report.** A zero calculates to a zero line, as it already
  did (vectors L14 and C07); it stays in the lines file and the line table.
  The report's data quality statement (spec 07.1) ends with "N source-months
  report a documented zero." when N is above zero, counted from the run's
  included zero lines by source and month, so a reader can tell a reported
  zero from an excluded source (the exclusions list) and from a month with
  no record (the coverage matrix).
- **Import.** `quantity` accepts 0. A row with 0 and an empty `note` is
  rejected: "quantity is 0: say in the note what showed that nothing was
  consumed". Two warnings, which do not reject: "quantity is 0 but
  '*Source*' recorded *q* *unit* the month before" when the source has an
  included non-zero record in the preceding month, and "the same note appears
  on *N* zero rows: say per source what showed nothing was consumed" when one
  note text is pasted down a column.

### The supplier or counterparty

- A record gains **Supplier or counterparty (optional)**, up to 120
  characters, beside **Data source** under "Source and traceability", with
  the hint "Who sold or billed it. Data source is what showed the figure." It
  is shown in the read-only summary, diffed in the correction history under
  the field name `supplier`, and matched by the register's search box.
- The import gains the column `supplier` after `data_source`, with the same
  limit. The preview notes, without rejecting, a supplier that differs from
  the source's meter or supplier: "'*Source*' is recorded with the supplier
  '*X*'; this row names '*Y*'".
- The supplier is not printed in the lines file, the report or the published
  fact snapshot; the evidence pack (ECO-35) is where a per-line counterparty
  belongs.

### The register that tells you it is busy

- Every act that changes records (entering, correcting, removing, importing,
  the bulk acts of spec 04.11) refreshes what the inventory pages read from
  the same records: their coverage, their review list and their validation,
  as well as the register.
- While the register switches tab or page and the new rows are not yet
  there, the table and the summary list are marked busy and dimmed; the
  previous rows are never shown as if they were the answer. When the fetch
  fails, the register prints the refusal where the table would be ("CarbonOS
  could not reach the server. Try again."), instead of a blank page.
- The governance procedure gains a browser-driven step after the correction
  of case C1: click **Resolve 7 items** and read seven rows.

### The monthly template

- `GET /organizations/{id}/activities/import-template.csv?facilityId=&month=YYYY-MM`
  returns one row per emission source of the facility, by name: `facility`
  and `emission_source` filled, `activity_type` pre-filled with the source's
  name, `period_start` and `period_end` the month's first and last day, and
  `quantity`, `unit`, `supplier`, `data_source`, `evidence_ref`,
  `data_quality`, `data_quality_tier`, `uncertainty_percent` and `note`
  blank. Data quality is left blank on purpose: a zero typed from memory is
  an estimate, and a pre-filled "MEASURED" would snapshot tier 1 onto nothing.
  The file is named `activity-<facility>-<month>.csv`. Without parameters the
  template is the one of spec 04.5 with its example row. An unknown facility
  is 404; a month that is not `YYYY-MM` is 422 under `month`.
- The import page's first step gains **Download a monthly template**: a
  facility select and a month field, and the link. Its copy: "One row per
  emission source of the facility for the month. A source that ran nothing
  is recorded as 0 with a note and its reading, not deleted."

## API

- `POST /organizations/{id}/activities` and `PUT /activities/{id}`:
  `quantity` accepts 0; `supplier?` (≤ 120). 422 `ghg.activity.zero-needs-note`
  under `errors.note`.
- `ActivityResponse` gains `supplier`; `issues` may hold `DOCUMENTED_ZERO`.
- `GET /organizations/{id}/activities/page?q=` also matches `supplier`.
- The import's `COLUMNS` gain `supplier`; `PreviewRow.issues` may hold
  `DOCUMENTED_ZERO`; the new rejection and warnings above.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/coverage`: `CoverageRow` gains `zeroMonths`.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/validation`: the COMPLETENESS INFO above.
- The report's `dataQuality.statement` gains the documented-zero sentence.
- `GET /organizations/{id}/activities/import-template.csv` takes
  `facilityId` and `month`.

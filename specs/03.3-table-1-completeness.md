# 03.3: Table 1 completeness

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 3, Table 1 (all five financial accounting
  categories) and "Consolidation at multiple levels"
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-08 (conformance review); implemented 2026-09-08
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`RelationshipType`, `Table1`, `LegalEntity`, boundary
  treatments), `src/features/ghg` (Legal entities page, boundary section)

## Problem

Table 1 of the Standard has five rows: group companies or subsidiaries,
associated or affiliated companies, joint ventures or partnerships or
operations under joint financial control, fixed-asset investments, and
franchises. Spec 03.1 models four of them, and the review of 2026-09-08 found
three differences between the model and the table:

1. **The subsidiary row is misnamed.** `WHOLLY_OWNED` is the type for any
   group company or subsidiary, which the Standard defines by financial
   control, not by ownership percentage. Holland Switzerland in the Standard's
   own example is an 83% subsidiary: 100% under financial control, 83% under
   equity share. The name and the printed `table1Row` text ("wholly owned
   operation or subsidiary") mislead the accountant and the verifier.
2. **One row is modeled twice.** The Standard's joint-control row does not
   distinguish incorporated from non-incorporated ventures; either can sit in
   any row depending on control. `NON_INCORPORATED_JV` derives exactly the
   shares of `JOINT_VENTURE` with the operated flag fixed to true. It is a
   redundant type with a misleading name.
3. **Franchises are missing.** The Standard: "In most cases, the franchiser
   will not have equity rights or control over the franchise. Therefore,
   franchises should not be included in consolidation of GHG emissions data.
   However, if the franchiser does have equity rights or operational/financial
   control, then the same rules for consolidation under the equity or control
   approaches apply."

Chapter 3 also requires that "once a corporate consolidation policy has been
selected, it shall be applied to all levels of the organization", and its
example consolidates a 50% venture held by an 83% subsidiary at 41.5% under
equity share and at 50% under financial control (50% of the subsidiary's
100%). CarbonOS models one entity layer, so the accountant must enter the
chained interest by hand and nothing records how it was derived.

## Behavior

- **Rename the subsidiary type.** `WHOLLY_OWNED` becomes `SUBSIDIARY`, labeled
  "group company or subsidiary (financial control)". Shares are unchanged. The
  `table1Row` text reads "group company or subsidiary under financial
  control; equity share: 83% economic interest". The reporting company's own
  entity is a `SUBSIDIARY` at 100%, held directly.
- **Retire the duplicate type.** `NON_INCORPORATED_JV` is removed. Existing
  entities and treatments of that type become `JOINT_VENTURE` with
  `operatedByCompany` true, which derives the same shares under every
  approach. Frozen versions keep their copied text.
- **Add franchises.** A `FRANCHISE` entity carries the same facts as any other
  entity plus `controlledByCompany` (financial control). Shares follow the
  Standard's rule that a franchise is consolidated only where the franchiser
  holds equity rights or control:

  | Row | Equity share | Financial control | Operational control |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Franchise | economic interest (0 when the company has no equity rights) | 100% when `controlledByCompany`, else 0 | 100% when operated, else 0 |

  A franchise with no interest, no control, and no operation stands outside
  the boundary under every approach, and review auto-excludes its records as
  it does for any zero-share entity (spec 05.1).
- **Consolidate through a parent.** An entity may name a `parentEntityId`
  within the organization. The effective economic interest is the product of
  the interests along the chain to the reporting company, and the Table 1 row
  is applied at each level as the Standard's example does: under financial
  control a venture at 50% held by a subsidiary is 50%, under equity share it
  is 50% of the subsidiary's 83%. In general the share under an approach is
  the entity's own row times each parent's row under the same approach. In a
  boundary, a parent's treatment counts where the parent is in the boundary,
  else the parent's facts. The boundary response shows the chain and the
  effective interest, the `table1Row` text ends with "held through X", and
  the version copies both. An entity with no parent is held directly, as
  today; a chain is optional. A parent chain cannot loop, and an entity that
  is the parent of others cannot be deleted.

Given Holland Industries: Holland America (`SUBSIDIARY`, 83%) and BGB
(`JOINT_VENTURE`, 50%, parent Holland America, operated by Rearden). Under
equity share BGB accounts at 41.5%; under financial control at 50%; under
operational control at 0. Under every approach Holland America accounts at
83%, 100%, and 100%.

## API

- `RelationshipType` values: `SUBSIDIARY`, `JOINT_VENTURE`, `ASSOCIATE`,
  `FIXED_ASSET_INVESTMENT`, `FRANCHISE`. Requests carrying `WHOLLY_OWNED` or
  `NON_INCORPORATED_JV` are refused with 422 naming the replacement.
- `EntityRequest` and `EntityResponse` gain `parentEntityId?` and
  `controlledByCompany?` (franchises only; 409 `Operation not allowed` for
  any other row), and the response gains `controlledByCompany`,
  `effectiveEconomicInterestPercent` and `chain[]` (the parents' names from
  the nearest parent up to the reporting company). 409 when a parent link
  would loop; 404 when it crosses organizations.
- `BoundaryTreatmentRequest` gains `controlledByCompany?`;
  `BoundaryEntityResponse` and `BoundaryVersionEntryResponse` gain
  `controlledByCompany`, `effectiveEconomicInterestPercent` and `chain[]`.

## Data

from the own interest) and `chain_names` on version entries.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A parent chain is optional, so a group can keep entering effective
  interests by hand for an entity held directly.
- Chains that cross two consolidation rows (an associate held through a joint
  venture). The Standard's example does not show one; the share is each row
  in turn, and `table1Row` names the chain.
- Entity fact history over time stays a non-goal (spec 03.1).

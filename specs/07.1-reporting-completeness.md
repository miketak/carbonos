# 07.1: Reporting completeness

- **Status**: Draft
- **Protocol**: Chapter 9 (required information), the Scope 2 Guidance (2015,
  dual reporting), the 2013 amendment adding NF3
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-02
- **Modules**: `ghg`, `src/features/ghg`

## Problem

Chapter 9 requires a public report to contain, at minimum: a description of
the company and the inventory boundary (organizational boundary and the
approach chosen; operational boundary, and for scope 3 the categories
covered); the reporting period; total scope 1 and scope 2 emissions
independent of any trades; emissions for each of the seven gases (CO2, CH4,
N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6, NF3) separately; biogenic CO2 separately from the
scopes; the base year, the recalculation policy and base-year emissions;
methodologies and any changes; and any specific exclusions of sources,
facilities or operations with justification. The Scope 2 Guidance adds that
scope 2 be reported both location-based and market-based wherever contractual
instruments exist. The run report today shows one CO2e figure per scope and
the boundary.

## Behaviour

- **Per gas.** Emission factors carry per-gas components and a GWP set (AR5 by
  default; AR6 selectable); run lines carry kg per gas alongside CO2e; the
  report totals each gas separately.
- **Biogenic CO2** is a factor property and is reported outside the scopes.
- **Scope 2 dual reporting.** A market-based factor may be recorded per
  facility and period (supplier-specific, contract, certificate, residual
  mix); when any exists, the report shows scope 2 location-based and
  market-based side by side.
- **Operational boundary declaration.** An inventory states which scope 3
  categories it covers and why others are excluded; the report prints it.
- **Base year and methodology** sections (spec 06) and the **exclusions**
  list (spec 05.1) appear in the report.
- The report page renders the Chapter 9 elements in the Standard's order.

## API

Factor responses gain `gases[]` and `gwpSet`; inventory gains
`operationalBoundary` `{scope3Categories[], exclusionsRationale}`; run gains
`byGas`, `biogenicCo2Kg`, `scope2MarketBasedKgCo2e`.

## Data

Per-gas columns on factors and run lines; `ghg_inventories.operational_boundary`;
`ghg_market_factors`.

## Events

None.

## Verification

Integration tests: a run over a multi-gas factor reports each gas and the
CO2e; a facility with a market-based factor reports both scope 2 figures; the
declaration and exclusions print.

## Non-goals and open questions

PDF export; an assurance statement; which GWP set the seeded library adopts
(recommendation: AR5, matching the seeded R-410A factor, with AR6 selectable).

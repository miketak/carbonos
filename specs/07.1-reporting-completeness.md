# 07.1: Reporting completeness

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 9 (required information), the Scope 2 Guidance (2015,
  dual reporting), the 2013 amendment adding NF3
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-02; implemented 2026-09-08
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`GwpSet`, `MarketFactor`, per-gas run lines, the report
  endpoint), `src/features/ghg` (report page, declaration and market-factor
  cards)

## Problem

Chapter 9 requires a public report to contain, at minimum: a description of
the company and the inventory boundary (organizational boundary and the
approach chosen; operational boundary, and for scope 3 the categories
covered); the reporting period; total scope 1 and scope 2 emissions
independent of any trades; emissions for each of the seven gases (CO2, CH4,
N2O, HFCs, PFCs, SF6, NF3) separately; biogenic CO2 separately from the
scopes; the base year, the recalculation policy and base-year emissions;
methodologies and any changes; and any specific exclusions of sources,
facilities or operations with justification. The Scope 2 Guidance adds that
scope 2 be reported both location-based and market-based wherever contractual
instruments exist. The 2013 amendment adds that each gas be reported in
metric tonnes and in tonnes of CO2e, that CO2e use 100-year potentials from
one IPCC assessment report per inventory where possible, and that the report
name the source of the potentials and say whether more than one report was
used. Before this spec the run report showed one CO2e figure per scope and
the boundary.

## Behavior

- **Per gas.** Emission factors carry per-gas components: kg of CO2, CH4,
  N2O, SF6 and NF3 per unit, and kg CO2e per unit for the HFC and PFC blends
  (whose potentials the source applied). The inventory chooses the reporting
  **GWP set** (AR5 by default; AR6 selectable), and CO2e per unit is the split
  weighted by that set's 100-year potentials; a factor with no split falls
  back to its source CO2e. The seed's splits reproduce the seeded CO2e exactly
  under AR5. Run lines carry kg per gas alongside CO2e; the run and the report
  total each gas separately, as kg of gas and as CO2e, and the report also
  states both in tonnes (spec 07.2). A blend factor carries the mass of HFCs
  or PFCs per unit and the assessment report its source applied; the
  methodology names every report used (spec 07.2).
- **Biogenic CO2** is a factor property (the biomass factor) and is reported
  outside the scopes, per line and per run. Lines whose factor has the
  reporting basis `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO` are left out of every total and
  printed in the block "Gases outside the scopes (Montreal Protocol)" (spec
  02.4).
- **Scope 2 dual reporting.** A market-based factor (kg CO2e per kWh) may be
  recorded per facility and inventory with its instrument (supplier-specific,
  contract, certificate, residual mix) and source; when any exists, every
  scope 2 line at that facility recorded in an energy unit carries a
  market-based figure beside the location-based one, and the run and the
  report show scope 2 both ways (facilities without an instrument fall back to
  location-based). A scope 2 record in a non-energy unit at such a facility
  warns. Whether an instrument meets the eight Scope 2 Quality Criteria,
  whether a residual mix is available, and the method behind the run total
  are spec 07.2.
- **Operational boundary declaration.** An inventory states which scope 3
  categories it covers and why others are excluded; the report prints it
  beside the categories actually reported.
- **Base year and methodology** sections (spec 06) and the **exclusions**
  list (spec 05.1) appear in the report; the methodology statement names the
  approach, Table 1, unit conversion within a dimension, the GWP set and the
  scope 2 methods.
- The report page renders the Chapter 9 elements in the Standard's order:
  company and boundary, operational boundary, period, emissions by scope,
  by gas, biogenic CO2, base year, methodology, exclusions, lines.

## API

- `EmissionFactorResponse` gains `gases{co2, ch4, n2o, hfcs, pfcs, sf6, nf3}`,
  `biogenicCo2KgPerUnit` and `gwpSet`.
- `InventoryRequest` gains `gwpSet?`; `PUT /inventories/{id}/operational-boundary`
  `{scope3Categories[], exclusionsRationale?}` (409 for a non-scope-3
  category); `InventoryResponse` carries `gwpSet`, `scope3Categories`,
  `scope3ExclusionsRationale`.
- `GET|PUT|DELETE /inventories/{id}/market-factors[/{facilityId}]`
  `{instrumentType, kgCo2ePerKwh, source}`.
- `RunResponse` gains `gwpSet`, `byGas`, `biogenicCo2Kg`,
  `scope2MarketBasedKgCo2e` (null without instruments); `RunLineResponse`
  gains `byGas`, `biogenicCo2Kg`, `marketBasedKgCo2e`,
  `marketFactorKgCo2ePerKwh`, `marketInstrument`.
- `GET /runs/{id}/report` → the Chapter 9 composite: `company`,
  `operationalBoundary`, `period`, `emissions`, `byGas`, `biogenicCo2Kg`,
  `baseYear`, `methodology`, `exclusions`, `lines`, `run`.

## Verification

`GhgApiIntegrationTests`: every seeded factor's split adds up to its CO2e
under AR5; a run over the biomass and landfill factors reports each gas, the
CO2e per gas and biogenic CO2, and the same facts under AR6 shift; a facility
with a market-based factor reports both scope 2 figures while another falls
back, and an inventory without instruments reports none; the declaration,
company, period, gases, methodology and lines print in the report. Frontend:
`RunDetailPage.test.tsx`, `InventoryDetailPage.test.tsx`.

## Non-goals and open questions

PDF export; an assurance statement; rescaling HFC and PFC blends to another
GWP set (they stay on the source's basis, and the report names it). Tonnes,
gas masses, the Scope 2 Guidance disclosures, purchased cooling and boundary
exclusions are spec 07.2.

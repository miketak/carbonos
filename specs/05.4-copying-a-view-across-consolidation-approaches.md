# 05.4: Copying a view across consolidation approaches

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-12)
- **Protocol**: Chapter 3 (the boundary follows from the consolidation
  approach; under equity share an associate and a joint venture are in by
  definition), Appendix F (the lessee's scope depends on the approach),
  Chapter 9 (exclusions reported with a reason that is true for the report
  in hand)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-12 (audit 2026-09-11, findings F37 and F30); implemented
  2026-09-12
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`InventoryService.copyView`, `InventoryAssignment`,
  `LeaseType.derive`, `BoundaryExclusion`, the BOUNDARY and CLASSIFICATION
  gates), `src/features/ghg` (inventory form, inventory page inheritance
  notice, boundary section)

## Problem

Spec 05.3 lets a new inventory copy its view from another one. The copy
takes the boundary, the boundary exclusions, and every assignment as they
stand, whatever the approach of either inventory. Copied across approaches,
two things go wrong. The equity-share view of Asante Gold Resources kept the
30% associate and the 40% joint venture "left out with the entity:
Methodology exclusion, 0% under operational control", although under equity
share their Table 1 shares are 30% and 40% and spec 03.4 would have included
them. And the leased-in head office kept the scope 1 and scope 2 it had
under operational control, so the equity-share run put 47.9 t and 196.9 t
there instead of scope 3 category 8, which spec 04.1 derived correctly on
8 September. No gate noticed either: an excluded entity with a non-zero
share raised nothing, and a stored scope that Appendix F contradicts raised
nothing.

## Behavior

- **Same approach: unchanged.** When the target inventory has the source's
  consolidation approach, "Copy the view from" behaves as spec 05.3
  describes.
- **Different approach: the boundary is rebuilt, the decisions are kept.**
  When the approaches differ, the copy keeps the source's declaration,
  report header, intensity denominators, contractual instruments and
  residual mix, and every assignment's decision (inclusion or manual
  exclusion with its reason, justification and magnitude; the factor, the
  category where it stays valid, the lease type, the scope and proxy
  justifications, the density). The organizational boundary is not copied.
  It is built the way spec 03.4 pre-populates a new inventory: every entity
  whose Table 1 share under the target approach is above zero joins with
  all its facilities, its membership window and financial-control override
  defaulted from the entity record. An entity at 0% under the target
  approach stays outside the boundary and the version records it as
  excluded with the computed reason, as spec 05.1 describes.
- **Boundary exclusions are filtered.** An automatic exclusion (one whose
  detail the source computed for its approach, such as "0% under
  operational control"; the implementation recognizes a detail that reads
  "0% ... under equity share, operational control or financial control",
  case-insensitively) is never copied. A hand-typed exclusion is copied
  only when the entity or facility still has a 0% share under the target
  approach. Every other exclusion is dropped, and the inventory records
  what was dropped and why ("Wassa Gold Associates: Methodology exclusion
  dropped, 30% equity share under this approach"). The inventory page
  lists the dropped exclusions in its inheritance notice until the
  inventory is frozen.
- **Appendix F is derived, never inherited.** An assignment that carries a
  lease type has its scope and category re-derived from `LeaseType.derive`
  under the inventory's approach at three moments: when the assignment is
  copied, when a classification is saved (as today), and when a draft
  inventory's approach changes. The stored scope of a leased assignment is
  therefore always the derivation. As a safety net the CLASSIFICATION gate
  errors when a leased assignment's stored scope or category disagrees
  with the derivation, naming the record and the two scopes.
- **Automatic exclusions are recomputed.** A copied assignment that the
  source excluded as `OUTSIDE_BOUNDARY` or `OUTSIDE_PERIOD` is re-reviewed
  against the target's boundary and period on the first review, as spec
  04.2 already does for automatic exclusions whose reason no longer holds.
  Manual exclusions stay.
- **Gate: an excluded entity with a share.** The BOUNDARY gate errors when
  a whole-entity boundary exclusion names an entity whose Table 1 share
  under the inventory's approach is above zero and the reason is
  `METHODOLOGY`, `OTHER` or `DUPLICATE`: "Wassa Gold Associates is excluded
  but holds a 30% equity share under this approach. Include it, or record
  why it emits nothing." Chapter 3 gives no methodology ground for leaving
  a consolidated operation out. The gate warns instead when the reason is
  `NON_GHG` (the entity has no emitting operation: a royalty holder, a
  holding company) or `NOT_APPLICABLE` (the entity had no operation in the
  period), because those are disclosures Chapter 9 accepts, so the reader
  sees them in the pre-flight and in the report. A facility-level exclusion is
  treated the same way when every facility of an entity with a non-zero
  share is excluded (error for `METHODOLOGY`, `OTHER`, or `DUPLICATE`;
  warning for `NON_GHG` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`); a partial exclusion warns,
  naming the facility and the share.
- **The copy says what it did.** The inheritance notice on the inventory
  page names the source, says "boundary rebuilt from Table 1 under equity
  share" when it was, counts the assignments whose lease treatment moved
  scope, and lists the dropped exclusions.

Given the Asante FY2025 operational-control view copied into "FY2025 Equity
share (AR6)": Wassa Gold Associates (30%) and Ahafo Camp Services JV (40%)
join the boundary with their facilities, their "Methodology exclusion, 0%
under operational control" rows are dropped and listed, Bonsu Royalty
Holdings stays outside at 0%, and every Accra Head Office assignment tagged
`OPERATING_LEASE_IN` lands in scope 3 upstream leased assets. The run then
carries Wassa's and Ahafo's lines at 30% and 40% and the head office in
category 8. Given the same copy into a second operational-control view: the
boundary, exclusions and scopes copy as spec 05.3 describes. Given a draft
equity-share inventory whose approach is changed to operational control:
the head-office assignments return to scope 1 and 2 and the gate is silent.

## API

- `InventoryRequest.copyFromInventoryId` is unchanged. The response of
  `POST /organizations/{id}/inventories` is unchanged.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/inheritance` gains `boundaryRebuilt` (true when
  the approaches differed), `leaseRederived` (the count of copied
  assignments whose scope changed by derivation) and `droppedExclusions[]`
  `{entityId?, entityName?, facilityId?, facilityName?, reason, detail,
  sharePercent}`; `detail` is the source's detail as typed, `sharePercent`
  the Table 1 share under the target approach (30 for 30%).
- `PUT /inventories/{id}` with a new `consolidationApproach` on a draft
  re-derives every leased assignment; the response is unchanged.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/validation`: the BOUNDARY gate carries the new
  error and warning; the CLASSIFICATION gate carries the derivation error.
  Both name the record or entity in the finding text as the other findings
  do.

## Data

`V35__copy_across_approaches.sql` (Number assigned in the planned
implementation order of the audit backlog; renumber if that order changes.):
`inheritance_notes` on `ghg_inventories`, a `text` column holding the JSON
`{boundaryRebuilt, leaseRederived, droppedExclusions[]}` as recorded at
creation (the same column type `published_report` uses; the inventory
carries the notes only for a copy across approaches). Assignments and
boundary tables are unchanged; the copy writes different rows into them.

## Verification

`GhgApiIntegrationTests.aViewCopiedAcrossApproachesRebuildsTheBoundaryAndRederivesLeases`
(the Asante case: the associate and the joint venture join at 30% and 40%, the
computed exclusions are dropped and listed, the head office lands in category
8, the run's scope totals move accordingly; the same copy within one approach
is unchanged; changing a draft's approach re-derives; a leased assignment
whose stored scope is forced to disagree blocks the run).
`GhgApiIntegrationTests.anExcludedEntityWithAShareBlocksTheRun` (a
`METHODOLOGY` exclusion of a 30% associate under equity share errors, a
`NON_GHG` exclusion of the royalty holder warns, the same `METHODOLOGY`
exclusion under operational control at 0% passes). `Table1Test` keeps
`leasedAssetsFollowAppendixF`. Frontend: `InventoryDetailPage.test.tsx` (the
inheritance notice with the rebuilt boundary and the dropped exclusions),
`InventoriesPage.test.tsx` (the form says the boundary is rebuilt when the
chosen approach differs from the source's). Manual: QA procedure 4, case D1
(the copy of 2025 Operational into 2025 Equity rebuilds the boundary and
lists the dropped exclusion); procedure 9, case B1 (a divested joint venture
is disclosed as not applicable, not excluded on methodology grounds).

## Non-goals and open questions

- Copying instruments across approaches is kept as is: a contractual
  instrument belongs to a facility, not to an approach.
- A category that is not valid for the derived scope after re-derivation
  falls back to the stream's or factor's default category; the gate's
  existing category check reports anything left inconsistent.
- Re-syncing a copied view when the source changes later stays out of
  scope (spec 05.3).

# 02.8: Viewing a pack's factors

- **Status**: Approved (2026-09-13)
- **Protocol**: Chapter 6 (choosing an emission factor appropriate to the
  activity), Chapter 9 (the source of each emission factor is documented and
  available to the preparer)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-13
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`FactorPackRowRepository`, `FactorPackEditionRepository`,
  the factor pack endpoints), `src/features/ghg` (emission factors page)

## Problem

A pack is a card on the emission factors page carrying its name, factor count,
source, publication year, Global Warming Potential (GWP) basis, retrieval date
and notes, with one control: **Import pack**. Nothing shows what the pack
contains.

A preparer choosing a factor set has to decide whether a pack covers the
activities they record. Chapter 6 asks them to choose a factor appropriate to
the activity, which they cannot do without reading the factors. Today the only
way to read them is to import the pack, which for `defra-2026` writes 1,868
rows into the organization and, under spec 02.6, cuts a version of every
lineage the organization already holds.

A platform administrator already has this: the workbench of spec 02.5 pages an
edition's rows with a search box and a filter. The gap is the organization's
side, and the query behind the workbench serves both.

## Behavior

- **A member of an organization can read the rows of a pack it can import.**
  The rows come back one page at a time, newest page size 50, in the order the
  publication lists them, which is the order the edition was seeded or
  authored in.
- **Search and filter.** A search term matches a row's code, its name, the
  publisher's activity or the publisher's detail. A filter narrows to one of
  the publisher's categories. The categories the edition carries come back
  with the page, so the filter offers only values that exist.
- **Published editions only, with one exception.** A `DRAFT` edition is never
  visible to an organization, and a request for one answers 404 rather than
  403, so its existence is not confirmed. A `SUPERSEDED` or `WITHDRAWN`
  edition stays readable, because an organization that holds its rows may need
  to explain a past import.
- **A row is identified by more than its name.** Each row shows its code, its
  name, the publisher's category and activity, its unit, its CO2e per unit and
  whether it is approved. The taxonomy and the unit are part of the identity,
  not decoration: 1,157 of the 1,868 rows of `defra-2026` share a name with
  another row, and the three rows named "Gaseous fuels: Butane" differ only by
  unit, at 3,033.38, 1.745 and 0.222 kg CO2e per unit. A list showing the name
  alone is ambiguous by a factor of a thousand.
- **Reading is not importing.** The view carries no import control and no edit
  control. **Import pack** stays on the pack card, beside the licence and the
  provenance a preparer weighs before taking it.

Given a preparer on the emission factors page of an organization that has not
imported `defra-2026`: they open the pack, search for "diesel", read the rows
the edition carries with their units and values, and close it, and the
organization holds exactly what it held before.

Given a request for a draft edition's rows by a member: 404, with no
indication that the edition exists.

## API

- `GET /organizations/{organizationId}/factor-packs/{packId}/rows`
  `?q=&category=&page=0&size=50` →
  `{rows[], page, size, total, categories[]}`, where a row carries `code`,
  `name`, `sourceCategory`, `sourceActivity`, `sourceDetail`, `unit`,
  `kgCo2ePerUnit`, `defaultScope`, `defaultCategory`, `reportingBasis`,
  `co2eOnly` and `approved`.
- The caller must be a member of the organization; an outsider gets 404, as
  spec 01 requires. `size` is clamped between 1 and 200.
- 404 when the edition does not exist, or exists as a draft.

## Data

None. The rows are read through the existing `FactorPackRowRepository.search`,
which spec 02.5 added for the workbench, and the edition is resolved with
`findByEditionIdAndStatusNot(editionId, DRAFT)`.

The assembled-pack cache of `FactorPacks` is deliberately not used: it holds a
whole edition in memory, which is what paging exists to avoid.

## Events

None.

## Verification

`GhgApiIntegrationTests`: `aMemberReadsTheRowsOfAPackItCanImport` (a page, the
total, the categories, the order), `theRowsOfAPackAreSearchedAndFiltered`,
`aDraftEditionsRowsAreNotFoundForAMember`, `anOutsiderCannotReadAPacksRows`,
`aPageOfPackRowsIsCappedAtTwoHundred`. Frontend:
`EmissionFactorsPage.test.tsx` for the control that opens the view, and
`PackRowsDrawer.test.tsx` for paging, searching and that two rows sharing a
name are told apart by their unit and taxonomy.

Manual: `docs/qa/mining/010-factor-pack-maintenance.md`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Searching across editions, and comparing one factor with another. Both were
  considered and set aside; they are recorded in `todo.md`.
- Importing a single row. Spec 02.6 makes the lineage the unit of import.
- Any write from this view. It reads.
- `GET /factor-packs/{packId}`, which returns an edition's every row to any
  authenticated caller and has no caller in the product, stays for now. It is
  worth deleting and is not in the way of this spec.

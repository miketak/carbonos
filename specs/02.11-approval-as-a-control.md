# 02.11: Approval as a control

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-17)
- **Protocol**: Chapter 7 (inventory quality management: QA/QC procedures
  and technical review of emission factors), ISO 14064-1:2018 section 8.1
  (information management: routine accuracy checks and internal review)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-17 (GHG inventory officer review of the 2026-09-16
  walkthrough)
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`EmissionFactor`, `GhgService.setFactorApproval`,
  `GhgRunFactor`, the report), `src/features/ghg` (emission factors page,
  run report)

## Problem

"Approved for use in runs" was a flag. Nothing said who set it or when, and
the person who typed a factor in could tick it in the same breath. Chapter 7
asks for a technical review of the factors an inventory applies, and ISO
14064-1 section 8.1 for routine checks by someone in a position to check. A
flag with no author is not a review, and a verifier sampling the factor
register could not tell a reviewed factor from a hurried one.

## Behavior

- **The record names the approver.** Approving a factor records who approved
  it and when (`approvedBy`, `approvedAt`); unapproving clears both. A factor
  entered by hand records who entered it (`createdBy`); a pack row has no
  author, its approval being the edition adoption record (spec 02.7).
- **The author does not approve their own factor while someone else can.**
  Approval by the person who entered the factor is refused with 409 while the
  organization has another member who may write (owner, reviewer or
  preparer): "You entered 'X'. A factor is checked by someone other than the
  person who typed it (Corporate Standard chapter 7): ask Abena Owusu to
  approve it." A verifier is read-only and does not count.
- **A self-approval is recorded, not hidden.** Where nobody else can check
  it, the author's approval goes through and is recorded as a
  self-approval. The register shows "self-approved: nobody else could check
  it"; the run snapshots the approver with the factor, and the report's
  factor table prints "self-approved by ..." on the row and a sentence under
  the table naming every such factor, so the reader of the report sees the
  control that was and was not available.
- **New factors arrive unapproved** (spec 02.1), so the approval is an act,
  not a default nobody cleared.

## API

- `EmissionFactorResponse` gains `createdBy`, `approvedBy`, `approvedAt`,
  `selfApproved`.
- `POST /emission-factors/{id}/approve` refuses the author with 409 while
  another writer exists.
- `ReportResponse.FactorRow` gains `approvedBy` and `selfApproved`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A reason for the approval, and a review checklist per factor, are not
  taken. The approver's identity and date are the control this spec adds.
- The approver's role is not constrained beyond "may write": a preparer may
  approve another preparer's factor. Whether approval should be a reviewer's
  act alone is a policy question for the organization, not settled here.

# 04.8: Exclusions without a false zero, and gases outside the scopes

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-12)
- **Protocol**: Corporate Standard chapter 9 (exclusions of sources
  disclosed and justified), Scope 3 Standard chapter 11 (exclusions
  justified and, where possible, sized), Corporate Standard chapter 4 (gases
  not covered by the Kyoto Protocol are reported separately from the scopes
  as optional information), the 2013 required-gases amendment (the seven
  gas groups the scopes cover)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-12 (audit 2026-09-11, findings F35 and F26)
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`ExclusionReason`, `InventoryAssignment`,
  `GhgRunExclusion`, the review, the report, `RunCsv`, `ReportPdf`),
  `src/features/ghg` (exclude dialog, activity view, report page)

## Problem

A manual exclusion requires an estimated magnitude in kg CO2e. A preparer
who has no factor for the record (3,200 tonnes of sodium cyanide, for which
no published factor exists) can only type 0, and the report then prints
"Sodium cyanide purchased: ~0 kg CO2e" and totals "Methodology exclusion: 2
records, 0 kg CO2e". A false zero is worse than "not estimated": it reads
as a sized exclusion when nothing was sized. The computed reasons already
print "3 not estimated"; the manual reasons cannot.

The same dialog was the only route for an R-22 top-up. HCFC-22 is a Montreal
Protocol gas: it is outside the scopes and its mass is reported separately,
as optional information. The Standard does not want 0 kg CO2e for it; it
wants 85 kg of gas in a block of its own. Spec 02.4 gives the factor library
a reporting basis for such gases, so a calculated non-Kyoto line lands in
that block. A record without such a factor still needs a way into the same
block, and today it has none.

## Behavior

### Not estimated is an answer

- A manual exclusion (`NON_GHG`, `DUPLICATE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`,
  `METHODOLOGY`, `OTHER`) still needs a justification of at least 10
  characters. Its **estimated magnitude** is one of three things: a positive
  number in kg CO2e; **zero**, only with the statement **This record emits
  nothing** ticked; or **not estimated**. The dialog offers a number field
  and a **Not estimated** choice. Zero typed without the statement is
  refused with 422 and the message "Type the emissions left out, tick 'This
  record emits nothing', or choose 'Not estimated'."
- Not estimated is stored as a null magnitude, the same representation the
  computed reasons already use. No flag is added: a null on a manual
  exclusion means the preparer chose not to size it, and a zero means the
  preparer stated the record emits nothing. Both are deliberate answers and
  the review never fills either in.
- The activity view prints the exclusion as "left out: Methodology
  exclusion; not estimated" or "left out: Methodology exclusion; emits
  nothing" or "left out: Methodology exclusion; about 8.4 t CO2e".
- The report's exclusion summary prints, per reason, "n records, X kg CO2e
  estimated over k records, m not estimated". It never prints a bare 0 for
  a record that was not sized, and it prints "emits nothing" beside a record
  stated to emit nothing. The PDF and the exclusions CSV carry the same
  three states: the CSV column `estimated_kg_co2e` is empty for not
  estimated, `0` for emits nothing, and gains a column
  `estimate_state` with `ESTIMATED`, `EMITS_NOTHING` or `NOT_ESTIMATED`.

### A reason for gases outside the scopes

- A new exclusion reason, `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO`, labelled **Outside the
  scopes: Montreal Protocol gas**, is offered for a record whose quantity is a
  mass. It needs a justification like every manual reason, and instead of a
  magnitude it records the **gas** (a free-text name such as "HCFC-22",
  defaulted from the record's activity type or stream fuel when either names a
  known gas of spec 02.4). Its magnitude is always null and the dialog does
  not ask for one.
- The run's exclusion record snapshots the gas beside the quantity and unit
  the record already carries. The report prints the exclusion, not in the
  exclusions section, but as a row of the block **Gases outside the scopes
  (Montreal Protocol)** that spec 02.4 defines: the gas, the mass in kilograms
  converted from the record's mass unit, basis `RECORDED_MASS`, no
  informational CO2e, and the record reference. The block's columns, wording
  and the list of known gases are spec 02.4's.
- The exclusion summary still counts the reason ("Outside the scopes: Montreal
  Protocol gas: 1 record") so the exclusions section reconciles with the
  review, and points the reader to the block.
- The review offers the reason only when the record's unit is a mass unit; 422
  `errors.reason` otherwise. When a factor with the outside-the-scopes basis
  of spec 02.4 exists for the gas and the record's unit, the gate informs:
  "ACT-0018 R-22 top-up is excluded as a Montreal Protocol gas; a factor
  exists that would report it as a calculated line".

Given the R-22 top-up of 85 kg excluded as `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO` with
the gas "HCFC-22" and the justification "HCFC-22 is a Montreal Protocol gas,
reported outside the scopes": the block reads "HCFC-22 (R-22): 85 kg,
recorded mass, ACT-0018", the scope totals are unchanged, and the exclusions
section says "1 record; see Gases outside the scopes". Given the sodium
cyanide record excluded as `METHODOLOGY` with "no published factor for
sodium cyanide; supplier study pending" and **Not estimated**: the report
prints "Methodology exclusion: 1 record, not estimated" and the record's
line reads "not estimated", never "~0 kg CO2e". Given a duplicate record
excluded as `DUPLICATE` with **This record emits nothing** ticked: the report
prints "Duplicate: 1 record, counted under another record" (the justification
names it).

## API

- `ExcludeRequest` gains `notEstimated?` (boolean), `emitsNothing?` (boolean)
  and `gas?` (string, 1 to 60 characters). Rules for a manual reason other
  than `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO`: exactly one of a positive
  `estimatedKgCo2e`, `estimatedKgCo2e = 0` with `emitsNothing = true`, or
  `notEstimated = true`; anything else is 422 `errors.estimatedKgCo2e`. For
  `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO`: `gas` required (422 `errors.gas`), a magnitude
  refused (422), and 422 `errors.reason` when the record's unit is not a mass.
- `AssignmentResponse` and `RunExclusionResponse` gain `estimateState`
  (`ESTIMATED`, `EMITS_NOTHING`, `NOT_ESTIMATED`, or null for a computed
  reason and for `OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO`) and `gas`.
- `ReportResponse.exclusionSummary` entries gain `estimatedCount` and
  `emitsNothingCount` beside `unestimatedCount`, which keeps counting every
  exclusion without a magnitude, so a computed reason prints "3 not
  estimated" exactly as it always has; the Montreal Protocol reason is
  counted apart and points at the block;
  `ReportResponse.outsideScopes[]` (spec 02.4) gains rows with basis
  `RECORDED_MASS`, `kgCo2eInformational` null and `recordRefs` set.
- The exclusions CSV gains `estimate_state` and `gas`.

## Data

`V41__exclusion_estimate_state_and_gas.sql` (Number assigned in the planned
implementation order of the audit backlog; renumber if that order changes.)
adds `gas` (nullable) on `ghg_assignments` and `ghg_run_exclusions`, and
`OUTSIDE_SCOPES_NON_KYOTO` to the exclusion-reason checks on both tables.
`estimated_kg_co2e` stays nullable; existing manual exclusions with 0 are set
to null and read as `NOT_ESTIMATED`, and the migration writes
"magnitude entered before the three states existed; confirm or size it" into
the exclusion's `detail`, which a manual exclusion never used, so the review
lists them once and the activity view shows the sentence. The run's
exclusions are snapshots of launched runs and are not touched, so no run
figure moves. No flag column is added.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A CO2e figure "for information" on a Montreal gas exclusion: the block
  prints mass only. A calculated line with an outside-the-scopes factor
  (spec 02.4) can carry a CO2e for information; an exclusion has no factor
  and states none.
- Converting a volume record of refrigerant to mass; the reason needs a
  mass unit, and the density registry of spec 02.2 does the conversion
  before exclusion where a density exists.
- The list of known gas names for the default is spec 02.4's Montreal
  Protocol rows; this spec adds none.

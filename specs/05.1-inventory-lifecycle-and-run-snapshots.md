# 05.1: Inventory lifecycle and run snapshots

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 7 (inventory quality management), Chapter 9
  (exclusions reported with justification), Chapter 10 (verification)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-02; implemented 2026-09-08
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`InventoryStatus`, `GhgRunExclusion`), `src/features/ghg`
  (lifecycle bar, report exclusions)

## Problem

Spec 03 made the boundary reproducible. Two gaps remained with one root cause:
the inventory itself had no lifecycle, so its parts were governed piecemeal.

First, the activity view was not reproducible. A run snapshotted the lines it
calculated, but an *excluded* assignment left no trace, and included ones
could be reclassified after the run. Chapter 9 requires exclusions to be
reported with justification, and a verifier (Chapter 10) audits completeness;
neither could be reconstructed for a past run.

Second, a run could be designated final while the boundary was later reopened,
changed and re-frozen, so the inventory's current boundary could differ from
its final report's. And a 0%-share facility was a boundary member
"contributing nothing"; in the Standard's terms it is outside the boundary
under that approach.

## Behavior

- **Inventory states.** `DRAFT`: boundary, operational boundary declaration,
  market factors and activity view editable, runs blocked. `FROZEN`: all of
  them read-only, runs allowed. `FINAL`: a run is designated final; reopening
  is refused until the designation is withdrawn. `PUBLISHED`: a report was
  issued; nothing may change, runs are refused, and a correction is a new
  inventory that supersedes this one (name, period, approach, GWP set,
  boundary, market factors and declaration copied as a new draft). Freezing is
  one act covering both halves; spec 03's boundary status folds into the
  inventory status. Deleting the final run withdraws the designation and
  returns the inventory to `FROZEN`.
- **Complete snapshots.** A run records every assignment that existed when it
  was launched: included ones as lines, excluded ones as exclusion records
  carrying the activity's facts, the documented reason and the detail. The
  report lists exclusions under the lines, grouped by reason.
- **Zero-share entities** are represented as outside the boundary under that
  approach: the gate warns and offers to remove them, review auto-excludes
  their records as `OUTSIDE_BOUNDARY` with the detail "0% accounting share
  under ...", and versions record them as excluded with the reason rather than
  as members at 0%.
- In any state but `DRAFT`, classify, exclude, include, review, boundary,
  market-factor and declaration writes are refused with 409 `Operation not
  allowed`, as are period, approach and GWP-set changes.

Given inventory A frozen and its run designated final, when a user tries to
reopen the inventory, then the request is refused until the final designation
is withdrawn; when they withdraw it, reopen, change the boundary, re-freeze and
run again, the earlier run still cites v1 and lists the exclusions as they
stood.

## API

`POST /inventories/{id}/freeze` → `BoundaryVersionResponse`, `/reopen`,
`/finalize` `{runId}`, `/withdraw-final`, `/publish`, `/supersede` `{name?}`
(201 with the new draft) → `InventoryResponse`, which carries `status`,
`supersededById` and `publishedAt` (instead of `boundaryStatus`).
`POST /runs/{id}/finalize` remains as a shortcut for `/finalize`.
`RunDetailResponse` gains `exclusions[]` `{activityId, facilityName,
activityType, quantity, unit, activityDate, exclusionReason, exclusionDetail}`.
Version entries carry `excluded` and `exclusionReason`.

## Data

`V12__inventory_lifecycle.sql`: `ghg_inventories.status` replaces
`boundary_status` (frozen inventories with a final run became `FINAL`),
`superseded_by_id`, `published_at`; `ghg_run_exclusions` denormalizing the
activity facts and reason as `ghg_run_lines` does; `excluded` and
`exclusion_reason` on version entries; `facility_id` on run lines (spec 06).

## Events

`InventoryPublished(inventoryId, runId)` for future reporting and notification
consumers.

## Verification

`GhgApiIntegrationTests`: a draft blocks the run until frozen; a frozen
inventory refuses every write until reopened; zero-share entities stand
outside the boundary, are excluded on review and recorded as excluded in the
version; a run's exclusion list outlives later reclassification and prints in
the report; a final designation must be withdrawn before reopening, publishing
makes a record that refuses writes, runs and deletion, and superseding creates
a draft correction from the published boundary. Frontend:
`InventoryDetailPage.test.tsx` (lifecycle bar), `RunDetailPage.test.tsx`
(exclusions).

## Non-goals and open questions

A preparer/approver workflow with separate roles needs multi-member
organizations (spec 01 non-goal). Diffing two runs' exclusion sets. Operations
left out of the boundary are reported with a reason under spec 07.2.

# 01.3: Organization confidentiality and deletion safeguards

- **Status**: Approved (2026-09-12, GHG officer review)
- **Protocol**: Corporate Standard chapter 7 (inventory quality management:
  controlled access and change control), chapter 9 (a published report is
  a record the company has issued), ISO 14064-1:2018 section 8.2 (retention of
  the records that support the inventory)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-12 (audit 2026-09-11, findings F3 and F6)
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`GhgAccess`, `Organization`, `GhgService`, the
  organization endpoints, audit events), `src/features/ghg` (GHG home,
  organization overview, delete dialog), `src/features/admin` (support
  access list)

## Problem

Spec 01.2 gave organizations members and roles but kept a platform
administrator's oversight of every organization. The audit signed in as a
fresh administrator with no memberships and found another team's
organization listed with **Edit** and **Delete**, its overview showing
"Your role: ADMIN", its final run totals, its members with editable roles,
and an **Add member** form. A hosted GHG platform holds several clients'
pre-publication inventories. Confidentiality and change control require
that access to an inventory be by membership, not by platform role. Support
staff sometimes need to look inside a client's organization, but that access
must be explicit, logged, and visible to the organization's owners.

Deleting an organization is a two-click dialog with no typed confirmation
and no reason, and it cascades through facilities, activity data, boundary
versions, runs and published reports. Everywhere else the product refuses
hard deletion: runs are voided (spec 05.2), records, facilities and entities
are kept on file as removed (spec 04.4). The organization is the one place
where the whole trail can vanish.

## Behavior

### Membership decides visibility

- An organization and everything under it is visible to its members only.
  A platform administrator who is not a member does not see the
  organization in the GHG home, is not counted in `myRole`, and gets 404 for
  the organization and everything nested under it, exactly like any other
  outsider (spec 01). The administrator's platform role still governs
  **Manage users** and the other platform pages; their GHG home lists the
  organizations they are a member of or hold support access to.


### Support access

- A platform administrator can **assume access** to an organization for
  support. The act needs a **reason** of at least 10 characters. It grants
  the administrator the rights of an `OWNER` in that organization for
  **24 hours** from the moment it is granted, or until the administrator
  ends it, whichever comes first. A day is long enough to work a support
  case across time zones and short enough that forgotten access expires on
  its own; a session-bound grant would vanish on every sign-out and invite
  repeated, less deliberate re-grants.
- While the access is active, `myRole` reads `ADMIN` and the organization
  appears in the administrator's GHG home with a **Support access** badge.
  After it ends or expires, the administrator is an outsider again.
- Assuming, ending and expiring are recorded in the organization's history
  as audit events: `ADMIN_ACCESS_ASSUMED` with the reason,
  `ADMIN_ACCESS_ENDED` when the administrator ends it, `ADMIN_ACCESS_EXPIRED`
  when the 24 hours run out. Owners see them on the organization overview
  under **Support access** ("Support access: support@ecoriv.com since
  12 September 2026, 09:14: ticket 4512, preparer cannot open the run") and
  in the organization history. An active grant is shown with its expiry;
  past grants stay listed.
- Administrators find organizations through an administrator-only list
  that returns each organization's id, name, owner emails, member count and
  whether the administrator currently holds support access. It carries no
  inventory data, no facility count and no totals.
- Every act an administrator performs under support access is attributed to
  the administrator's own account in the audit events and carries the marker
  "under support access", so the history distinguishes it from a member's
  act. The organization's owners can read who did what.

### Deleting an organization

- Only an `OWNER` can delete an organization; support access never grants
  deletion or membership changes.
- Deletion is refused with 409 while any inventory of the organization is
  `PUBLISHED` or `FINAL`, or while any run is designated final. The problem
  detail names each blocking inventory by name and status. A published
  report is a record the company has issued; the trail that supports it
  stays.
- When nothing blocks it, deletion needs the organization's **name typed
  exactly** and a **reason** of at least 10 characters. The organization is
  then removed with a **tombstone**, in the vocabulary of spec 04.4: the row
  stays in the database with who removed it, when and why; it no longer
  appears in any list; every request for it or for anything under it
  returns 404; its children stay in the database untouched. Nothing is
  cascaded. The removed name is released for reuse.
- The delete dialog shows, before the fields, the list of inventories that
  block deletion when there are any, and disables **Delete** with the
  sentence "Publish records are kept: withdraw the final designation or
  supersede the published inventory first." When nothing blocks, the dialog
  shows the typed-name field ("Type Asante Gold Resources Ltd to confirm")
  and the reason field, and **Delete** stays disabled until both are valid.

Given the Sankofa Gold plc organization with two members and an
administrator who is not one of them: the administrator's GHG home does not
list it, a direct URL to its overview returns 404, and the administrator's
support list shows "Sankofa Gold plc, owners admin@carbonos.test". When the
administrator assumes access with "ticket 4512: preparer cannot open the
run", then the organization appears with a **Support access** badge until
the next day at the same time, the overview tells the owners who holds
access and why, and the administrator's classification of a record is
recorded under the administrator's email.

Given Asante Gold Resources Ltd with a published FY2025 inventory: when its
owner clicks **Delete**, then the dialog lists "FY2025 Corporate:
Published" and refuses. Given the same organization after every inventory
is a draft: when the owner types the name and the reason "test tenant
created for the walkthrough, no client data", then the organization
disappears from the list, its URL returns 404, and the row stays with the
owner's email, the time and the reason.

## API

- `GET /organizations`: a platform administrator receives the organizations
  they are a member of or hold active support access to. An outsider's
  request for `/organizations/{id}` and everything under it returns 404
  whatever their platform role.
- `GET /admin/organizations` → `{id, name, ownerEmails[], memberCount,
  supportAccess: {grantedAt, expiresAt, reason} | null}[]`. Administrator
  only (403 otherwise).
- `POST /organizations/{id}/support-access` `{reason}` → `{grantedAt,
  expiresAt, reason}` (201). Administrator only; 422 `errors.reason` when
  shorter than 10 characters; 409 when the administrator is already a member
  of the organization.
- `DELETE /organizations/{id}/support-access` → 204; ends the caller's
  active grant. 404 when there is none.
- `OrganizationResponse` gains `supportAccess: {adminEmail, grantedAt,
  expiresAt, reason}[]` (active grants, for the owners' overview) and keeps
  `myRole`, which reads `ADMIN` only while the caller holds an active
  grant.
- `DELETE /organizations/{id}` takes `{name, reason}` in the body: 422
  `errors.name` when the typed name differs from the organization's name,
  422 `errors.reason` under 10 characters, 409 problem detail "Organization
  cannot be deleted" listing the blocking inventories, 403 for anyone but an
  owner, including an administrator under support access.
- `GET /organizations/{id}/events` → `AuditEventResponse[]` for the
  organization-level events (support access assumed, ended, expired;
  organization deleted). `AuditEventResponse.action` gains
  `ADMIN_ACCESS_ASSUMED`, `ADMIN_ACCESS_ENDED`, `ADMIN_ACCESS_EXPIRED` and
  `ORGANIZATION_DELETED`.

## Data

`V37__organization_confidentiality_and_deletion.sql` (Number assigned in the
planned implementation order of the audit backlog; renumber if that order
changes.):

- `ghg_support_access` (id, organization_id, admin_user_id, admin_email,
  reason, granted_at, expires_at, ended_at), with an index on
  (organization_id, expires_at).
- `deleted_at`, `deleted_by` (varchar 320) and `delete_reason` (varchar
  500) on `ghg_organizations`; the unique index on `lower(name)` becomes a
  partial index over rows where `deleted_at IS NULL`, so a removed name can
  be reused.
- `ghg_audit_events.inventory_id` becomes nullable and the table gains
  `organization_id` (backfilled from the inventory) with a check that one of
  the two is set; the action check gains the four new actions; an index on
  (organization_id, created_at DESC).
- Every organization-scoped query filters `deleted_at IS NULL`, as the
  facility, entity and record queries do since spec 04.4.

## Events

None new. The `ghg` module resolves the administrator's email through
`UserDirectory`, as spec 01.2 does for members.

## Verification

(an administrator without membership gets an empty list and 404; the
support list shows the organization without inventory data; assuming access
with a reason lists the organization with `myRole` `ADMIN`, records the

grant behaves as ended).

and reason, the disabled button), `OverviewPage.test.tsx` (the support
access notice), `admin` feature test for the support list and the assume
form. Manual: QA procedure 001 gains the support-access case.

## Non-goals and open questions

- Restoring a deleted organization; the tombstone keeps the data for an
  operator with database access.
- Notifying owners by email when support access is assumed; the overview
  and the history carry it.
- A shorter or configurable support window; 24 hours is fixed.
- Whether an owner can revoke an active support grant. Not in this spec:
  the grant is the platform operator's tool and the owner sees it.

# 05.5: Review at scale and deliberate lifecycle acts

- **Status**: Implemented (2026-09-12)
- **Protocol**: Chapter 7 (inventory quality management: a review and
  approval step, records of who decided what and why), Chapter 9 (the
  report is a dated, versioned document a reader can follow), ISO 14064-1:2018 section 8.2 (records of changes to the inventory)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-12 (audit 2026-09-11, findings F36, F43, F54 and
  F31); implemented 2026-09-12 (the factor picker's contents follow with
  spec 02.3)
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`InventoryService` freeze, reopen, designateFinal,
  searchAssignments; `BoundaryVersion`; `GhgAuditEvent`; the report header),
  `src/features/ghg` (lifecycle bar, inventory page, activity view, report
  page, PDF)

## Problem

Four acts on an inventory are less deliberate than the acts around them.
Freezing went through with three unclassified records and the launch on
hold, so the only way to fix them was a reopen that cut a new boundary
version. "Reopen as draft" posts at once and the History says "reopened as
a draft" with no why, while void, withdrawal, correction and removal all
ask for a reason. "Mark as final" acts on one click with no confirmation
and no reviewer's note, although the final run is what the base year, the
publication and the report attach to. The report then cites "Boundary
version 3" in section 01 beside "Version 1" in the header, two numberings a
reader confuses. And the activity view renders every record with a
107-option factor select: 36 records took 8 seconds to load and produced a
215,000-character accessibility tree; a mine with thousands of monthly rows
cannot be reviewed in that layout.

## Behavior

- **Freeze needs a clean classification.** `POST /inventories/{id}/freeze` is
  refused with 409 while the CLASSIFICATION gate has errors (an included
  record without a factor, a scope departure without its justification, a
  leased assignment whose scope disagrees with Appendix F) or while a draft
  record at a facility in the boundary has a period overlapping the
  inventory's. The problem detail names the records ("3 records block the
  freeze: ACT-0012 'Genset diesel' at Nkran is not classified, ..."). The
  BOUNDARY gate's "neither in the boundary nor excluded" error keeps allowing
  the freeze, as spec 07.2 decided, so the two halves can be drawn in either
  order. The **Freeze inventory** dialog shows the gate summary first and
  disables its button while the freeze would be refused, saying why.
- **Reopen needs a reason.** `POST /inventories/{id}/reopen` takes a `reason`
  of at least 10 characters (422 `errors.reason` otherwise). The `REOPENED`
  audit event carries it, the boundary version the reopen supersedes records
  who reopened it, when, and why, and the History prints "reopened as a draft:
  instruments added for Nkran". The **Reopen as draft** control opens a dialog
  with the reason field and a sentence that the next freeze cuts a new
  boundary version.
- **Final is confirmed with a note.** **Mark as final** opens a confirmation
  that names the run and its total, offers an optional **review note** (up to
  500 characters), and says that the final run is what the report and the base
  year attach to. The `FINAL_DESIGNATED` audit event carries the note; the
  inventory records the note, who designated the run and when; the report
  header prints "Final designated by abena@asantegold.com on 12 September
  2026" and the note when there is one. The server already restricts the act
  to `REVIEWER` and `OWNER` (`GhgAccess.checkApprove`); the page shows the
  button per the approve set of spec 01.4, disabled with its tooltip for a
  preparer. The PDF prints the same header lines.
- **Two numberings, two names.** Everywhere the product prints a number of
  either kind it says which: **report version** (1 for the first inventory of
  the period, one more per correction, spec 07.4) and **boundary version**
  (one per freeze, spec 03). The report header row reads "Report version 1"
  and section 01 reads "Boundary version 3 of 3". The inventory page's
  lifecycle bar shows "Boundary version 3" while frozen and "3 boundary
  versions cut" as a draft; the boundary version panel lists each version with
  who cut it, and, when it was superseded by a reopen, who reopened it and
  why. The PDF uses the same words; the lines CSV has no version column (its
  header names the run's fields), so nothing there needed renaming.
- **Review at scale.** The activity view stays server-paged at 50 rows through
  `GET /inventories/{id}/assignments/page` (spec 04.5), and its filters grow:
  **scope**, **category**, **stream**, and **lease** join status and facility
  beside the search box; the counts in the review header (total, included,
  excluded, unclassified) are served with every page and describe the whole
  view, not the page. A row no longer renders a factor `<select>`. It shows
  the chosen factor as text with its pack and approval marks and opens the
  searchable factor picker of spec 02.3 on demand, opened by **Choose
  factor** or **Change factor**; the factor list is fetched once per page
  load and shared by every row, and the picker filters that one list in the
  browser. Scope, category and lease stay
  as small selects that render only once a factor is chosen. The page target
  is under two seconds for 50 rows on the reference machine, and the
  accessibility tree of a page is under 30,000 characters, so assistive
  technology and test harnesses can walk it.

Given the Asante FY2025 draft with ACT-0012, ACT-0019 and ACT-0033
unclassified: **Freeze inventory** is disabled with "3 records are not
classified; classify or exclude them first", the API refuses with 409 naming
the three, and after they are classified the freeze cuts boundary version 1.
Given the frozen inventory reopened "instruments added for Nkran": version 1
records the reopen and its reason, the History prints it, and the next freeze
cuts version 2. Given Abena (Reviewer) marking Run 003 final with the note
"reconciled against the fuel ledger": the header reads "Final designated by
abena@asantegold.com on 12 September 2026: reconciled against the fuel
ledger", and Kofi (Preparer) sees **Mark as final** disabled with "Needs the
Reviewer or Owner role." (spec 01.4). Given 1,200 records in the view: the
first page renders 50 rows with their factors as text, the header counts say
1,200, and filtering by scope 2 and Nkran returns the 84 electricity rows over
two pages.

## API

- `POST /inventories/{id}/freeze`: 409 problem detail, title "Operation not
  allowed", detail naming the blocking records, with `errors.records[]`
  `{activityId, recordRef, activityType, facilityName, problem}`. `GET
  /inventories/{id}/validation` carries the same list as `freezeBlockers[]`,
  so the dialog can disable its button before asking.
- `POST /inventories/{id}/reopen` `{reason}`: 422 `errors.reason` when
  missing or under 10 characters. `BoundaryVersionResponse.version` (the
  summary, which the version list also returns) gains `reopenedBy`,
  `reopenedAt` and `reopenReason` (null while the version is current or the
  inventory was never reopened from it).
- `POST /inventories/{id}/finalize` `{runId, note?}` and `POST
  /runs/{id}/finalize` `{note?}`: 422 when the note exceeds 500 characters.
  `InventoryResponse` gains `finalDesignatedBy`, `finalDesignatedAt` and
  `finalNote`. `ReportResponse.header` gains the same three fields and
  `boundaryVersionNo` and `boundaryVersionCount`; its `version` is labeled
  "Report version" wherever it prints.
- `AuditEventResponse.reason` carries the reopen reason and the final note
  for `REOPENED` and `FINAL_DESIGNATED`.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/assignments/page` gains `scope`, `category`,
  `streamId` and `leaseType` query parameters beside `q`, `facilityId`,
  `status`, `page` and `size`; the envelope `{items, page, size, total,
  included, excluded, unclassified}` is unchanged. `GET
  /inventories/{id}/assignments` (unpaged) is kept for tests and small
  views and is no longer called by the activity view.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A formal review state between frozen and final (a "ready for review"
  flag with sign-off) stays out of scope; the note and the roles record the
  step.
- Server-side sorting of the activity view; the page keeps entry order.
- Virtualized rendering within a page; 50 rows render as a list.
- The searchable factor picker's contents (pack, approval, duplicates) are
  spec 02.3's; this spec only requires that a row opens it on demand. The
  plain search box shipped here was replaced by that picker on 2026-09-12.
- The role-aware screen of spec 01.4 is applied here to **Mark as final**
  only (disabled with "Needs the Reviewer or Owner role." for a preparer);
  the other controls follow with spec 01.4.
- Whether a freeze should also wait for COMPLETENESS warnings (straddling
  records, missing evidence) is left to the accountant; only errors block.

## Problem

The register lists facts but says nothing about whether each one is ready
for review. An engineer collecting a year of data from six sites works from
partial information: a dispensing total arrives before its invoice, a meter
reading before its unit is confirmed. Today such a record cannot be saved at
all, so the knowledge that a source exists lives in a spreadsheet until the
figures are complete, and the inventory's completeness gate cannot see it.
Records have no human-readable identity: a verifier citing a line says
"the diesel record for Nkran in March", or copies a UUID. An import is
validated and saved in one request and the file is discarded, so nothing
on file says which spreadsheet a row came from. Evidence is reachable only
one record at a time; there is no view of the documents an organization
holds, no way to find a document whose record was removed, and nothing a
verifier can take as an evidence index. Editing is one modal per record.

## Behavior

- **Record numbers.** Every record carries a number unique within its
  organization, shown as `ACT-0001` (`recordRef`). Numbers are taken from
  the organization's counter under a row lock; an import takes a block. A
  number is never reused, a removed record keeps its number, and numbers
  follow entry order (the one-off backfill numbered existing records by
  period start, then entry time). The register searches by number
  (`ACT-0012`, `act12` and `12` all find it); run lines and exclusions
  snapshot the number and print it as `record_ref` beside `record_id` in the
  calculation files and in the report's exclusion and line tables.
- **Readiness.** A record's **status** is computed, never set: `READY` when
  it has quantity, unit, period, a source stream, a data source and
  evidence; otherwise `NEEDS_ATTENTION` with the **issues** named
  (`MISSING_QUANTITY`, `MISSING_UNIT`, `MISSING_PERIOD`, `NO_STREAM`,
  `NO_DATA_SOURCE`, `NO_EVIDENCE`), or `DRAFT`. Evidence means a file or a
  link attached, or a document reference typed on the record (a paper
  dispensing log produced on the site visit is evidence); a reference with
  nothing attached adds the informational issue `EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_ONLY`,
  which does not change the status. Data quality tier and uncertainty do not
  affect readiness: they describe the data, and the gate already reports
  tier 4 and 5. The page counts `ready`, `readyWithDocument` (ready and
  something attached), `needsAttention` and `drafts` over the records that
  match the search and filters; the register filters by status. Readiness
  reflects completeness, not assurance, and the UI says so.
- **Drafts.** A record can be saved as a draft with only a facility and an
  activity type; quantity, unit and period may be missing. A draft is not a
  fact: inventories do not review it, the gates do not check it, the coverage
  matrix does not count it as covered, imports do not treat it as a
  duplicate, and it can never be included in a run. Editing a draft needs no
  reason and leaves no revision. Entering a draft as a fact needs the three
  fields and leaves one revision of kind `ENTERED` with who, when and every
  value, so the audit trail names who entered the figures rather than who
  opened the stub. A fact never goes back to a draft (409). CSV import
  creates facts only.
- **Drafts and completeness.** A draft at a facility in the inventory's
  boundary whose period overlaps the reporting period, or has no period, is
  a known source with data outstanding: the COMPLETENESS gate warns "n draft
  records are not entered: ACT-0021 'Diesel' at Nkran Mine (no period), ...
  Complete or remove them before the run" (non-blocking). The coverage
  matrix marks the months a draft's period touches as **pending** (data
  expected, not received), distinct from covered and from empty.
- **The evidence gate, aligned.** The gate judges evidence exactly as the
  register does, through `ActivityReadiness`: a WARNING when a record has
  neither a reference nor an attachment ("has no evidence: no reference and
  nothing attached"), an INFO when it cites a reference but nothing is
  attached. Spec 04.4's "has no evidence reference" warning is replaced.
- **The register.** Tabs All / Needs attention / Ready (and Drafts when any
  exist) over the same search, facility filter and a month picker; filters
  live in the URL, so a link opens the same view. Rows show the record's
  stream and number, never a scope: scope is an inventory decision (spec
  04.1) and the same record can be scope 1 in one inventory and scope 3 in
  another. A completeness banner reads "14 of 18 records ready, 9 with a
  document on file" with a "Resolve n items" link to the Needs attention
  tab. Clicking a row opens a **drawer** beside the table with Activity and
  Evidence tabs, the readiness checks, the record's fields in three groups
  (activity details; source and traceability, where the reference is
  labelled "Document reference (invoice, meter reading or log number as
  printed on the document)" and the stream's meter or supplier is shown;
  notes for the reviewer), a hint "Stream default: Scope 1 · Stationary
  combustion. Scope is confirmed in each inventory's review" when a stream is
  chosen, a record navigator, and Save draft, Save and Save & next. Keyboard:
  `n` adds a record, `/` focuses the search, `j`/`k` or the arrows move
  between rows, Enter opens, Esc closes; keys typed in a field do nothing
  else. Bulk selection offers Remove with one reason written to every
  record; there is no bulk evidence link (one URL on fifty records would
  manufacture readiness).
- **Import preview and provenance.** Choosing a CSV runs a **dry run**: the
  same validation as the import, saving nothing, returning each row as it
  would import with its readiness, **control totals** (rows and summed
  quantity per facility, stream and unit, to check against the spreadsheet's
  footer), and **warnings** that do not reject a row: a row matching a draft
  on facility, activity and period, mixed units within one stream in the
  file, a period longer than one month. "Add records" then imports the same
  file. Every import keeps the file in the object store under
  `ghg/imports/<batch>` with its name, SHA-256, size, row count, who and when;
  each imported record carries the batch and its row. The kept files are
  listed with the source documents and can be downloaded.
- **Source documents.** A page under Activity data lists every document of
  the organization's records newest first, searchable by document name,
  activity, facility or reference, filtered by facility and to links only
  (link rot) or to documents whose record was removed (orphans), each linked
  to its record in the drawer; the files each import came from are listed
  above them with their digests. An **evidence index** downloads as CSV
  (record ref, activity, facility, period, reference, document, kind, URL,
  content type, size, who, when) for the verifier's pack. Evidence of a
  record a run has calculated cannot be deleted (409): the run's line names
  the file and the file stays on file.

Given a July dispensing total of 12,500 litres at Nkran whose invoice has
not arrived: the engineer saves it as a draft with the quantity and the
period and no source; the register shows it under Drafts, the July cell of
the coverage matrix reads pending, and the gate warns that one draft is not
entered. When the invoice arrives, they attach it, type "INV-2938" as the
document reference and press Save: the record becomes ACT-0034, READY, and
its history shows "Entered from a draft" with every value and the
engineer's name. Given a 13-row CSV with one row in gallons under a stream
recorded in litres: the preview lists the 13 rows, totals 12 rows of
143,300 litres and 1 row of 300 gallons for the stream, warns on the gallon
row, and imports once the engineer confirms; the file is on the Source
documents page with its digest. Given a verifier sampling ACT-0034: the
line's `record_ref` is in the calculation file, the record opens from
`?record=`, and the evidence index names the invoice.

## API

- `ActivityResponse` gains `recordNo`, `recordRef`, `draft`, `status`,
  `issues`, `importBatchId`, `importRow`, `createdAt`; `quantity`, `unit`,
  `periodStart` and `periodEnd` are null on a draft that lacks them.
- `CreateActivityRequest` gains `draft?`; `quantity`, `unit`, `periodStart`
  and `periodEnd` are optional in the schema and required by the service
  unless `draft` is true: 422 `errors.quantity`, `errors.unit` or
  `errors.periodStart` ("... is required unless the record is saved as a
  draft"). `periodEnd` defaults to `periodStart`.
- `PUT /activities/{id}`: no `reason` needed while the record is a draft;
  entering it (`draft: false`) records an `ENTERED` revision; `draft: true`
  on a fact is 409.
- `GET /activities/{id}` → `ActivityResponse`.
- `GET /organizations/{id}/activities/page` gains
  `status=READY|NEEDS_ATTENTION|DRAFT` and `sort=recordNo`; `q` matches the
  record number; the response gains `counts: {total, ready,
  readyWithDocument, needsAttention, drafts}` over the search and filters.
- `POST /organizations/{id}/activities/import?dryRun=true` →
  `{dryRun: true, imported: 0, rejected, rows: [{row, facilityName,
  streamName, activityType, quantity, unit, periodStart, periodEnd,
  dataSource, evidenceRef, dataQuality, dataQualityTier, status, issues}],
  totals: [{facilityName, streamName, unit, rows, quantity}], warnings:
  [{row, message}]}`; without `dryRun` the response gains `batchId`,
  `totals` and `warnings`.
- `GET /organizations/{id}/import-batches` → `[{id, fileName, sha256,
  rowCount, sizeBytes, importedBy, importedAt}]`;
  `GET /import-batches/{id}/file` streams the CSV.
- `GET /organizations/{id}/evidence/page?q&facilityId&filter=ALL|LINK_ONLY|ORPHANED&page&size`
  → `{items: [{id, kind, name, url, contentType, sizeBytes, uploadedBy,
  uploadedAt, activityId, recordNo, recordRef, activityType, streamName,
  facilityId, facilityName, periodStart, periodEnd, evidenceRef,
  recordRemoved}], page, size, total}`;
  `GET /organizations/{id}/evidence/index.csv`.
- `DELETE /evidence/{id}`: 409 when a run line snapshots the record.
- `RunLineResponse`, `RunExclusionResponse` gain `recordRef`; the
  calculation files gain `record_ref` after `record_id`.
- `CoverageRow` gains `pendingMonths`.
- The gate's evidence findings change wording as described above and a
  draft warning is added.

## Data

`V34__activity_drafts_record_numbers_readiness.sql`: `organization_id`
(denormalized from the facility), `record_no` (unique per organization,
backfilled by period start then entry time), `draft`, `import_batch_id` and
`import_row` on `ghg_activities`; `quantity`, `unit`, `period_start` and
`period_end` become nullable with a check that a non-draft carries all four;
`next_record_no` on `ghg_organizations`; `record_no` on `ghg_run_lines` and
`ghg_run_exclusions` (backfilled); `ghg_import_batches`; `ENTERED` in the
revision kind check.

None. `ghg` calls the `media` module's public `MediaStorage` to keep import
files under `ghg/imports/<id>`.

## Non-goals and open questions

- A restructured sidebar (Needs attention, Ready for review and Source
  documents as sections, a reporting-year month grid); the tabs and the
  month picker live on the page.
- A supplier or counterparty field; the stream carries the meter or supplier.
- Bulk evidence links; bulk actions through the backend; XLSX.
- In the preview: which facility-months the file would cover, and outliers
  against a stream's history. A chase list of facility-months with ready
  records but no document.
- A readiness issue for tier 4 and 5 records with no note explaining the
  estimate (`ESTIMATE_UNEXPLAINED`).
- A documented zero: the `quantity > 0` check forbids "genset not run this
  month". Engineers must not use a draft to mean "nothing to report"; a
  follow-up spec should allow an explicit zero with a note.

# 04.3: Source streams, explicit scope choice and proxy factors

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 4 (scope follows the reporter's relationship to the
  source; contractor equipment is scope 3 unless the company directs its
  operation), Chapter 7 and ISO 14064-1 section 9.3.3 (completeness needs a
  controlled list of sources), Scope 3 Standard category 1 and 4 guidance
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-09 (audit findings F10, F26, F27, F30, ticket T-11);
  implemented 2026-09-09
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`SourceStream`, activity records, classification, the
  CLASSIFICATION gate, run lines), `src/features/ghg` (facility page: stream
  register; activity form; activity view)

## Problem

"Activity" was free text and factor candidates were filtered by unit
dimension only, so genset diesel offered Petrol and Water supply and nothing
prevented "Waste to landfill" for cyanide. Choosing a factor classified the
record at once with the factor's default scope, so contractor fleet diesel
landed in scope 1, and the gate warned "'Diesel' suggests scope 1" on every
contractor line for ever. Scope 2 and scope 3 factors were locked to their
scope, although mines run their own landfills (scope 1 methane) and host
contractors' consumption. A proxy factor could not be flagged, and the
record's own description was dropped from the snapshot line.

## Behavior

- **Source streams.** Each facility has a register of source streams: a
  name ("Standby gensets"), a stream kind (`STATIONARY_COMBUSTION`,
  `MOBILE_COMBUSTION`, `PROCESS`, `FUGITIVE`, `PURCHASED_ELECTRICITY`,
  `PURCHASED_HEAT_STEAM_COOLING`, `WASTE`, `TRANSPORT`, `TRAVEL`,
  `COMMUTING`, `PURCHASED_GOODS`, `OTHER`), the fuel or material where it
  applies ("Diesel"), the meter or supplier, whether the source is **owned
  or controlled** by the company or **operated by a contractor**, and a
  note. The stream kind fixes the categories a record of the stream may be
  classified into; the ownership fixes the default scope: owned combustion
  and process streams default to scope 1, purchased energy to scope 2, and a
  contractor-operated stream to scope 3 purchased goods and services (or
  upstream transport for a transport stream).
- **Records name a stream.** An activity record may name the stream it
  belongs to; the free-text activity type stays as the record's own
  description. When a record names a stream, the picker offers factors whose
  category matches the stream's kind (and whose unit converts), the
  classification defaults to the stream's default scope and category, and
  the CLASSIFICATION gate does not warn about a scope that matches the
  stream's default. A record without a stream behaves as before.
- **Scope is an explicit choice.** Choosing a factor no longer classifies a
  record by itself: the classification is saved with the scope and category
  the accountant chose, defaulted from the stream, or from the factor when
  there is no stream. Any factor may be used in any scope with a
  **justification**: a classification whose scope differs from the stream's
  default (or, without a stream, from the factor's default) records a
  `scopeJustification` of at least 10 characters; the gate errors when it
  is missing and, once it is recorded, warns no more. The "inherent scope"
  lock is gone.
- **Proxy factors.** A classification may flag the factor as a **proxy**
  with a justification ("emulsion explosive: no published factor; ANFO
  applied"). Proxy lines print the flag and the justification, and the
  report's methodology counts them.
- **Lines carry the record.** Every snapshot line records the record's own
  description, its stream, the scope justification and the proxy flag with
  its justification (spec 07.5 exports them).

Given the Rocksure contractor fleet diesel recorded against the stream
"Contract mining fleet, contractor-operated": the classification defaults
to scope 3 purchased goods and services with the Diesel factor, the gate is
silent, and the line reads "Contract mining fleet diesel, Diesel, scope 3".
Given the camp landfill as an owned `WASTE` stream: "Waste to landfill" is
chosen in scope 1 fugitive emissions with the justification "company-operated
landfill", and the gate is silent.

## API

- `GET|POST /facilities/{id}/streams`, `PUT|DELETE /streams/{id}`
  `{name, kind, fuel?, meterOrSupplier?, contractorOperated, note?}`; a
  stream with records cannot be deleted (409).
- `CreateActivityRequest` gains `streamId?`; `ActivityResponse` and
  `AssignmentResponse` carry `streamId`, `streamName`, `streamKind`,
  `contractorOperated`, `defaultScope` and `defaultCategory`.
- `ClassifyRequest` gains `scopeJustification?`, `proxy?` and
  `proxyJustification?`; 409 when a proxy has no justification.
- `RunLineResponse` gains `streamName`, `scopeJustification`, `proxy` and
  `proxyJustification`.
- `EmissionFactorResponse.scopeAgnostic` stays for information; it no longer
  locks anything.

## Data

`V26__source_streams.sql`: `ghg_source_streams`; `stream_id` on
`ghg_activities`; `scope_justification`, `proxy` and `proxy_justification`
on `ghg_assignments`; `stream_name`, `scope_justification`, `proxy` and
`proxy_justification` on `ghg_run_lines`.

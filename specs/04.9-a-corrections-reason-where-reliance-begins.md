# 04.9: A correction's reason, where reliance begins

- **Status**: Draft
- **Protocol**: Chapter 7 (inventory quality management: documented QA/QC
  procedures, error detection and correction, documentation and archiving),
  Chapters 5 and 9 (errors found after reporting, recalculation and the
  context a report gives for significant changes), ISO 14064-1 clause 8 (GHG
  information management: procedures to prevent, detect and correct errors)
  and 9.3.3 (describing a change to data previously used, with its
  justification), ISO 14064-3 (what a verifier tests)
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-13
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`ActivityRecord`, `ActivityRevision`,
  `GhgService.updateActivity`, `InventoryAssignment`, run lines),
  `src/features/ghg` (activity drawer, history modal, register page)

## Problem

Correcting a fact needs a reason of at least 5 characters, always, from the
moment the record stops being a draft (spec 04.4, CORRECT-01). The rule does
not ask whether anything has used the record. A preparer who enters July's
dispensing figures in August, then registers the source stream in September
and goes back to name it on the record (spec 04.3), is asked to justify a
change to a number no inventory has read.

The standards do not ask for that. Chapter 7 and ISO 14064-1 clause 8
require a documented procedure to prevent, detect and correct errors, at a
granularity the company sets and then applies consistently. ISO 14064-1
9.3.3's obligation to describe a change to data previously used, with its
justification, attaches to data that was reported. Below that line it is our
own data management procedure, and the procedure is what a verifier tests.

The current rule costs us three things, each of which a verifier would meet
in a sample:

- **Reason fatigue.** A 5-character minimum is satisfied by "typo". The log
  fills with noise, the material corrections become hard to find, and a
  low-signal log is answered with a wider sample.
- **Draft parking.** The rational preparer leaves records in DRAFT to stay
  out of the prompt, which degrades the readiness checks and the coverage
  matrix (spec 04.6): stronger controls than the reason field.
- **Batching.** Unrelated edits get bundled into one save to face one
  prompt, so the reason no longer describes the change it is attached to.

Membership of an inventory is the obvious trigger and the wrong one.
`syncAssignments` (spec 05.1) creates an assignment for every fact in the
organization, including the ones it auto-excludes as outside the period or
the boundary, and a current-year draft inventory is typically open from
January. Membership of a draft is computed from facility, period and
boundary. It is not an act of reliance by a person.

## Behavior

### Reliance, and what it requires

A record is **relied upon** once its value has been used by someone other
than the person changing it, or has entered a computed result. Exactly three
things start reliance:

| Cause | Trigger | Stamped |
|---|---|---|
| `RUN_LINE` | The record is calculated into a run line, in any run, including one later voided or superseded | When the run is launched |
| `LOCKED_INVENTORY` | An assignment that includes the record belongs to an inventory that reaches `FROZEN`, `FINAL` or `PUBLISHED` | When the inventory freezes |
| `CLASSIFIED` | An accountant classifies the record in any inventory, a draft one included (`InventoryAssignment.isClassified()`) | When the classification is saved |

Correcting a record that is relied upon needs a reason of at least 5
characters, as today. Correcting one that is not needs no reason: the
correction is accepted, and the revision is still written with who, when,
and every field's value before and after.

**Reliance is monotonic.** It is stamped on the record (`relied_upon_at`
with its cause) at the moment it starts, and never lifted. Voiding a run,
reopening an inventory or clearing a classification does not return a record
to reason-free editing; nothing that was once relied upon becomes
unreliable again.

### The revision says why there is no reason

Every revision records the **reliance evaluation at the moment of the
edit**: either the cause that required the reason, or "not relied upon: no
run lines, no locked inventory membership, unclassified". Without this, a
missing reason asks the verifier to take the relaxation on trust. With it,
the relaxation is a control they can test: sample a reasonless revision,
confirm the record was genuinely unused at that timestamp.

The `ENTERED` revision that captures a draft's first fact value (spec 04.6)
is unchanged, so the trail keeps its anchor.

### Cases

Given a fact with no run lines, unclassified in every inventory, in no
frozen inventory, when the preparer names a source stream and saves with the
reason field empty, then the correction is accepted, and the history reads
"Stream: none to Haul fleet" with who and when, and "Not used by an
inventory when changed".

Given the same record after an accountant classified it in a draft
inventory, when the preparer changes the quantity with no reason, then the
save is refused 422 `errors.reason`: "A correction needs a reason of at
least 5 characters: an accountant has classified this record in an
inventory." Saved with a reason, the correction invalidates that
classification so it is re-performed, as a reviewed number may not move
underneath its review.

Given a record calculated into a run that was later voided, when it is
corrected, then a reason is required: `RUN_LINE` reliance is not lifted by
voiding.

Given a record included in a published inventory, when it is corrected, then
a reason is required, and the correction is a candidate for recalculation
(spec 06.1); past runs and the published figures are unaffected, as they
snapshot.

Given a record relied upon only through an inventory the editor may not see
(spec 01.3), when they correct it without a reason, then the refusal says a
reason is required and names the cause in the abstract ("this record has
been used in an inventory"), never the inventory's name or existence.

### In the register

The drawer asks for the reason only where it is required: "Reason for the
correction *", with the inline refusal of spec 08 when it is missing. Where
it is not required, the field stays visible as "Reason for the change
(optional)", hinting that the record has not been used by an inventory yet,
so a preparer who wants to explain still can, and the field does not appear
and disappear as a surprise.

The register's row payload carries the flag: opened from the register, the
drawer renders the row it was given and does not fetch the record at all.

## API

- `PUT /activities/{id}`: `reason` becomes optional in the schema and is
  required by the service only for a record that is relied upon. 422
  `errors.reason` otherwise, with the cause in words and no inventory named.
- `ActivityResponse` gains `reliedUpon` (boolean) and `reliedUponCause`
  (`RUN_LINE`, `LOCKED_INVENTORY`, `CLASSIFIED`, or null). The register page
  resolves both for a page of records in one query, not per row.
- `ActivityRevisionResponse`: `reason` becomes nullable and the response
  gains `relianceAtChange`, the evaluation stamped on the revision.
- `DELETE /activities/{id}` is unchanged: a removal always needs its reason.

## Data

Migration `V48__reliance_and_optional_correction_reason.sql`:

- `ghg_activity_revisions.reason` drops `NOT NULL`; adds
  `reliance_at_change varchar(200)`.
- `ghg_activities` gains `relied_upon_at timestamptz` and
  `relied_upon_cause varchar(20)`.
- Backfill: stamp `relied_upon_at` on every record that has a run line (the
  earliest run line's creation), that is included in an assignment of a
  `FROZEN`, `FINAL` or `PUBLISHED` inventory, or that is classified in any
  assignment, with the matching cause. Existing revisions get no
  `reliance_at_change`; they were all written under the always-require rule,
  and the null reads as "before this spec".

The backfill rewrites existing rows, so it is replayed against a seeded
database with `psql` before it is deployed, not only against the empty
database CI migrates.

## Events

None. Reliance is stamped inside the `ghg` module by the acts that cause it.

## Verification

Backend, `GhgApiIntegrationTests`: a fact with nothing using it is corrected
with no reason and the revision records the evaluation; a classification
makes the next correction need a reason and the correction invalidates the
classification; a run line makes the reason required and voiding the run
does not lift it; a freeze makes the reason required; the refusal for a
record relied upon through an invisible inventory names no inventory.
`ActivityRecord` unit tests for the stamp's monotonicity.

Migration: replay `V48` on a seeded database and assert the stamped
population matches a query written independently of the migration.

Frontend: `ActivityDrawer.test.tsx` (the field is optional and the save goes
through without it; required and refused inline when the record is relied
upon), `ActivityHistoryModal` (a reasonless revision reads its evaluation).

QA: procedure 3 gains a case for correcting an unused record, and cases C3
and D1 are re-cut against the new rule.

## Non-goals and open questions

- **Reclassifying fields.** The officer review would require a reason, even
  for an unused record, when a change moves it between inventory
  populations: period, facility, entity, unit, activity type. By that logic
  the source stream qualifies too, since it sets the default scope and
  category. The recommendation here is to exempt the stream while the record
  is unused, because naming a stream on a record nothing has read completes
  it rather than restates it. This is the owner's call and the spec is not
  implementable until it is made.
- **Base year.** Correcting a record the base year inventory uses is a
  candidate recalculation trigger (Chapter 5, ISO 14064-1 6.4). Whether this
  spec routes it into the recalculation log of spec 06.1, or only requires
  the reason and leaves the routing to 06.1, is open.
- **Restatement linkage.** Connecting a revision to the inventory correction
  version and the disclosure text it caused (Chapter 9's context for
  significant changes) is deferred to its own spec.
- **Import.** CSV import creates facts only and rejects duplicates (spec
  04.6), so there is no import edit channel to leak through. Should import
  ever update an existing fact, it carries a batch-level reason stamped onto
  every revision it writes.
- **Removal** keeps its always-required reason: removing a record is a
  deliberate act and the dialog puts the field in front of the person doing
  it.
- **The control description.** A verifier tests this rule once as a control
  rather than sampling hundreds of records, which is worth an exportable
  description for the inventory management plan. Deferred.
- Clause numbers here follow ISO 14064-1:2018; confirm them against our copy
  before the text is quoted outward.

# 03.1: Legal entities and Table 1

- **Status**: Draft
- **Protocol**: Chapter 3, Table 1 ("accounting for emissions from various
  types of legal structures") and the definition of equity share as economic
  interest
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-02
- **Modules**: `ghg`, `src/features/ghg`

## Problem

Spec 03 derives the accounting share from three facts and the approach. Table
1 of the Standard is richer, and it consolidates *legal entities*, not sites.
Three consequences:

1. **Table 1 rows the rule cannot express.** A jointly financially controlled
   joint venture is accounted at equity share *under the financial-control
   approach*; an associate over which the company has significant influence
   but not control contributes nothing under either control approach; a
   fixed-asset investment with no significant influence contributes nothing
   even under equity share.
2. **One fact, not three percentages.** A 40% stake in a joint venture company
   that owns three sites is one relationship, held once. Today it is three
   facility percentages to keep in step.
3. **Economic interest versus legal ownership.** Equity share reflects economic
   interest, the extent of rights to the risks and rewards of an operation.
   Usually that equals ownership percentage; where it does not, substance
   overrides form. The model holds only one number.

## Behaviour

- An organization has **legal entities**; each facility belongs to one.
- An entity carries a **relationship type** and an **economic interest**
  percent. Relationship types, following Table 1:
  wholly owned operation or subsidiary (control); joint venture or partnership
  with joint financial control; non-incorporated joint venture operated by the
  company; associate or affiliate (significant influence, no control);
  fixed-asset investment (no significant influence). Legal ownership percent
  may be recorded alongside for disclosure.
- The accounting share derives from **relationship type × approach**, exactly
  as Table 1:

| Relationship | Equity share | Financial control | Operational control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wholly owned / subsidiary | economic interest | 100% | 100% if operated |
| JV, joint financial control | economic interest | economic interest | 100% for the operator, else 0 |
| Non-incorporated JV, company operates | economic interest | economic interest | 100% |
| Associate (significant influence) | economic interest | 0 | 0 |
| Fixed-asset investment | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- The share flows down to the entity's facilities. Per-inventory overrides
  remain possible, at the entity level, and are versioned by the boundary
  freeze (spec 03).
- Facilities without an explicit entity belong to the organization's own
  entity (wholly owned), which is what today's data becomes.

Given the Sankofa group: Obuasi pit and Accra office under the parent (wholly
owned), Tarkwa plant under "Tarkwa Gold JV Ltd" (JV, joint financial control,
40% economic interest, Sankofa operates), Takoradi terminal under "Takoradi
Port Co" (associate, 30%). Under financial control the plant now accounts at
40%, not 0; under equity share the terminal accounts at 30%; under
operational control the plant is 100% and the terminal 0. Today the plant is 0
under financial control, which is wrong.

## API

Sketch: `GET|POST /organizations/{id}/entities`, `PUT|DELETE /entities/{id}`;
`FacilityRequest` gains `entityId`; boundary treatments become per entity with
per-facility inclusion; `BoundaryEntryResponse` names the entity and the Table
1 row applied. The BOUNDARY gate warns when a facility's entity relationship
would give a share the accountant overrode.

## Data

New `ghg_entities` (organization, name, relationship type, economic interest,
legal ownership); `ghg_facilities.entity_id`; treatments and version entries
keyed by entity, entries carrying facility inclusion. The migration creates one
"own" entity per organization and one entity per facility whose facts differ
from wholly owned, so behaviour is preserved.

## Events

None.

## Verification

Integration tests for every cell of the table above; a migration test that
today's Sankofa fixture yields the same shares before and after.

## Non-goals and open questions

Group structures deeper than one entity layer; franchises; whether a boundary
version snapshots entities, facilities or both (recommendation: both, the
entity row and its facilities beneath it).

# 03.1: Legal entities and Table 1

- **Status**: Implemented
- **Protocol**: Chapter 3, Table 1 ("accounting for emissions from various
  types of legal structures") and the definition of equity share as economic
  interest
- **Owner**: Michael Takrama
- **Created**: 2026-09-02; implemented 2026-09-08
- **Modules**: `ghg` (`LegalEntity`, `Table1`, boundary treatments by entity),
  `src/features/ghg` (Legal entities page, facility form, boundary section)

## Problem

Spec 03 derived the accounting share from three facility facts and the
approach. Table 1 of the Standard is richer, and it consolidates *legal
entities*, not sites. Three consequences:

1. **Table 1 rows the rule could not express.** A jointly financially
   controlled joint venture is accounted at equity share *under the
   financial-control approach*; an associate over which the company has
   significant influence but not control contributes nothing under either
   control approach; a fixed-asset investment with no significant influence
   contributes nothing even under equity share.
2. **One fact, not three percentages.** A 40% stake in a joint venture company
   that owns three sites is one relationship, held once.
3. **Economic interest versus legal ownership.** Equity share reflects economic
   interest, the extent of rights to the risks and rewards of an operation.
   Usually that equals ownership percentage; where it does not, substance
   overrides form.

## Behaviour

- An organization has **legal entities**; each facility belongs to exactly
  one. Creating an organization creates its own entity, the **reporting
  company** (wholly owned, 100%, operated), which facilities default to. It
  follows the organization's name, cannot be deleted, and its relationship
  cannot change. An entity with facilities cannot be deleted either.
- An entity carries a **relationship type**, an **economic interest** percent,
  an optional **legal ownership** percent for disclosure, and whether the
  company **operates** it. Relationship types, following Table 1:
  `WHOLLY_OWNED` (operation or subsidiary, control), `JOINT_VENTURE`
  (incorporated JV or partnership under joint financial control),
  `NON_INCORPORATED_JV` (the company operates it), `ASSOCIATE` (significant
  influence, no control), `FIXED_ASSET_INVESTMENT` (no significant influence).
- The accounting share derives from **relationship type × approach**, exactly
  as Table 1 (`Table1.share`):

| Relationship | Equity share | Financial control | Operational control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wholly owned / subsidiary | economic interest | 100% | 100% if operated, else 0 |
| JV, joint financial control | economic interest | economic interest | 100% for the operator, else 0 |
| Non-incorporated JV, company operates | economic interest | economic interest | 100% |
| Associate (significant influence) | economic interest | 0 | 0 |
| Fixed-asset investment | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- A boundary treatment is **per entity**, prefilled from the entity's facts
  (relationship, economic interest, operated) and overridable per inventory,
  with the entity's facilities **included beneath it**. Ticking a facility in
  brings its entity into the boundary; ticking the entity in brings every
  facility of it; removing an entity's last facility removes the entity.
  Overrides apply at the entity level, so every facility of the entity shares
  them. The share flows down to the facilities and is versioned by the freeze
  (spec 03).
- The boundary response names the entity and spells out the **Table 1 row
  applied** (`table1Row`), e.g. "joint venture under joint financial control;
  financial control: 40% economic interest (jointly controlled)".
- The BOUNDARY gate warns when a treatment's relationship, interest or
  operated flag differs from the entity record, naming both sides.

Given the Sankofa group: Obuasi pit and Accra office under the parent (wholly
owned), Tarkwa plant under "Tarkwa Gold JV Ltd" (JV, joint financial control,
40% economic interest, Sankofa operates), Takoradi terminal under "Takoradi
Port Co" (associate, 30%). Under financial control the plant accounts at 40%
and the terminal at 0; under equity share the plant at 40% and the terminal at
30%; under operational control the plant at 100% and the terminal at 0.

## API

All under `/api/ghg`, session-authenticated, tenant-scoped.

- `GET|POST /organizations/{id}/entities`, `PUT|DELETE /entities/{id}`.
  `{name, relationshipType, economicInterestPercent, legalOwnershipPercent?,
  operatedByCompany}`; the response adds `reportingCompany` and the share
  under each approach (`equityShare`, `financialControlShare`,
  `operationalControlShare`). 409 on a duplicate name, on changing the
  reporting company's structure, on deleting it, or on deleting an entity
  with facilities.
- `FacilityRequest` is `{name, location, entityId?}`; `FacilityResponse`
  carries `entityId`, `entityName`, `relationshipType`.
- `GET /inventories/{id}/boundary` → one `BoundaryEntityResponse` per entity
  of the organization: `{entityId, entityName, reportingCompany, inBoundary,
  relationshipType, economicInterestPercent, operatedByCompany,
  accountingShare, table1Row, effectiveFrom, effectiveTo, facilities:
  [{facilityId, facilityName, location, inBoundary}]}`.
- `PUT|DELETE /inventories/{id}/boundary/entities/{entityId}` adds or updates
  the entity's treatment (all its facilities on creation) or removes it;
  `PUT|DELETE /inventories/{id}/boundary/{facilityId}` includes or removes one
  facility. Both `PUT`s take `BoundaryTreatmentRequest` `{relationshipType?,
  economicInterestPercent?, operatedByCompany?, effectiveFrom?, effectiveTo?,
  clearWindow?}` with prefill-or-keep semantics (spec 03).
- Version entries are per entity with `facilities[]` beneath
  (`BoundaryVersionEntryResponse`); summaries carry `entityCount` and
  `facilityCount`.

## Data

`V11__legal_entities.sql`: `ghg_entities` (organization, name unique per
organization, relationship type, economic interest, legal ownership, operated,
`reporting_company` with a partial unique index); `ghg_facilities.entity_id`
replaces `equity_share_percent`, `financial_control`, `operational_control`.
The migration creates one reporting-company entity per organization and one
entity per facility whose facts differed from wholly owned (financial control
maps to wholly owned, operated-but-not-financially-controlled to an operated
joint venture, neither to an associate). `ghg_boundary_treatments` is re-keyed
by entity with `ghg_boundary_facilities` beneath; `ghg_boundary_version_entries`
is re-keyed by entity with `ghg_boundary_version_facilities` beneath. Boundary
treatments and version entries that existed before this migration are not
carried over (as V6 did for pre-1.0 runs): drafts are redrawn, and earlier
versions keep their header row only.

## Non-goals and open questions

Group structures deeper than one entity layer; franchises as a structure;
time-versioned entity facts (an interest that changes on a date is a new
boundary version with a window, spec 03.2, not entity history).
